# Government Health Schemes: National Source Discovery & Package Master Inventory

> **Context:** StewardMD Government Health Schemes Database (Phase 1 Source Discovery)  
> **Reference Baseline:** Andhra Pradesh (`Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva Trust` workbook ingested with 3,713 package rows).  
> **Scope:** Inventory and discovery of official government health-assurance schemes, responsible authorities, official portals, and published package masters / rate lists / procedure code masters across 35 Indian jurisdictions (27 remaining States + 8 Union Territories) and Central Government schemes.

### Key Structural Discoveries:
1. **Convergence Architecture:** Most Indian states (e.g., Gujarat PMJAY-MA, Maharashtra MJPJAY, UP MMJAA, Assam AA-MMJAY, Karnataka AB-ArK, Odisha GJAY) operate in a converged model combining their legacy state schemes with the central Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY framework.
2. **State-Level Standalone Packages vs Central HBP:** 
   - States with extensive custom rate workbooks include **Tamil Nadu** (`cmchistn.com`), **Meghalaya** (`mhis.org.in`), **Himachal Pradesh** (`hpsbys.in`), **Telangana** (`aarogyasri.telangana.gov.in`), **Maharashtra** (`jeevandayee.gov.in`), **Goa** (`dhsgoa.gov.in`), **West Bengal** (`swasthyasathi.gov.in`), and **Uttar Pradesh** (`sachis.in`).
   - UTs and smaller Northeastern states (e.g. Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar, Tripura, Sikkim) rely directly on the central **National Health Authority (NHA) HBP 2022** master data.
3. **Format Standards:** Official rate documents are distributed primarily as **HTML search tables**, **PDF government gazettes/orders**, and downloadable **Excel (XLSX/XLS)** or **CSV** workbooks.
