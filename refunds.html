Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

This policy governs payments made to StewardMD ("we", "us", "our") through the PhonePe Payment Gateway. By making a payment you agree to this policy. It should be read together with our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. StewardMD applies a strict refund policy because it is an instantly-delivered digital clinical decision-support service.

At a glance: Cancellation is allowed only within 30 minutes of a successful payment and only if the paid service has not been used. Eligible refunds are returned to your original payment method and are initiated within 14 days of approval. Everything else is non-refundable.

1. Scope

This policy applies only to payments processed through the PhonePe Payment Gateway on the StewardMD website (stewardmd.in) and the StewardMD Android app. Purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play are processed by, and refunded solely under, those stores' own policies — please contact Apple or Google directly for those. This policy does not apply to any other payment method.

2. Nature of the service

StewardMD is a paid digital subscription/service that is activated and made available immediately (or shortly) after a successful payment. Because access to the digital service begins at once, cancellation and refund rights are strictly limited, as set out below.

3. Cancellation window — 30 minutes

You may request cancellation of a payment within 30 (thirty) minutes of the successful transaction, and only if you have not accessed, used, or consumed any part of the paid service, feature, or content during that period.

  • Requests received after 30 minutes are not eligible for cancellation or refund.
  • If any part of the paid service has been accessed or used within the window, the payment is not eligible for cancellation or refund.
  • The transaction timestamp recorded by PhonePe / our systems is final for calculating the window.

4. When a refund may be considered

A refund will be considered only in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment — you were charged more than once for the same order (the duplicate charge is refunded).
  • Debited but not activated — money was deducted but the subscription/service was not activated, due to a verified technical or payment-gateway failure.
  • Valid cancellation — a cancellation requested within the 30-minute window and meeting all conditions in Section 3.

All refund requests are subject to verification. We reserve the right to decline any request that does not meet these conditions.

5. Non-refundable (no refund will be issued)

Without limitation, the following are not eligible for any refund:

  • Change of mind, or any request made after the 30-minute cancellation window.
  • Any subscription, feature, or content that has been accessed, used, or consumed — even partially.
  • Dissatisfaction with clinical content, recommendations, or outcomes. StewardMD is advisory decision-support only and does not guarantee any clinical or other result.
  • Auto-renewals that were not cancelled before the renewal date. To avoid a future charge, cancel your subscription before it renews; the current paid period remains active until it ends and is not refunded.
  • Non-use of the service, an incompatible device or network, or account suspension/termination for breach of our Terms.
  • Promotional, discounted, complimentary, or bundled purchases, unless a refund is required by applicable law.

6. How to request a refund or cancellation

Email support@stewardmd.in with the subject "Refund request", within the applicable window, including:

  • Your registered email address and/or phone number;
  • The PhonePe transaction / UTR / order ID;
  • The date, time, and amount of the payment;
  • The reason for the request.

Please do not include any patient-identifying information in your email.

7. How and when approved refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are made only to the original payment method/instrument used for the transaction, through PhonePe. We do not refund to any other account, card, or instrument, and we do not refund in cash.
  • Once approved, a refund is initiated within 14 (fourteen) days.
  • The time for the amount to actually reflect in your account depends on your bank, card issuer, or PhonePe and is outside our control; it may take additional working days after initiation.
  • Where only part of an order is eligible, only that part is refunded.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us first so we can resolve any payment issue. Raising a bank/card chargeback for a transaction that is not eligible under this policy may delay resolution and may affect your access to the service.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in effect at the time of your payment applies to that payment. Any changes take effect when posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact & grievances

For any question about this policy, a refund, or a grievance, contact support@stewardmd.in. This policy is governed by the laws of India.
