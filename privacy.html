Privacy Policy

Effective 5 July 2026 · StewardMD is operated by Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana.
StewardMD is a clinical decision-support tool for qualified healthcare professionals. It is not a consumer health app and is not intended for patients or the general public. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Who we are

StewardMD ("we", "us") is operated by Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana. For any privacy question, data-access or deletion request, contact privacy@stewardmd.in (grievance/data-protection contact: Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana).

3. AI features and third-party processing

AI features are optional and off by default. When you use them, the content you submit (clinical findings, your typed question, and/or a captured image) is sent to Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) solely to produce the response and is not stored by StewardMD. Google processes it as a data processor under its Cloud terms and does not use it to train its models.

Images may contain patient identifiers. Before submitting an image, you are shown a notice and asked to remove or cover any patient-identifying details you are not authorised to share. You control whether to submit.

4. Ward Sync (institutional clinical-system access)

Ward Sync is an optional feature for clinicians at partnered institutions (currently GITAM Institute of Medical Sciences, GIMSR). An authorised institution doctor signs in with their own institution (GHIS) credentials to view clinical data — laboratory results, radiology reports, patient lists and medications — from the institution's own system.

  • The institution (GIMSR / GITAM) is the controller of this patient data. StewardMD acts only as a secure conduit that relays it to your device for your review over encrypted connections.
  • StewardMD does not store this patient data — it is not written to disk or cached.
  • StewardMD does not store your institution password. Your login is used only to open a short-lived session; when it expires you simply log in again.
  • Access requires valid institution credentials and is unavailable to anyone without them. Each doctor's own login provides the institution's audit trail.

5. Service providers (sub-processors)

  • Google — Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (your saved cases), Vertex AI (optional AI features).
  • Cloudflare — application hosting and delivery.

6. Where your data is stored

Cloud data (account and saved cases) is stored with Google Cloud in India (asia-south1, Mumbai). On-device data stays on your device. Some processing may occur in other regions where our providers operate; all transfers use encrypted connections and contractual safeguards.

7. Retention

We keep your account and saved cases until you delete them or your account. On-device data persists until you erase it or uninstall the app. AI request content is not retained by us.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete everything, in-app: Account → Delete account & data permanently removes your account and all saved cases from your device and the cloud. Guests can use Erase all data on this device.
  • Access / correction: request a copy or correction of your data at privacy@stewardmd.in.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off AI features or notifications at any time in settings.
  • You may have additional rights under India data-protection law (e.g. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

9. Patient data — your responsibility

StewardMD is a tool you use in your professional capacity. Any patient information you enter or capture, you enter as the treating clinician and data fiduciary. You are responsible for having the authority and any consent required to process it, for minimising patient identifiers, and for complying with your professional and legal obligations.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), authenticated access, and per-user access rules so your saved data is accessible only to you. No system is perfectly secure; use the app on a device you control.

11. Not for children

StewardMD is intended only for qualified healthcare professionals and is not directed to anyone under 18.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date and, where appropriate, in-app notice.
