1. Who we are & scope

StewardMD (the "Service") is operated by Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the StewardMD website, progressive web app, and iOS/Android applications. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact privacy@stewardmd.in.

2. Our roles (data fiduciary / processor)

For account and app data that we determine the purposes of (e.g. your account and the cases you save with us), we act as the data fiduciary / controller. For Patient Data you process using the Service, you or your institution are the data fiduciary/controller and we act as a processor handling only the limited data described below on your behalf and instructions. For Ward Sync, the partnered institution is the controller of the clinical data and we are a conduit (see §8).

3. Definitions

"Personal data" means information about an identifiable individual. "Patient Data" means personal data about a patient that you enter, paste, capture, or import. "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data. Terms such as "Data Principal", "Data Fiduciary", and "consent" have the meanings given in India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

5. Purposes & legal grounds

We process personal data to: (a) create and operate your account and sync your saved work; (b) provide the decision-support features you use; (c) deliver optional AI explanations and notifications you enable; (d) secure, maintain, and improve the Service and prevent misuse; and (e) comply with law. Depending on your location, our legal grounds are your consent (which you give when you sign in, enable a feature, or submit content, and can withdraw), the performance of our agreement with you (Terms of Use), our legitimate/legitimate-use interests in operating and securing the Service, and legal obligation. Under the DPDP Act we rely on your consent and applicable legitimate uses.

6. What we do NOT do

We do not: use advertising or third-party analytics trackers; sell, rent, or trade your data; build advertising profiles; or use your clinical inputs or Patient Data to train our own or any provider's AI models.

7. AI features and third-party processing

AI Features are optional and off by default.

  • Explain: the content you submit (clinical findings and your typed question) is sent to Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) solely to produce the response. It is processed by Google as a processor under its Cloud terms, is not used to train models, and is not stored by StewardMD.
  • AI Vision (app only): the photo is read entirely on your device — the image never leaves your device. The app automatically redacts obvious identifiers (name, MRN/UHID, date of birth, phone, email) from the recognised text before only that text is sent to Vertex AI to structure the values. If you are offline, over your usage limit, or AI is off, values are read fully on-device with no network call. You always review every value before it is applied. AI Vision is unavailable on the web version.

8. Ward Sync (institutional clinical-system access)

Ward Sync is optional and for clinicians at partnered institutions (currently GITAM Institute of Medical Sciences, GIMSR). An authorised doctor signs in with their own institution (GHIS) credentials to view clinical data (labs, radiology, patient lists, medications) from the institution's system.

  • The institution (GIMSR/GITAM) is the controller of this patient data; StewardMD is a secure conduit relaying it to your device over encrypted connections.
  • StewardMD does not store this patient data — not written to disk or cached.
  • StewardMD does not store your institution password; your login opens only a short-lived session.
  • Access requires valid institution credentials; each doctor's own login provides the institution's audit trail.

9. Cookies & local storage

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The Service uses strictly-necessary browser/device storage (e.g. localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, cache, and authentication tokens) to keep you signed in, remember your settings, store guest data locally, and enable offline use. You can clear this at any time via your browser/OS or the in-app erase options.

Some processing (e.g. content delivery/security, and AI processing) may occur in other regions where our providers operate. All transfers use encrypted connections and contractual safeguards, and are made only to jurisdictions/providers consistent with applicable law.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), authenticated access, and per-user access rules so your saved data is accessible only to you, plus data minimisation and provider-level controls. No system is perfectly secure; you should use the Service on a device you control and keep your credentials safe.

14. Personal-data-breach notification

If a personal-data breach affecting your data occurs, we will act to contain and assess it and will notify affected Data Principals and the relevant authority (including, where applicable, the Data Protection Board of India) as and to the extent required by law.

15. Your rights & choices

  • Delete everything, in-app: Account → Delete account & data permanently removes your account and saved cases from your device and the cloud. Guests can use Erase all data on this device.
  • Access & correction: request a copy, a summary, or correction/completion/updating of your personal data at privacy@stewardmd.in.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal-retention exceptions).
  • Withdraw consent: turn off AI features or notifications, or sign out, at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Nomination: under the DPDP Act you may nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Depending on your location you may have further rights (e.g. data portability, objection, restriction) under laws such as the DPDP Act or GDPR. We will respond to verified requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

16. Grievance redressal

If you have a grievance about how your personal data is handled, contact our grievance contact at privacy@stewardmd.in (Grievance contact: Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana). We will acknowledge and address grievances within the period required by applicable law. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate to the relevant data-protection authority (for India, the Data Protection Board of India).

17. Automated decision-making

The Service produces decision-support outputs by rule-based logic, but it does not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about any individual without human involvement. Every clinical output is intended to be reviewed and acted upon by a qualified professional (you).

18. Patient data — your responsibility

You use the Service in your professional capacity. Any Patient Data you enter or capture, you process as the treating clinician and data fiduciary/controller. You are responsible for having the authority and any consent required to process it, for minimising patient identifiers, and for complying with your professional, confidentiality, and data-protection obligations. Please do not enter more identifying patient information than necessary.

19. Not for children

The Service is intended only for qualified healthcare professionals and is not directed to, or intended for use by, anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children as users.
