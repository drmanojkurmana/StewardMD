1. Who we are & scope

StewardMD (the "Service" or the "app") is operated by Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana ("we", "us", "our"). This Policy applies to the StewardMD mobile applications for iOS and Android, distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. StewardMD is a mobile app only; there is no public web app. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, contact privacy@stewardmd.in.

2. Our roles (Data Fiduciary / Data Processor)

For account and app data whose purposes we determine (for example, your account and the cases you choose to save with us), we act as the Data Fiduciary (controller) within the meaning of the DPDP Act. For Patient Data that you process using the app in your professional capacity, you or your institution are the Data Fiduciary and we act only as a Data Processor, handling the limited data described below on your behalf and on your documented instructions. For Ward Sync, the partnered institution is the Data Fiduciary of the clinical data and we act as a secure conduit (see §10).

3. Definitions

"Personal data" means data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data. "Sensitive personal data or information" ("SPDI") has the meaning in the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and includes information relating to physical, physiological, and mental-health condition. "Patient Data" means personal data about a patient that you enter, paste, capture, import, or otherwise process using the app. "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data. "Data Principal", "Data Fiduciary", "Data Processor", and "consent" bear the meanings given in the DPDP Act.

5. Device permissions

With your consent, the app may request access to your device's camera (to photograph a report, monitor, or medication list for on-device text recognition), microphone (for optional voice dictation and clinical scribe), notifications (to deliver alerts you enable), and local storage/files (to save or export documents you create). Permissions are granted through the operating-system prompt and may be changed at any time in your device settings. The app functions without a given permission, with the related feature disabled. Camera images used for ICU AI-Vision are processed on your device and are not uploaded; the experimental image modules (SknX/ThoreX, off by default) may upload the image to our cloud endpoint with your consent (see §9).

6. Purposes & legal basis

We process personal data to: (a) create and operate your account and synchronise your saved work; (b) verify professional eligibility; (c) provide the decision-support features you use; (d) deliver the optional AI explanations and notifications you enable; (e) secure, maintain, and improve the app and prevent misuse; and (f) comply with law and enforce our Terms. Under the DPDP Act, our lawful basis is your consent (given when you sign in, enable a feature, or submit content) and, where applicable, the "legitimate uses" recognised by the Act; we also rely on performance of our agreement with you and, where relevant, legal obligation. Where the GDPR or another regime applies to you, our bases include consent, contract, legitimate interests, and legal obligation, as applicable.

7. Consent & withdrawal

Where we rely on consent, it is sought for the specified purposes above, is capable of being withdrawn, and is as easy to withdraw as to give. You may withdraw consent at any time by turning off the relevant feature (e.g. AI or notifications), signing out, or deleting your account in-app; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and may limit features that depend on it. Consequences of withdrawal are borne by you to the extent the law permits.

8. What we do NOT do

We do not: use advertising, cross-app tracking, or the device advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID); use analytics or crash-reporting trackers or SDKs; sell, rent, or trade your personal data; build advertising or behavioural profiles; or use your clinical inputs or Patient Data to train our own or any provider's AI models.

9. AI features and third-party processing

AI Features are optional and off by default.

  • Explain: the content you submit (clinical findings and your typed question) is transmitted to Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) solely to generate the response. It is processed by Google as our processor under its Cloud Data Processing terms, is not used to train models, and is not stored by StewardMD.
  • AI Vision: the photo is read entirely on your device using the device's on-device text recognition — the image never leaves your device. Before only the recognised text is sent to Vertex AI to structure the values, the app automatically redacts obvious identifiers (name, MRN/UHID, date of birth, phone, email). If you are offline, over your usage limit, or AI is off, values are read fully on-device with no network call. You review every value before it is applied. This on-device claim covers ICU AI-Vision only.
  • Experimental image AI (e.g. SknX skin, ThoreX chest X-ray): these modules are off by default and unlocked with an access code. When you enable one and analyse an image, the captured clinical image may be transmitted to StewardMD's own cloud endpoint (Google Cloud Run, Mumbai) for analysis; it is processed there and not retained. You are shown a specific consent notice before the first upload and can withdraw consent at any time in the module's settings.

10. Ward Sync (institutional clinical-system access)

Ward Sync is optional and for clinicians at partnered institutions (currently GITAM Institute of Medical Sciences, GIMSR). An authorised doctor signs in with their own institution (GHIS) credentials to view clinical data (labs, radiology, patient lists, medications) from the institution's system.

  • The institution (GIMSR/GITAM) is the Data Fiduciary of this patient data; StewardMD is a secure conduit relaying it to your device over encrypted connections.
  • StewardMD does not store this patient data — it is not written to disk or cached.
  • StewardMD does not store your institution password; your login opens only a short-lived session.
  • Access requires valid institution credentials; each doctor's own login provides the institution's audit trail.

11. On-device storage

The app keeps strictly-necessary data on your device (settings, your signed-in session and authentication tokens, guest cases, recent-case history, and cached content) to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and enable offline use. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking. You can clear this data at any time through the in-app erase options (Account → Delete account & data, or Erase all data on this device) or by uninstalling the app.

13. App stores

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google process limited data as independent Data Fiduciaries under their own privacy policies — for example your download/install, app-store account, and any in-app purchase or subscription (we never receive your card or payment details). Your use of the stores is governed by Apple's and Google's respective terms and privacy policies.

14. Where your data is stored & cross-border transfers

Cloud account and saved-case data are stored with Google Cloud in India (asia-south1, Mumbai). On-device data remains on your device. Some processing (content delivery / security, and AI processing) may occur in other regions where our providers operate. Any transfer of personal data outside India is made in accordance with the DPDP Act (and not to any country restricted by notification of the Central Government), on encrypted connections and under contractual safeguards, and — where the GDPR applies — under an approved transfer mechanism.

16. Security safeguards

Consistent with our obligation under the DPDP Act and the "reasonable security practices and procedures" contemplated by Section 43A of the IT Act, 2000 and Rule 8 of the SPDI Rules, 2011, we implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), authenticated access, per-user access rules so your saved data is accessible only to you, data minimisation, and provider-level controls. No system is perfectly secure; you should use the app on a device you control and keep your credentials safe.

17. Personal-data-breach notification

In the event of a personal-data breach affecting your data, we will act to contain and assess it and will notify affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board of India, and (where applicable) the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) under the directions issued under Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000, in the manner and within the timelines required by law.

18. Your rights & choices

  • Delete everything, in-app: Account → Delete account & data permanently removes your account and saved cases from your device and the cloud. Guests can use Erase all data on this device.
  • Access & correction: request a copy or summary of your personal data, and its correction, completion, or updating, at privacy@stewardmd.in.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal-retention exceptions).
  • Withdraw consent: at any time (see §7).
  • Grievance redressal: under §19, and escalation to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nomination: under the DPDP Act you may nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Depending on your location you may have further rights (e.g. portability, objection, restriction) under laws such as the GDPR. We respond to verified requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

19. Grievance redressal

If you have a grievance about the processing of your personal data, contact our Grievance Officer, Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana, at privacy@stewardmd.in. We will acknowledge and address grievances within the period required by the DPDP Act and applicable law. If you remain dissatisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

20. Automated decision-making

The app produces decision-support outputs by rule-based logic and, optionally, generative AI, but it does not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on any individual without human involvement. Every clinical output is intended to be reviewed and acted upon by a qualified professional (you).

21. Patient data — your responsibility

You use the app in your professional capacity. With respect to any Patient Data you enter or capture, you (or your institution) are the Data Fiduciary and we act only as a processor of the limited data described above. You are responsible for having the authority and any consent required to process it, for minimising patient identifiers, and for complying with your professional, confidentiality, and data-protection obligations (including under the DPDP Act, the IT Act and the SPDI Rules, and the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020). Please do not enter more identifying patient information than necessary.

22. Not for children

The app is intended only for qualified healthcare professionals and is not directed to, or intended for use by, anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process the personal data of a child (a person under 18) as a user; if we learn we have, we will erase it.

23. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy; material changes will be reflected by a new version/effective date and, where appropriate, by in-app notice. Please review it periodically. Continued use after a change takes effect indicates your acceptance to the extent permitted by law.
