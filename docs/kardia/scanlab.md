# Kardia Scan Lab

Status: `ai_drafted` until the owner reviews the clinical text. British spelling. No em or en dashes in app text.

## What it is

Scan Lab is the default scanning surface inside the Echo Lab tab. A real 3D probe sits on the skin of a WebGL body. Dragging the grip slides it along the skin, dragging the marker dashes rotates it, and dragging the tail fans and rocks it. A pale ghost probe shows the target pose. The beam fan is drawn into the chest. Off target the monitor shows the KARDIA_US simulated cut at the live probe plane. On target, held for half a second, the real patient clip crossfades into the same sector shape, with its attribution under the monitor.

## Why round 1 never loaded (fixed)

1. Fetch order: the manifest candidates tried `/k2-assets/manifest.json` (404) and the R2 host (404, not uploaded yet) before the working `/work/k2-assets/manifest.json`. Round 2 tries the worktree path first on the web build and the R2 atlas host first on native.
2. SHA-256: round 1 used WebCrypto `crypto.subtle`, which is absent on non-secure contexts, and swallowed the rejection per asset, so no mesh ever registered. Round 2 hashes with a pure JS SHA-256 (`sha256Hex`, unit tested against the known `abc` digest) and reports mismatches by file name.
3. Gzip: the two BodyParts3D `.bin.gz` assets were never decoded at all (only `.glb` files were fetched). Round 2 decodes them with `DecompressionStream` (or an injected decoder in tests) and parses the atlas chunk layout (`ATLAS_CHUNKS`: Float32 positions, Int16 normals, Uint32 indices, registered with `ATLAS_ORG`, the FJ2422 bounds centre from `atlas/3d/manifest.json`).
4. GLB parse: round 2 reads typed accessors with component type and stride handling, keeps registered positions as points for the cut helper, and adds flat arrays for WebGL upload. Every asset must parse with a vertex count above zero or it is named in the error list.
5. Completion was never wired: loading was hidden by an unrelated clips fetch while the asset load was fire and forget. Round 2 has one loading state machine: progress per asset, a 10 second watchdog, success hides the overlay only after the first WebGL frame, and failure shows the specific error with a Retry button (`kslRetryLoad`).
6. Registration and draw: the body is now a three.js WebGL scene (merged, one BufferGeometry per part, pixel ratio capped at 2, no shadows on low power). Skin (translucent), thorax bones filtered from the skeletal chunk, the HuBMAP heart, the arterial chunk near the heart, the liver, plus a red aorta and blue venae cavae, are registered in the patient frame and drawn every frame. A procedural ribs, heart, skin and liver fallback keeps the body readable if one asset fails.

## Probe, monitor and clip

- Probe: a lathe turned body with grip, cable (tube along a curve), marker notch and a rotating ring of marker dashes, seated on the skin surface (`skinY`). The ghost probe uses the same builder in translucent blue at the target pose. The beam is a translucent fan mesh from the footprint along the live beam to the set depth. Drag zones follow the projected probe: grip slides, marker ring rotates, tail fans and rocks; empty skin orbits, pinch zooms, double tap resets. Keyboard accessible handle buttons (`kslGrip`, `kslMarker`, `kslTail`) nudge the same pose.
- Monitor: `KARDIA_US.create` on `kslMonitor`, the same quality path Echo Lab uses (speckle, pericardium, myocardial texture, blood pools, valves, depth gain, sector depth marks, ECG strip). Each frame sets the probe from `planeFromPose`, which returns the target plane exactly at the target pose, and the depth from the pose. The heart rate line (`kslHr`) reads the heart model state. Scan Lab creates its own heart, geometry and probe from `/kardia/views.json` when the host does not pass them.
- Real clip: `KARDIA_CLIPS.preloadFor` (Cache API bytes, object URL, offline capable) chooses the clip for view plus pathology plus severity. Round 1 set the video source but left the element in normal flow below the canvas with no height, so it never appeared in the sector. Round 2 overlays the video absolutely on the monitor, masks it with the Echo Lab `sectorClipPolygon`, crossfades opacity over 300ms (instant with reduced motion), plays it, and shows the attribution under the monitor. If the byte cache path fails, the hosted clip URL streams directly; a video error then reports offline once and keeps the simulation.

## Layout and controls

Practice (`kslPractice`) restarts the attempt timer. Layers (`kslLayersBtn`) switches skin, glass and anatomy opacities. Labels (`kslLabels`) toggles anatomy sprites. Full screen (`kslFullscreen`) expands the stage. The view picker is grouped by window (Parasternal, PSAX, Apical, Subcostal, Suprasternal) with a drawn sector icon per view. Readouts show rotation, fan, rock and depth with target ranges. Scoring, the seven step tutorial, SyncProbe poses and the pathology switch behave as in round 1.

## Browser verification (round 2, headless Chrome 156, SwiftShader WebGL)

Served from the repo root at 1194 by 834 and driven through the app UI (Kardia open, Echo Lab). The sandbox blocked local port binding, so repo files were served to the browser through request interception at a virtual repo-root host; the WebGL engine, three.js build and all app code were the real ones. Findings: loading reached ready with all five meshes registered (skin 12763, bones 113953, heart 8088, liver 1317, vessels 101732 vertices) and 35 draw calls on the first frames; the monitor cut was non-empty (41 to 79 cut structures); orbit drag, pinch zoom and double tap reset were exercised; grip drag slid the contact point, marker drag rotated 40 to 67.6 degrees with the contact fixed, tail drag fanned 0 to 14 and rocked 0 to 9.1 degrees; holding at the PLAX target crossed to the real clip (label, masked sector video, attribution). Two real bugs were found and fixed by this pass: frame timestamps (requestAnimationFrame) were mixed with Date.now, which froze the at-target dwell; and drag maths used clientWidth, which disagrees with the on-screen rect under the app root transform, which mis-zoned the probe handles. Screenshots: work/review/verify-k2-initial-1194.png, verify-k2-plax-1194.png, verify-k2-marker-tail-1194.png. Frame rate measured 6 to 8 fps under SwiftShader software rendering with the whole app running; that number does not transfer to a phone GPU, so 60 fps on an iPhone remains a device check, not a claim.

## Round 3 (better than Probe Lab pass)

- Heart: the HuBMAP heart now renders as a physical material (clearcoat sheen, warm emissive tint) with an epicardial fat shell, curved aorta (ascends, arches posteriorly and left, descends), pulmonary trunk, SVC, IVC, four pulmonary veins and LAD, RCA and circumflex coronary tubes on the surface. The whole heart group scales with `heartBeatScale(cycleTimeMs / cycleMs)` from the same heart model that drives the monitor ECG, so the beat and the trace share one clock.
- Materials and light: hemisphere plus warm key, cool fill and rim lights, ACES tone mapping, dark studio background (`#0b1116`) with fog so the torso fades out. Skin uses a sheen physical material (subsurface tint), bone uses a clearcoat glass physical material.
- Probe placement: every target contact in `kardia-scan-targets.js` is an exact vertex of the decimated HuBMAP skin mesh in the correct landmark region (left parasternal, apex beat, subcostal, jugular notch). At run time the probe contact Y is projected onto the loaded skin vertices, so the probe touches the skin. The test `every target contact lies on the skin mesh within 3 mm and in its landmark region` checks point to triangle distance and the landmark box for all 15 views.
- Layout: iPad landscape is a 55/45 grid at full viewport height. Phone portrait keeps the scene at 45dvh, the monitor under it and the picker sticky at the bottom. Readouts are one compact row. Layers, labels, full screen, practice and tutorial are 44 px round icon buttons over the scene corner with accessible names. The old handle explainer row and the old Echo Lab chest map instruction are removed; Grip, Marker and Tail are tutorial steps 1 to 3.
- Monitor: off target it is the KARDIA_US sector (depth marks, focus, ECG, heart rate, probe marker). On target it crossfades over 600 ms to the real clip, shows a `Real patient` badge, and puts attribution in a `Clip details and credits` details row.
- Picker windows are Parasternal, Short axis, Apical, Subcostal and Suprasternal, one drawn icon per view.
- Performance: one merged BufferGeometry per body part, pixel ratio capped at 2 (1.5 on low power), no shadows on low power, rendering pauses on `visibilitychange`, and the live frame rate and draw calls show under the monitor (`getDebug().fps`). Capture and FPS runner: `work/review/r3-capture.py` with `work/review/r3-harness.html`; it writes `work/review/r3-<state>-<width>.png` for initial, plax, a4c, sc4c, suprasternal, layers and tutorial at 1194x834 and 390x844. In the round 3 agent sandbox every Chromium build aborted on launch (SIGABRT/SIGSEGV) and local port binding was blocked, so those captures and the FPS numbers must be taken by that runner outside the sandbox; no FPS number is claimed here.
