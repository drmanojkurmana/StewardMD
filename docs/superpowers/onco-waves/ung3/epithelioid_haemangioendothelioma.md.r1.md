# R1 Clinical-Safety Review - Epithelioid Haemangioendothelioma (EHE)

VERDICT: APPROVE

Disease id: epithelioid_haemangioendothelioma
Adversarial verdict file (.verdict.md): NOT PRESENT (no prior flags to reconcile).

## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful directive statements. Every clinical claim is
hedged as decision-support ("reasonable first step", "valid option", "does not
appear to", "supports a measured approach"). No absolute cure/never claims.
- The strongest statement (metastatic disease should not by itself withhold
  resection/transplantation) is a genuine, well-documented feature of EHE
  biology and is correctly softened with "does not appear to" - safe.
- Observation-first for multifocal hepatic disease is paired with an explicit
  serial-monitoring requirement (Monitoring section), so it does not read as
  "do nothing." Safe.

## 2. GROUNDING
Treatment claims are consistent with DeVita/NCCN standard of care for this rare
vascular sarcoma:
- Surgery as treatment of choice for localised disease - correct.
- Multifocal recurrence pattern, indolent/variable natural history - correct.
- Liver transplantation for liver-confined unresectable disease, metastases not
  an absolute contraindication - matches established hepatic-EHE transplant data.
- Systemic therapy reserved for progressive/symptomatic disease; chemo,
  VEGF-pathway inhibitors, checkpoint inhibitors all reported - correct and
  appropriately caveated for weak evidence base.
- Non-DeVita claims (mTOR/interferon, palliative RT for painful bone lesions,
  cross-sectional imaging cadence) are EXPLICITLY relabelled "General oncology
  standard, not from DeVita's section on this disease." No claim is
  mis-attributed to DeVita. The radiotherapy section correctly states DeVita
  does not define an RT role before adding the general-standard caveat.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, or numbered schedules. The only numerics are a
prognostic figure ("fewer than 20 percent die of disease") and "phase II" trial
phase - neither is a dose.

## 4. SCOPE
Appropriately scoped as decision-support, not a directive. Consistent framing
toward multidisciplinary/expert-centre referral and individualised decisions.

## 5. ADVERSARIAL FLAGS
No .verdict.md exists; no outstanding flags. Attribution discipline is already
strong (general-standard claims are separated from DeVita-sourced ones).

## Advisory (non-blocking)
- Line 20: "slow disease progression or produce a partial response in the
  majority of treated patients." The phase II antiangiogenic (bevacizumab)
  data show partial response in a MINORITY with disease control (PR+stable) in
  the majority. As written ("slow progression OR partial response ... in the
  majority") it reads as combined disease control and is defensible, but
  consider tightening to "disease control (stability or partial response)" to
  avoid implying PR in most patients. Not blocking.
