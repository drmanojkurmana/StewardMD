# DICER1 syndrome — management

Note: DeVita's text contains only an incidental mention of a somatic DICER1 mutation in a
pulmonary blastoma molecular-pathology list, with no dedicated section on DICER1 syndrome
management. The guidance below reflects NCCN and DICER1/PPB Registry international
surveillance-consensus practice rather than DeVita-specific content.

## Overview of management approach

DICER1 syndrome is not itself treated; management is organism-wide surveillance for the component
tumours plus tumour-specific treatment when a lesion is found. Because penetrance is incomplete
and manifestations cluster by age, the approach is longitudinal and multidisciplinary rather than
a single line of therapy.

## Surveillance (the core of management)

- Chest imaging in early childhood aimed at detecting lung cysts and pleuropulmonary blastoma (PPB)
  before progression to more aggressive solid or sarcomatous forms.
- Abdominal and pelvic ultrasound surveillance for cystic nephroma and ovarian Sertoli-Leydig cell
  tumour.
- Thyroid ultrasound and clinical examination for multinodular goitre and differentiated thyroid
  cancer, continued into adulthood and intensified after any chemotherapy or radiotherapy given for
  another DICER1-associated tumour.
- Neuro-imaging when pineoblastoma or other intracranial disease is suspected clinically.
- Cascade genetic testing of at-risk relatives once a germline pathogenic variant is identified in
  an index case, given autosomal dominant inheritance with variable penetrance.

## Treatment by tumour type and intent

- Pleuropulmonary blastoma: managed with surgical resection, combined with systemic chemotherapy
  for higher-risk (solid or mixed solid-cystic) histologic subtypes; purely cystic (type I) lesions
  may be managed with resection and close surveillance alone. Radiotherapy is used selectively,
  with awareness that it raises subsequent malignancy risk in a syndrome already prone to second
  tumours.
- Ovarian Sertoli-Leydig cell tumour and cystic nephroma: primary surgical management (fertility-
  or organ-sparing where feasible), with systemic therapy reserved for higher-grade or advanced
  disease.
- Multinodular goitre and differentiated thyroid cancer: surgery (lobectomy or thyroidectomy)
  according to standard thyroid cancer management principles when malignancy is confirmed or
  strongly suspected; benign goitre may be followed clinically.
- Embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma and pineoblastoma arising in this syndrome are managed with the same
  multimodal principles (surgery, systemic chemotherapy, and radiotherapy where indicated) used for
  their sporadic counterparts, adapted for the individual patient's age and prior treatment
  exposure.
- Across tumour types, radiation exposure should be minimised where alternatives exist, given the
  syndrome's inherent susceptibility to treatment-related malignancy, particularly of the thyroid.

## Monitoring after treatment

- Continued age-appropriate surveillance imaging (chest, abdomen/pelvis, thyroid) persists after
  treatment of an index tumour, since carriers remain at risk for additional, unrelated
  DICER1-associated tumours over time.
- Long-term thyroid monitoring is particularly important in anyone who has received chemotherapy or
  radiotherapy for a DICER1-associated tumour.

## When to refer

- Refer to medical genetics for germline DICER1 testing, interpretation, and cascade screening of
  at-risk relatives once the syndrome is suspected or confirmed in an index patient.
- Refer to a multidisciplinary team (paediatric oncology, gynaecologic oncology, urology,
  endocrinology, and genetics as relevant) to coordinate the surveillance schedule and treat each
  component tumour as it arises, since no single specialty covers the full phenotypic spectrum.
- Any child with a lung cyst, a pelvic or abdominal mass, a rapidly enlarging thyroid nodule, or
  neurological signs in the context of a known or suspected DICER1 pathogenic variant warrants
  prompt specialist referral rather than routine follow-up.

Sources: NCCN Guidelines. DICER1/PPB Registry international surveillance consensus (Schultz et al.).
