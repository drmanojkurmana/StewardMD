# R1 Clinical-Safety Review: oral_cancer.md (Wave 7)

VERDICT: APPROVE (with one relabelling note carried from adversarial verdict)

## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful statement. Every therapeutic claim is hedged as
decision-support ("generally preferred", "should be considered", "guided by pathologic
findings", "at tumour board"). No absolute directive that could harm if followed:
- Surgery-first default for resectable oral cavity disease is correctly stated as a default,
  not an unconditional rule, with definitive CRT/RT offered as the alternative for patients who
  cannot tolerate an extensive operation.
- Neck management thresholds (DOI, elective levels, bilateral triggers, >=18 node yield) are
  framed as risk-based considerations, not hard mandates.
- Recurrence salvage statements ("neck soft-tissue recurrence rarely curable") are prognostic,
  not instructions to withhold care.
No false-reassurance risk: "single negative biopsy does not rule out recurrence" and
"non-healing lesion needs biopsy rather than repeated empirical treatment" both push toward
safe escalation, not away from it.

## 2. GROUNDING
Consistent with DeVita 12th ed. Ch. 27 / NCCN standard of care. The adversarial verdict
spot-checked each numeric/clinical claim (histology %, imaging workup, surgery-first rationale,
lip-cancer equipoise, single-node upstaging + ~50% survival drop, DOI thresholds, cN0/cN+
elective levels, bilateral-neck triggers, node yield, follow-up/recurrence management) against
the source and found them verbatim or near-verbatim. No fabricated or outdated regimen. The
sidecar correctly self-discloses that recurrent/metastatic systemic therapy (chemo,
EGFR-targeted, immunotherapy) is general HNSCC standard, NOT from DeVita's oral-cavity section
(lines 61-65) — this is honest attribution, not scope-creep.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. All numerics are percentages, DOI mm thresholds, T-stage labels, nodal levels I-V,
node-yield count (18), and edition/chapter numbers. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, Gy, or numbered drug
schedule anywhere. Systemic therapy is named by class only.

## 4. SCOPE
Appropriately scoped as decision-support. Consistently defers definitive treatment selection to
the multidisciplinary tumour board and to pathologic findings; does not diagnose definitively or
prescribe. "When to refer" section is a clean, safe hand-off.

## 5. ADVERSARIAL FLAGS (from oral_cancer.verdict.md)
The verdict returned CLEAN with two minor non-blocking items, both still present in the sidecar:
  (a) "oral submucous fibrosis" listed among premalignant lesions (lines 18-19).
  (b) "fails to heal within a few weeks -> biopsy" time threshold (lines 17-18, 119).
Neither is in DeVita's Ch. 27 text; both are the author's clinical generalizations sitting under
a DeVita-only citation. Per R1 policy I would REVISE if these were controversial or
dose/regimen-bearing — they are neither.
  - (a) is a well-established premalignant condition, especially high-yield for this app's India
    context (betel/areca-nut exposure); clinically uncontroversial.
  - (b) is standard head-and-neck / dental teaching; clinically uncontroversial and safety-positive
    (biases toward biopsy).
APPROVE is therefore permitted, on the condition below.
