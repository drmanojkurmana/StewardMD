## 1. Safety
No unsafe, misleading, or absolute directive found. The one clinically sensitive
claim — that CBL-mutated / Noonan-associated forms can be indolent or self-resolve
and may be observed rather than transplanted immediately — is correctly guarded:
it is conditioned on genotype confirmation AND paired with explicit monitoring for
transition to a progressive course (lines 12, 34). This matches the recognised
biology and does not license withholding transplant from progressive disease.
"Curative intent for the majority via transplant" is appropriately hedged, not
stated as a guarantee.

## 2. Grounding
Claims are consistent with current JMML standard of care:
- Allo-HSCT as principal curative modality for typical/progressive JMML — correct.
- RAS-pathway drivers (PTPN11, NRAS, KRAS, NF1, CBL) — correct.
- CBL / Noonan-associated indolent subset amenable to watchful waiting — correct.
- Pre-transplant cytoreduction with a hypomethylating agent or low-intensity
  chemotherapy as a bridge, not definitive therapy — correct.
- Relapse managed by donor lymphocyte infusion or second transplant — correct.
- Splenectomy adjunctive/selected, not curative; no primary radiotherapy role — correct.
No fabricated or outdated regimen. No mis-attribution: the draft explicitly states
no DeVita JMML section was located (JMML is a paediatric MDS/MPN, outside DeVita's
adult focus) and labels every claim "general oncology / paediatric haematology
standard, not from DeVita." This is the correct handling — it does NOT cite DeVita
for any claim. NCCN correctly noted as not applicable to this paediatric entity.
The standing FLAG for manual sourcing against a paediatric haematology reference
(WHO haematolymphoid classification / paediatric haem-onc text) is retained and
should stay until sourced.
