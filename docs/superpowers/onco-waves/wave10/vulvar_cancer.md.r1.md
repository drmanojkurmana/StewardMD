# R1 Clinical-Safety Review: vulvar_cancer

VERDICT: APPROVE

Clinically safe, grounded in DeVita 12th ed. (per adversarial verdict: line-by-line match to
Ch. 49 "Carcinoma of the Vulva"), dose-free, and appropriately scoped as decision-support.

## 1. Safety
No unsafe, misleading, or absolute statement. Every treatment claim is hedged
("generally," "often preferred," "individualized," "discussed with the patient"). Conservative
nodal-management thresholds (SLNB selection criteria, ITC/micromet vs. macromet stratification,
adjuvant nodal RT indications) match standard of care. Referral triggers (any invasive lesion or
HSIL with invasion features -> gyn-onc; locally advanced -> multidisciplinary center; suspected
vulvovaginal melanoma -> prompt referral with KIT testing) are correct and appropriately cautious.
No directive that could cause harm if followed.

## 2. Grounding
No claim mis-attributed to DeVita. Adversarial verdict cross-checked epidemiology/pathology, VIN/HSIL
management, invasive local treatment, nodal management (GROINSS-V I/II, AGO-CaRE-1, NCDB framing),
chemoradiation, recurrent/metastatic systemic therapy, and vulvovaginal melanoma — all traceable to
the cited source. The two general-oncology items (bevacizumab anti-VEGF side-effect profile; the
surveillance schedule) are explicitly self-flagged inline as NOT DeVita-sourced. That is the correct,
safe direction — the draft under-claims rather than over-claims grounding. No fabricated or outdated
regimen.

## 3. Dose-free
Confirmed. Zero drug doses. All numerics are survival/recurrence percentages, tumor-size cutoffs (cm),
depth-of-invasion (mm), treatment-time interval (weeks), and staging labels (T2/T3/T4, PD-1). DeVita's
specific RT Gy figures and chemo schedules are correctly stripped.

## 4. Scope
Appropriately hedged throughout as decision-support, not a directive.

## 5. Adversarial flags
Verdict returned CLEAN. Only non-blocking note: the bevacizumab disclaimer is over-conservative (the
core extrapolation-from-cervical-cancer claim is in fact in DeVita's chapter; only the generic
side-effect list is a general add-on). This under-claims grounding — safe, not a mis-source. No
DeVita-cited claim was found absent from DeVita. No blocking issue remains.

Advisory (non-blocking, optional): line 22 "close margins without other risk factors can be managed
with close observation plus postoperative radiotherapy" reads as two options fused; reword to
"...close observation OR postoperative radiotherapy" for clarity. Does not affect approval.
