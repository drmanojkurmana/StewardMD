## 1. Safety
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful directive. Core claims are correct standard of care:
surgery is the mainstay across grades; conventional chondrosarcoma is chemo- and
radio-resistant; grade-1 central lesions may be managed by intralesional curettage +
adjuvant local measures; grade 2-3 need wide resection; dedifferentiated and mesenchymal
subtypes behave aggressively. No absolute/guarantee language; recurrence and late-metastasis
risk (incl. clear cell) are acknowledged. No harm if followed.
