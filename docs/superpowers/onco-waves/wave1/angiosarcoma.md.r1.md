## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or absolute directive found. Every treatment claim is
hedged as decision-support, not a command:
- "resection alone is rarely curative", "responds, at least initially",
  "individualised based on prior treatment", "emerging option",
  "area of active investigation rather than an established standard".
- Molecular markers explicitly framed as prognostic / trial-referral aids,
  with "a change in standard systemic therapy based on them is not established".
- Pulmonary metastasectomy is qualified ("isolated, fully resectable",
  "selected sarcoma patients") — no over-broad surgical directive.
No false-negative risk: it does not tell a clinician to withhold or stop any
therapy, and correctly routes suspected cases to a sarcoma MDT and early
palliative care. No PHI.

## 2. GROUNDING (DeVita/NCCN standard of care)
Consistent with cited DeVita 12th ed and NCCN soft-tissue-sarcoma framing:
- Vascular endothelial origin; lymphangiosarcoma folded in; Stewart-Treves
  (chronic lymphoedema) and post-radiation (classically post-breast-RT)
  secondary settings; scalp/face in elderly — all correct.
- Surgery mainstay with difficult margins, RT for local control, lung as
  dominant haematogenous site — correct.
- Relative chemosensitivity vs other STS, activity of anthracyclines and
  taxanes (taxane activity notably angiosarcoma-selective) — correct.
- Second-line options (taxanes, gemcitabine, vinorelbine, pegylated liposomal
  doxorubicin, multitargeted TKIs, ifosfamide) — correct and appropriately
  non-prescriptive on sequencing.
- Antiangiogenic rationale: anti-VEGF antibody phase II in angiosarcoma +
  epithelioid haemangioendothelioma (bevacizumab), and an approved
  antiangiogenic multitargeted TKI for advanced STS post-chemo (pazopanib) —
  accurately described without naming the drug or dose.
- Checkpoint blockade combination signal + higher TMB in head/neck cutaneous
  disease — correct and correctly labelled investigational.
- Molecular: MYC/FLT4 amplification enriched in radiation/lymphoedema-associated
  disease, FLT4 co-amp worse outcome, CIC rearrangement in younger primary
  disease with inferior DFS — all grounded, no fabrication.
No outdated or invented regimen detected. The "Not addressed in the grounding
source" section honestly flags the absence of a validated
angiosarcoma-specific staging/sequencing scheme.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, numbered cycle schedules, or dosing intervals.
