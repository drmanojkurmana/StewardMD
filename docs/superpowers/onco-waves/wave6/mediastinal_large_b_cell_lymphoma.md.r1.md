## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful statement. Definitive phrasings ("first-line
therapy consists of anthracycline-based chemoimmunotherapy plus an anti-CD20
antibody"; "no surgical role in definitive treatment") are clinically
uncontroversial standard of care and are appropriately framed. RT-omission is
correctly gated on a PET-based complete metabolic response rather than presented
as automatic. SVCS / airway / haemodynamic compromise at presentation is
flagged for assessment. No harm risk if a clinician follows the text.

## 2. GROUNDING
Treatment claims are consistent with DeVita 12th ed / NCCN standard of care:
- DA-EPOCH-R without RT producing excellent PFS/OS (Dunleavy et al. lineage) — grounded.
- CHOP+RT vs DA-EPOCH-R broadly similar, no prospective RCT — accurately hedged.
- CD19 CAR-T (axi-cel, liso-cel) approved in R/R PMBCL — correct.
- Pembrolizumab: meaningful ORR, few CRs, durable responses (KEYNOTE-13/170) — correct.
- Brentuximab monotherapy low single-agent response — correct.
- Nivolumab + brentuximab higher response in single-arm data (CheckMate-436),
  labelled as cross-study, not head-to-head — correctly qualified.
- 9p24.1 amplification / JAK-STAT / PD-L1-PD-L2 biology and cHL overlap — correct.
No fabricated or outdated regimen. No claim is mis-attributed to DeVita: general
-standard and inferential claims (no surgical role, PET-adapted RT omission,
PET-CT response assessment, late-effects surveillance, referral triggers) are
each explicitly relabelled as "general oncology standard, not from DeVita's
section on this disease." This is exactly the discipline required.
