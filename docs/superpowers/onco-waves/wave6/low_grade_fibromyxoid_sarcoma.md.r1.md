Goldens changed: no (intended: n/a — narrative content only, no engine/rule change).

## 1. Safety
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful statement. No absolute/directive language that
oversteps decision-support. Core clinical actions (negative-margin resection, RT for
margin concerns, MDT referral, long-term chest surveillance, metastasectomy for
isolated resectable pulmonary mets, escalation to sarcoma oncology for unresectable/
metastatic disease) are all safe standard-of-care and correctly conditional, not
mandatory. The draft explicitly avoids inventing a systemic regimen where none is
grounded, which is the safe posture for a rare translocation-driven sarcoma.

## 2. Grounding
Treatment claims are consistent with DeVita/NCCN sarcoma standard of care. Disease
facts are accurate: LGFMS/Evans tumor, MUC4 IHC, FUS-CREB3L2 (>90%) with FUS-CREB3L1/
EWSR1-CREB3L1 variants, late (decades-delayed) pulmonary metastasis, and the
LGFMS–SEF spectrum with hybrid histology. No fabricated or outdated regimen. Crucially,
every claim that is NOT specifically in DeVita's LGFMS section is transparently labelled
"general oncology standard, not from DeVita's section on this disease," and only the
late-pulmonary-metastasis behaviour and the LGFMS/SEF spectrum are attributed to DeVita
— which is correct. No claim is mis-attributed to DeVita. This satisfies the R1 rule on
source attribution.

## 3. Dose-free
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, or numbered schedules. The only numerics are fusion
prevalence (>90%) and epidemiology (third/fourth decade) — not doses. Compliant.

## 4. Scope

reasonable candidates, should be escalated to, warrants evaluation). Defers agent
selection to the treating sarcoma oncologist. No definitive diagnostic or prescriptive
