# R1 Clinical-Safety Review: li_fraumeni_syndrome

APPROVE — clinically safe, grounded, dose-free, and appropriately scoped as decision-support.

## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful directive. The load-bearing clinical claim (avoid radiotherapy
in TP53 carriers) is consistently hedged: "where possible", "when a reasonable alternative exists",
"individualized and clearly discussed with the patient", and "multidisciplinary discussion when
radiotherapy appears to be the only feasible local control option". Surgery-preferred-for-local-
control is stated as a trade-off to discuss, not an absolute. No definitive diagnostic or
treatment-stopping instruction. No absolute survival/outcome promise.

## 2. GROUNDING
Consistent with DeVita 12th ed standard of care. Adversarial pass confirmed the specific claims
are verbatim/supported: 80% cancer by age 45, 36% index sarcoma, RT second-malignancy risk >20%
"albeit based on very limited data", whole-body MRI sarcoma screening, annual breast MRI for LFS +
first-degree relatives (expert opinion), HER2-positive propensity, osteosarcoma/glioma/choroid
plexus carcinoma associations. Non-DeVita items (ACC, MRI-over-CT surveillance, mastectomy trade-
off) are explicitly labeled "general oncology standard, not from DeVita's section on this disease".
No fabricated regimen, drug, trial, or staging system — correctly, since LFS has no syndrome-level
systemic therapy, which the draft states plainly rather than inventing content.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, Gy, or numbered schedule. The percentages present are epidemiologic/
risk statistics, not doses.

## 4. SCOPE
Appropriately scoped. Frames management as site/histology-driven, defers systemic-therapy detail to
each cancer's own chapter and structured protocol templates, and repeatedly routes to genetics,
hereditary-surveillance programs, disease-site specialists, and MDT. Reads as decision-support, not
directive.

## 5. ADVERSARIAL FLAGS
Adversarial verdict = CLEAN. No ungrounded, mis-sourced, or scope-creep claim requiring REVISE. The
draft does NOT cite DeVita for anything not in DeVita.

## Advisory (non-blocking)
The adrenocortical carcinoma bullet is labeled "general oncology standard, not from DeVita's section
on this disease." This under-claims: DeVita's Adrenal Tumors chapter does tie TP53/Li-Fraumeni to ACC
(up to ~15% adult, up to ~80% pediatric germline TP53). This is the conservative/safe failure mode
(no mis-attribution to DeVita, no fabrication), so it does not block. Optional tightening: cite
DeVita's Adrenal Tumors chapter for the ACC association instead of disclaiming it.
