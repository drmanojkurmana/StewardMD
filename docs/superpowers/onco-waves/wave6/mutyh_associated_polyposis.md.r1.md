## VERDICT: APPROVE

## 1. SAFETY - PASS
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful absolute statement. Management is framed correctly as lifelong
risk reduction (surveillance, endoscopic clearance, escalation to surgery), not cytotoxic/targeted
therapy for the syndrome. All surgical and systemic statements are conditional ("considered",
"individualised", "standard-of-stage"), so nothing directs a clinician toward harm. The high
lifetime-CRC-risk figure is correctly conditioned on absence of timely surveillance, which supports
(rather than overstates) the case for screening. The one potentially dangerous extrapolation trap -
the SCLC MUTYH/platinum-sensitivity association - is explicitly quarantined as an incidental
lung-cancer biomarker finding that must NOT be applied to MAP-associated CRC/duodenal cancer. Good.

## 2. GROUNDING - PASS
Consistent with DeVita/NCCN standard of care. Every DeVita-attributed claim (autosomal recessive
inheritance; 43-100% lifetime CRC risk; ~4% duodenal cancer risk; MAP among familial gastric-risk
syndromes; SCLC platinum note) is verified in the adversarial pass against the 12th-ed text and I
find no over-reach beyond those passages. No fabricated regimen, trial, or drug name. General
hereditary-polyposis standards (endoscopic surveillance, colectomy for unmanageable burden,
cascade/genetic testing) are uncontroversial standard of care and are labeled as general standard,
NOT mis-attributed to DeVita. No DeVita-for-a-non-DeVita-claim violation.

## 3. DOSE-FREE - PASS
No numeric dose. Only numbers are epidemiologic risk percentages (43-100%, ~4%), which are risk
figures, not dosing. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, cycle/day schedule, or interval numbers.

## 4. SCOPE - PASS
Appropriately hedged as decision-support. "Lines of therapy" reframed honestly as a surveillance ->
surgery escalation ladder. Surveillance intervals and surgical thresholds are deliberately NOT
invented; the file self-flags for manual sourcing against NCCN Genetic/Familial High-Risk: Colorectal
or GeneReviews before sign-off. Consistent referral to hereditary-CRC/clinical-genetics MDT. No
directive tone.
