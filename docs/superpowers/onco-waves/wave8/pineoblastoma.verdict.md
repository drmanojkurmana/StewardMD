The sidecar's Radiotherapy section instead says these are "related to the tumour itself **as well as** to surgery and radiation near the hypothalamic-pituitary axis" — this reverses/blends the source's "rather than" framing into a both-causes claim. Minor but a real inversion of what DeVita states, not just an omission.
   - Everything else checked (PNET/pineoblastoma classification, markers AFP/beta-hCG, neuraxis imaging before surgery, surgery-difficulty/biopsy-vs-resection framing incl. germinoma contrast, endoscopic biopsy + third ventriculostomy, VMAT/IMRT/proton to reduce late effects, the 12-patient induction-chemo → CSI-with-boost → high-dose-chemo-with-stem-cell-rescue series incl. the two radiation-free infants and "still investigational" framing, recurrent-disease high-dose-chemo underperformance, and the platinum/alkylator chemo being germ-cell- not pineoblastoma-specific) is traceable to the "Primitive Neuroectodermal or Embryonal CNS Neoplasms" intro and "Pineal Region Tumors and Germ Cell Tumors" section (Surgery/Radiation Therapy/Chemotherapy subsections) of DeVita.
   - Claims explicitly self-flagged in the sidecar as "general oncology standard, not from DeVita's section on this disease" (germline RB1/DICER1 screening, metastatic-disease craniospinal-field principle, follow-up/monitoring recommendations) are correctly labeled — I confirmed RB1/DICER1/trilateral retinoblastoma do not appear anywhere near the pineal/PNET section of devita.txt (only unrelated RB1-pathway oncology-signaling hits elsewhere in the text).
