# R1 CLINICAL-SAFETY REVIEW: papillary_tumor_pineal_region (PTPR)

VERDICT: APPROVE

## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or absolute statement. Every clinical claim is standard,
conservative neuro-oncology practice and is hedged. No harm pathway if followed:
- Surgery-first / maximal safe resection, RT for residual/unresectable/recurrent,
  chemo role explicitly "uncertain/unestablished" - all correct and non-directive.
- Hydrocephalus + CSF diversion (ETV/shunt) and Parinaud/urgent neurosurgical
  referral are clinically appropriate and safety-positive.
- No false-negative risk (no missed red flag, no wrong sequencing presented as fact).

## 2. GROUNDING
Consistent with DeVita/NCCN standard of care for CNS tumours. Critically, the draft
does NOT misattribute anything to DeVita: it discloses that DeVita ch. 64 covers
germinomas/NGGCT/pineal parenchymal tumours and never names PTPR (an ependymal-derived
entity), and correctly declines to transfer germinoma-specific detail (radiosensitivity,
CSI framework, survival figures). All claims are labelled "general oncology standard,
not from DeVita's section on this disease." No fabricated or outdated regimen. Matches
the adversarial verdict, which confirmed by direct grep that PTPR is absent from the
source and rated the sidecar CLEAN.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, Gy, %, or numbered schedule in the body. Only digits are
the chapter number "64" and edition "12th" in the sourcing/citation lines.

## 4. SCOPE
Appropriately hedged as decision-support: "no established regimen," "no randomized data,"
"no validated first-/second-line sequence," "benefit not established," MDT/clinical-trial
framing throughout. Reads as guidance, not directive.
