# R1 Clinical-Safety Review: anti_yo_cerebellar_degeneration

VERDICT: APPROVE

Reviewed against DeVita/NCCN standard of care (12th ed. cited). Goldens changed: no.

## 1. SAFETY - PASS
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful directive. Core clinical message is correct and
non-dangerous: tumor control is the primary lever and immunotherapy is adjunctive with
limited/uncertain benefit for an intracellular-antigen antibody. The empiric-treatment
statement ("do not wait for autoantibody results... once viral/bacterial infection
reasonably excluded") is appropriately conditioned, not absolute. The "poor response to
all immunotherapy for Yo/PCA-1" statement reflects real prognosis and is not defeatist to
the point of discouraging tumor treatment - it explicitly keeps cancer control as the main
modifiable factor. Aspiration/fall-risk supportive-care flags are correct and safe.

## 2. GROUNDING - PASS
Treatment claims are consistent with the cited source and standard of care:
- Yo (PCA-1) -> rapidly progressive cerebellar syndrome, high-risk antibody (>70% cancer
  probability), ovarian/breast association - matches DeVita Table 89.1.
- First-line steroids then IVIG/plasma exchange; second-line rituximab / azathioprine /
  mycophenolate / cyclophosphamide - matches the PNS chapter.
- Tocilizumab (rituximab-refractory autoimmune encephalitis) and bortezomib (NMDAR
  encephalitis) correctly flagged as NOT established for Yo - no fabricated indication.
No fabricated or outdated regimen. Every non-DeVita claim (Purkinje-loss plateau,
platinum/anthracycline-class systemic therapy, risk-reducing surgery, monitoring cadence,
rehab referral) carries an explicit "general oncology standard, not from DeVita" disclaimer.
No claim mis-attributed to DeVita.

## 3. DOSE-FREE - PASS
No mg / mg/m2 / AUC / numbered schedule anywhere. Only numerics are "70 percent" (a cancer-
association probability category, not a dose) and "12th ed." (edition). Confirmed the
extensive dosing present in DeVita's source paragraph was correctly stripped to drug names.

## 4. SCOPE - PASS

No absolute directive. A dedicated "What DeVita does not specify" section transparently
bounds the source and refuses extrapolation (Yo-specific protocol, response rates, genetic
