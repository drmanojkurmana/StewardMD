# R1 Clinical-Safety Review: anti_ma2_encephalitis (management narrative)

VERDICT: APPROVE

## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful directive found. Language is consistently hedged
("standard", "can be", "may", "generally guided by severity and local practice"). No
absolute claim that oversteps decision-support. Two genuinely safety-positive inclusions:
- Bulbar/brainstem involvement flagged for airway + swallowing safety (aspiration/ventilation
  risk correctly surfaced).
- Empiric immunotherapy started only after infectious causes are "reasonably excluded" — matches
  standard autoimmune-encephalitis practice and does not encourage premature immunosuppression.
Realistic-recovery caveat (intracellular-antigen antibodies respond less robustly) sets honest
expectations and is not misleading.

## 2. GROUNDING
Treatment claims are consistent with DeVita Ch.89 / standard paraneoplastic oncology-neurology
practice: Ma2 as high-risk onconeural antibody; limbic/brainstem phenotype; testicular germ cell
tumor (seminoma) + NSCLC associations; treat-primary-tumor-plus-immunotherapy; ~2-year repeat
screening window; first-line steroids/IVIG/plasma exchange; second-line rituximab/azathioprine/
MMF/cyclophosphamide; tocilizumab for rituximab-refractory disease; surface- vs intracellular-
antigen response gradient. No fabricated or outdated regimen. Orchiectomy as standard for testicular
GCT is correct and appropriately labelled general oncology standard.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, g/day, or numbered cycle/day schedule. All numerals are non-dose
tokens (Ma1/Ma2, PNMA2, CV2/CRMP5, anti-CD20, interleukin-6, "two years", "12th ed.", "Chapter 89").
"prednisone-class dosing" is a word, not a number.

## 4. SCOPE
Appropriately scoped as decision-support, not a directive. Defers regimen specifics for the
underlying cancers to their own stage-specific standards; routes escalation and tumor-directed
steps through neurology/oncology referral rather than prescribing.

## 5. ADVERSARIAL FLAGS
The .verdict.md is CLEAN. All four previously-flagged items (occult-tumor biopsy/orchiectomy x3
recurrences; first-line "choice guided by severity" statement) are now inline-tagged as general
standard, NOT attributed to DeVita's section on this disease. No DeVita mis-attribution remains.
The Ma1/Ma2 age-tumor split and Ma2-as-intracellular-antigen classification are also correctly
relabelled as general literature. Nothing outstanding requires REVISE.

Clinically safe, grounded, dose-free, correctly scoped, no mis-attribution. APPROVE.
