## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe or harmful directives. Key claims are correctly hedged:
- Surgery framed as "primary and usually curative," not absolute.
- Persistent post-resection hypoglycemia correctly redirected to a search for residual/metastatic
  disease rather than mislabelled as a separate endocrine disorder — this is the safe, correct call
  and prevents a missed-recurrence false-negative.
- Interim hypoglycemia measures (glucocorticoids, continuous carbohydrate provision) are named
  without dose and explicitly deferred to endocrinology — no risk of a clinician acting on a
  self-directed steroid regimen.
No definitive/absolute statement that oversteps decision-support.

## 2. GROUNDING (DeVita/NCCN standard of care)
Consistent with standard SFT/Doege-Potter oncology:
- big-IGF-2 (incompletely processed high-molecular-weight IGF-2) as the paraneoplastic mechanism — correct.
- SFT resistance to anthracycline-based chemotherapy — correct and standard.
- Activity of angiogenesis-directed multi-targeted TKIs as the best-evidenced systemic option — correct.
- Size >10 cm and mitotic rate >4/10 HPF as metastatic-risk features; retroperitoneal site as high
  distant-recurrence risk — consistent with accepted SFT risk stratification.
No fabricated or outdated regimen. Notably, the draft does NOT name specific agents or sequencing and
includes an explicit "What is not addressed here" section that bounds its DeVita attribution to what
DeVita actually states (resistance to anthracyclines + TKI activity; no RT role detailed). No claim is
mis-attributed to DeVita.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, or numbered schedule. The only numerics (>10 cm, >4/10 HPF, 10-year
horizon) are pathologic risk-stratification thresholds and a surveillance timeframe — not drug doses —
and are appropriate.

## 4. SCOPE
Appropriately hedged throughout: multidisciplinary sarcoma-team framing, "individualized by the team,"
referral triggers, and explicit non-substitution of hypoglycemia monitoring for surveillance imaging.
Reads as decision-support, not a directive.
