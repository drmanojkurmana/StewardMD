## 2. Grounding (DeVita 12th ed.)

- Stewart-Treves poor wound healing → limb-salvage difficulty / amputation in some cases.

- Retained (at least initial) anthracycline AND taxane sensitivity, with taxanes being a
  relative area of activity specific to angiosarcoma vs sarcomas generally — near-verbatim to DeVita.
- Antiangiogenic TKI / VEGF-pathway rationale via KDR mutation and MYC/FLT4 amplification.
- Propranolol + metronomic vinblastine as a reported small-study emerging strategy.
- Higher TMB in H&N cutaneous subgroup and PD-1+CTLA-4 combination responses in that subgroup.
- Poor prognosis with age/depth/size adverse factors; high local+distant (lung) recurrence.
No fabricated or outdated regimen. No claim is mis-attributed to DeVita. The only
generalization ("elderly men" → "elderly patients") is clinically immaterial and not a
fabrication. Sequential-therapy framing is a reasonable synthesis with no invented
regimen/drug/dose attached.
