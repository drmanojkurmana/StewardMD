## 1. Safety
Clinically safe. Core claims match standard of care for a benign periscapular lesion:
- Observation for asymptomatic/radiologically typical lesions; marginal excision reserved for symptomatic disease — correct and appropriately conservative.
- No systemic therapy / no radiotherapy role — correct; avoids over-treatment of a benign entity.
- Seroma/haematoma flagged as the main excision complication — accurate and useful.
- Explicitly warns against mislabelling an atypical mass as elastofibroma without confirmation, to avoid missing a chest-wall soft-tissue sarcoma — this is the key false-negative guard and it is present. No unsafe or absolute directive; nothing that would harm a clinician who followed it.
