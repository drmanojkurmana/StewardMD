This is standard endocrine practice for tumor-induced hypoglycemia but is not sourced to DeVita's text as cited, and no other DeVita location was checked/found for it. Flag as an addition beyond the cited source, though clinically uncontroversial.
- **Neoadjuvant systemic therapy as an option for borderline-resectable disease**: DeVita's passage does not mention neoadjuvant therapy, sequencing, or borderline-resectable management at all — it only covers resection, chemo-resistance, and TKI activity. This is reasonable generic sarcoma-team practice but not attributable to the cited paragraph.

Note: the sidecar deliberately avoids naming sunitinib/sorafenib specifically (DeVita names both) and instead generalizes to "multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitors" — this is a conservative omission, not a fabrication.
