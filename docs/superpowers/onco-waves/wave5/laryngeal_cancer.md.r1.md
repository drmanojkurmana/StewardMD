## 1. Safety
No unsafe, misleading, or harmfully absolute statements. Consistently hedged
("generally", "usually", "tends to", "roughly"). Two safety-positive inclusions:
- Aspiration/pulmonary-reserve prerequisite before supraglottic (partial) laryngectomy (lines 44-45).
- Explicit airway red-flag: stridor / rapidly worsening obstruction warrants urgent airway
  management ahead of oncologic treatment, and airway anticipation in bulky/endophytic disease
  (lines 94-96, 113-115). Good false-negative guarding.
Minor: "early recurrences after radiotherapy respond well to surgical salvage" (line 34-35) is
mildly optimistic (post-RT salvage carries higher morbidity/lower success than de novo), but it
is qualified with "usually still possible" — acceptable, not misleading.

## 2. Grounding (DeVita/NCCN)
Consistent with standard of care. Notably strong attribution hygiene: the draft explicitly does
NOT attribute to DeVita's larynx chapter claims that come from broader HN chapters — the
concurrent platinum chemoradiation claim (lines 55-57) and the recurrent/metastatic systemic
therapy claim (EGFR antibody + anti-PD-1, lines 80-86) are both labelled "general oncology
standard, not from DeVita's section on this disease." This is exactly the relabelling the R1
mandate requires; no DeVita mis-citation found.
- T4a → primary total laryngectomy + adjuvant preferred (lines 62-64): presented as
  "consensus-preferred / several studies suggested," not attributed to DeVita — consistent with NCCN.
- Verrucous carcinoma → surgery preferred, RT reserved (lines 30-32): matches classic teaching.
- CIS/microinvasive, early glottic/supraglottic modality trade-offs: standard and correctly hedged.
No fabricated or outdated regimens.

## 3. Dose-free
PASS. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, or numbered chemo schedules. The only numerics are staging (T1-T4a,
N2b-N3), a follow-up interval ("~three months"), and a tumour-volume selection threshold
("roughly under 6 mL"). None is a drug dose; the volume cut-off is a patient-selection criterion,
not a treatment dose — permissible.

## 4. Scope
Appropriately decision-support, not directive. Repeated MDT-first framing (lines 10-12, 108-112),
"choice is less clear-cut," "depends on local expertise," and deliberate omission of
regimen sequencing (lines 84-86). Well scoped.
