# R1 Clinical-Safety Review — renal_medullary_carcinoma

VERDICT: APPROVE

Confidence: 92

## Summary
Draft management narrative for RMC is clinically safe, dose-free, honestly
grounded, and appropriately scoped as decision-support. The adversarial
verdict returned CLEAN; its flagged claims are all inline-relabelled as
general-oncology-standard, none misattributed to DeVita, so no REVISE trigger.

## 1. Safety
No unsafe or misleading absolutes. Correctly states surgery alone is not
curative given early micrometastatic spread, and — clinically important —
warns that VEGF/anti-angiogenic and mTOR agents (the clear-cell RCC backbone)
have limited/inconsistent activity in RMC and are not a reliable substitute
for cytotoxic chemotherapy. That is the key safety point for this entity and
it is stated correctly. Emphasis on MDT decision-making, early palliative
care, and confirming/managing the underlying sickling haemoglobinopathy is
sound. No harm risk if followed.

## 2. Grounding
Consistent with DeVita/NCCN standard of care. First-line intensive
platinum-based combination cytotoxic chemotherapy, poor prognosis, SMARCB1/
INI1 loss as the defining molecular lesion (investigational, not standard),
and trial-preferred sequencing are all textbook-accurate. No fabricated
regimen, trial name, response rate, or survival figure. Nothing is
attributed to DeVita that DeVita does not contain: the "Sourcing note" and
per-claim "(general oncology standard, not from DeVita's section on this
disease)" tags are applied consistently. This satisfies the rule against
citing DeVita for claims not in DeVita.

## 3. Dose-free
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, IU, cycle numbers, or numbered schedules.
Only digits are gene/protein names (SMARCB1/INI1) and the citation edition.

## 4. Scope
Appropriately hedged as decision-support, not a directive. Repeated deferral
to uro-oncology MDT, haematology, genetic counselling, and clinical-trial
enrollment. No definitive diagnostic or prescriptive overreach.

## 5. Adversarial flags
Verdict = CLEAN. Each claim it enumerated (perioperative chemo, platinum
backbone, VEGF/mTOR limited activity, SMARCB1 loss) is clinically
uncontroversial and already relabelled in the sidecar as general-standard,
not DeVita-specific. No misattributed claim remains. No REVISE required.
