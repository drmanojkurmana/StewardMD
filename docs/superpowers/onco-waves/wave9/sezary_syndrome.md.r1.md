# R1 Clinical-Safety Review - Sezary syndrome (sezary_syndrome)

Verdict: APPROVE

Reviewer: stewardmd-clinical-reviewer (R1, hard gate)
Source cited: DeVita, Hellman & Rosenberg's Cancer: Principles & Practice of Oncology, 12th ed.
Adversarial verdict file (.verdict.md): NOT PRESENT - no prior flags to reconcile.

## 1. Safety
No unsafe, misleading, or harm-inducing statement found. The one near-absolute
claim - allo-HSCT as "the only potentially curative option" - is standard of care
and is correctly hedged ("reported to offer", "regarded as"). No diagnostic
directive, no instruction that could harm if followed. Infection risk and
large-cell transformation are appropriately flagged as monitoring priorities.

## 2. Grounding (DeVita/NCCN standard of care)
Treatment claims are consistent with standard MF/SS management:
- ECP as a mainstay for the leukemic compartment; combination with bexarotene/IFN-alfa.
- Mogamulizumab (anti-CCR4) higher response in SS vs vorinostat (MAVORIC) with
  clearance of circulating disease - accurate, correctly scoped to SS.
- Brentuximab vedotin gated on CD30 expression (ALCANZA) - accurate.
- HDAC inhibitors (romidepsin, vorinostat) FDA-approved for refractory disease.
- Bexarotene toxicities (hyperlipidemia, central hypothyroidism) - accurate.
- Single-agent preferred over multi-agent cytotoxics; TSEBT for T4 erythroderma.
No fabricated or outdated regimen. Attribution is honest: line 13 explicitly labels
topical steroids as "general oncology standard, not from DeVita's section," and line
49 explicitly excludes investigational agents (KIR3DL2, PI3K inhibitors) for lack of
grounding. No claim is mis-attributed to DeVita.

## 3. Dose-free
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, or numbered schedule. "T4" is a TNMB stage
(erythroderma), not a dose. Passes DOSE-FREE policy.

## 4. Scope
Appropriately hedged as decision-support ("guided by", "considered for",
"generally preferred", "in practice"). Not directive. Refer-out and tertiary-center
evaluation are emphasized.
