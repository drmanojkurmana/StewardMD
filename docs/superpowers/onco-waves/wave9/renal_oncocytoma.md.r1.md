## 1. Safety — PASS
No unsafe, harmful, or oversteping statement. The one clinically dangerous trap for
this disease (treating "benign oncocytoma" as certain when it cannot be reliably
separated from chromophobe RCC / hybrid oncocytic-chromophobe tumours) is handled
correctly and repeatedly: diagnostic uncertainty is foregrounded in the Overview,
work-up, surgery, monitoring, and referral sections. "Benign, non-metastasising" is
the correct textbook characterization and is consistently contextualized with the
residual-malignancy caveat, so it will not lull a clinician into false certainty.
Escalation-to-surgery triggers (malignancy not excludable, hybrid features, interval
growth) are sound. No absolute directive.
