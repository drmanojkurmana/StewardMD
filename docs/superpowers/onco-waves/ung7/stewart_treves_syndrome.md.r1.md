## 1. Safety
No unsafe, harm-inducing, or overstepping directive found. Aggressive-management
claims (wide excision, amputation/shoulder disarticulation/hemipelvectomy when
limb-sparing control is not achievable, adjuvant RT) are each hedged as
"treatment of choice," "recommended when... cannot be adequately controlled,"
"should be considered." Strong phrases ("essentially all resected cases,"
"neither has been shown to improve overall survival") are bounded by
decision-support framing ("should be considered," "in this disease") and do not
instruct a clinician to withhold or apply therapy absolutely. Monitoring
guidance (re-biopsy any new violaceous/nodular change rather than reassurance)
is appropriately cautious and safe.

## 2. Grounding (DeVita/NCCN)
Consistent with standard cutaneous-angiosarcoma / Stewart-Treves care in DeVita
12th ed: four clinical variants, multifocality and early hematogenous spread,
surgery as primary modality, amputation for uncontrollable extremity disease,
adjuvant RT, chemosensitivity to anthracyclines and taxanes, paclitaxel response
in lymphatic spread, palliative intent in metastatic disease. No fabricated or
outdated regimen. Attribution discipline is correct: the VEGF/antiangiogenic and
KDR/FLT4/MYC molecular material is explicitly labelled "general oncology
standard, not from DeVita's section on this disease" and downgraded to
"molecular background... area for specialist-directed, trial-informed therapy" -
so DeVita is NOT cited for a treatment claim it does not make. Prognosis figures
are explicitly scoped to cutaneous angiosarcoma broadly, not Stewart-Treves-
specific. No mis-attribution.

## 3. Dose-free
Confirmed. No mg, mg/m2, AUC, cycle counts, or numbered schedules. Drug/class
names only (anthracycline-based, taxane, paclitaxel) - permitted; doses belong
in structured templates. Numerics present are prognostic (50% by 15 months,
10-30% 5-yr, <5 cm), not doses - acceptable.

## 4. Scope
Appropriately hedged as decision-support: "treatment of choice," "should be
considered," "recommended when," referral triggers, and an explicit
"no universally accepted staging system" caveat. Reads as guidance, not directive.

## 5. Adversarial flags
None to reconcile (no .verdict.md). The single non-DeVita claim is already
self-relabelled inline as general standard - the exact remediation R1 would
otherwise require.
