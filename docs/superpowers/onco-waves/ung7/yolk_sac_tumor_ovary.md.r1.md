# R1 clinical-safety review — yolk_sac_tumor_ovary

APPROVE — clinically safe, grounded in DeVita 12th ed, dose-free, and appropriately scoped as decision-support.

## 1. SAFETY
No unsafe, misleading, or harmful directive. Core message (surgery first, fertility-sparing even with
metastatic disease, adjuvant platinum/etoposide/bleomycin for essentially all pure yolk sac tumours) is
the correct, non-harmful standard of care. The one potentially risky idea — observation after resection —
is heavily caveated ("treated cautiously," "select low-volume centres with close marker/imaging follow-up")
AND explicitly labelled as general standard, not a DeVita carve-out for this histology. The draft
correctly does NOT extend the dysgerminoma/immature-teratoma IA chemo-sparing exception to yolk sac tumour.
No absolute claim overstepping decision-support: strong statements ("always an option," "essentially every
stage") are hedged and attributed, not issued as commands.

## 2. GROUNDING
Consistent with DeVita/NCCN standard of care. Adversarial verdict confirmed by direct source read that all
core claims (MGCT epidemiology/median age, near-universal unilaterality, fertility-sparing surgery with
metastases, yolk sac biology — hematogenous spread, AFP, Schiller-Duval bodies, the IA chemo-sparing
exceptions, adjuvant bleomycin-etoposide-cisplatin, extrapolation from testicular GCT) trace verbatim/
near-verbatim to DeVita section 52. No fabricated or outdated regimen. Radiotherapy correctly excluded
(dysgerminoma, not yolk sac, is the radiosensitive subtype). Non-DeVita claims (MDT referral, IA
observation option) are honestly flagged inline as general standard — no claim is mis-attributed to DeVita.

## 3. DOSE-FREE
Confirmed. Only digits are staging/grading nomenclature ("FIGO stage IA," "grade 1 or 2"). No mg, mg/m2,
AUC, cycle count, or numbered schedule.

## 4. SCOPE
Appropriately hedged. Explicit dose deferral to structured protocol templates, "Deliberate omissions"
section, and consistent decision-support framing. Not directive.

## 5. ADVERSARIAL FLAGS
Verdict = CLEAN. Its only note is cosmetic and non-blocking.

## Advisory (non-blocking)
- Verdict suggests tightening "platinum agent"/"platinum-etoposide-bleomycin" to DeVita's specific
  "bleomycin-etoposide-cisplatin." Optional only: the generic "platinum" phrasing is clinically defensible
  (carboplatin-etoposide regimens exist for ovarian GCT) and is a conservative genericization, not a
  fabrication. Since the narrative attributes the regimen naming to DeVita, matching DeVita's exact
  "cisplatin" wording is the cleaner grounding, but this does not gate approval.
