# CliniX fact-check, 2026-10-25

An independent second model checked the 15 most important claims in each of the 87 new O&G, paediatrics and surgery pathways against the StewardMD knowledge base, the owner-authorised source texts and current guidelines. Workers then re-checked every finding before changing anything.

## Examples of serious fixes

- Caesarean section: oxytocin after delivery was taught as a 10 IU IV bolus. Now 5 IU by slow IV injection.
- Eclampsia airway care (shared O&G skill): removed the mouth gag; nothing goes between the teeth of a woman having a fit.
- Endometriosis: add-back therapy now starts with the GnRH analogue, not after six months.
- Pre-eclampsia: HELLP haemolysis is shown by schistocytes, not burr cells.
- Severe acute malnutrition (shared paediatric skill): MUAC thresholds updated to WHO and MOHFW (SAM below 11.5 cm, normal from 12.5 cm, ages 6 to 59 months).
- Paediatric normal values (shared skill): hypoglycaemia action threshold is now 70 mg/dL, and a venous pH row was added.
- Many case files: vitals, examination findings, investigations and OSCE prompts made consistent with the case diagnosis.
