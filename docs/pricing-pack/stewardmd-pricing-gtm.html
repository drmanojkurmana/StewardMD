The one idea

How StewardMD is priced

Free where it is cheap and habit-forming. Paid where it costs us money or scales to a team.
Deterministic tools (reasoning engine, drug safety, calculators, knowledge base) cost us almost nothing and build the daily habit, so they stay free forever, that is our acquisition engine. We charge for AI compute, imaging, per-patient services, and multi-user hospital workflow.
1 · Meter by rupee, not by count
  • A text answer costs ₹0.03; an imaging read ₹0.40; a voice consult ₹3-5.
  • Caps are worst-case ceilings, typical use is ~15% of the cap.
2 · Every tier profits after 18% GST
  • Price always exceeds maximum AI exposure.
  • Heavy per-patient use is quota + token metered, never flat-unlimited.
3 · B2C is the funnel, hospitals are the revenue
  • Cheap individual tiers capture doctors.
  • Doctors carry us into their hospital (land and expand).
Never hard-block a safety warning (drug interaction, renal/QT, critical value). It is ethically required and it builds the trust that sells the paid tiers.

Finance guardrails

AI cost discipline (why we cannot go bankrupt)

  • Daily text-AI caps (₹/day): Free 0.5 · Trainee 5 · Co-Res 6 pooled · Pro 10 · Physician 15 · Physician Pro 25. Set so under 5% of paying users ever hit them.
  • AI Tokens, not "half-price rupees": we sell abundance. One wallet, big-number packs with a genuine bulk discount, 1M ₹29 · 5M ₹119 · 15M ₹435 ₹299. Blended cost ≈ ₹12/M, so every pack profits (~1.6-2.4x). Powers all overage (text, imaging, voice, FollowCare).
  • Per-patient AI (voice consult, FollowCare) is quota + token metered, never flat-unlimited.
  • On-device voice dictation costs us ₹0, a real edge versus per-minute scribe competitors.
  • Live tuning: the AI Control Center shows per-user daily cost, so caps are tuned from real hit-rates with no redeploy.
  • Target: AI cost of goods stays under 25% of revenue on every tier.
Launch offer
Founding Doctor: first 500 doctors, ₹399 for the whole year.
A fixed annual AI token pool (≈ ₹240/yr cost ceiling), then top-up token packs. Profitable even at full use (~₹150/yr, 38% margin). A controlled land-grab that seeds 500 loyal advocates; they roll to Pro ₹599/mo at renewal. Public countdown drives urgency.
Merchandising rules
  • Every price shows a struck anchor (launch vs regular, annual vs monthly x12, token bulk vs entry rate) so it reads as a saving. Anchors must be genuine, never a fabricated MRP (India ASCI/CCPA).
  • Referral: refer a doctor, both get bonus tokens (e.g. 5M) on activation.
  • Coupons work on Android + Web only. Apple forbids external codes in IAP, so the coupon field is hidden on iOS; iOS uses Apple Offer Codes or "redeem on stewardmd.in" (buy on web, entitlement syncs to the account, unlocks the iOS app).
Go-to-market

How we grow

Positioning

The clinical operating layer for doctors and hospitals, not "an AI scribe". From thinking through a case, to deciding, documenting, the patient record, follow-up and hospital workflow.

Think (Free) → Learn (Trainee) → Practice (Pro) → Run your clinic (Physician) → Without limits (Physician Pro) → Run care (Hospital)

Who we target

  • Residents / trainees - volume + virality, carry us into hospitals
  • Practising physicians - core B2C revenue (Pro, Physician)
  • Hospitals 50-500 beds - the revenue tier, landed via doctors already inside

Channels, cheapest first

  1. Community / word of mouth - resident groups, colleges (₹0-50 CAC)
  2. App-store optimisation - "MELD calculator", "antibiotic dose", high intent
  3. Programmatic SEO - thousands of drug / calculator / guideline pages that capture Google searches (the compounding moat)
  4. KOLs, colleges, CME, conferences
  5. Referral loop - refer a doctor, both get ₹150 credits
  6. Paid ads - last, and small, only after LTV:CAC is proven
SEO prerequisite: the app is behind a private gate today, so search engines cannot index it. Ship a public, crawlable content site (/learn, /drugs, /calculators) with an "Open in app" call to action, that is step one of growth.
