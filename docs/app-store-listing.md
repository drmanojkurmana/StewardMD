## Full description
StewardMD is a clinical **decision-support** tool for qualified healthcare professionals. It brings antimicrobial stewardship guidance, differential reasoning, and everyday clinical references together in one fast, offline-capable app.

**For qualified clinicians only.** StewardMD supports — it does not replace — your clinical judgment. Every recommendation must be verified against current guidelines, local protocols, and full prescribing references before use.

What's inside:
• **Antibiotic decision support** — structured, guideline-referenced empiric guidance with stewardship in mind.
• **Clinical reasoning** — build a differential from findings across infective and non-infective causes, with the discriminators and next steps to consider.
• **Drug & interaction lookup** — a searchable drug reference and interaction checker.
• **Clinical tools** — calculators, electrolyte correction, antibiogram, syndromic references and guidelines.
• **Knowledge library** — searchable disease and syndrome references.
• **Works offline** and stays current automatically.

Privacy-first: no ads, no tracking, and your saved work can be deleted anytime in-app. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

*Educational clinical decision-support aid. Not a diagnostic device. Not a substitute for professional examination, investigation, or judgment. Drug information is AI-summarised from official labels and pending clinician review — always verify doses against the primary source.*

## Notes for review team (App Review "Notes" field)
- StewardMD is intended for qualified, registered healthcare professionals; a first-run gate requires the user to attest to this and accept the disclaimer/terms.
- It is decision-support/educational; it does not diagnose or prescribe autonomously.
- If asked about the institutional "Ward Sync" feature: it is access-gated to a partner institution's own clinicians using their own credentials; that institution is the controller of any patient data shown.
- Reviewer access: **use Guest mode** — tap "Try guest access" on the sign-in screen; no login is required to review the core features (decision support, reasoning, drug lookup, tools). Only cloud sync / saved-cases require a Google login; if the reviewer wants to test that, a throwaway Google account can be supplied on request.
