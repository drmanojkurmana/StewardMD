## Reviewer: confirm units

The PhysioNet page and the 2014 paper do not state units for pCO2 and BDecf. The app shows pCO2 in kPa (header median 7.0, range 0.7 to 12.3) and BDecf in mmol/L. Please confirm both against the source hospital's reporting before sign-off. Risk-factor and Induced header fields are not shown in vignettes because their 0/1 coding is unconfirmed.
