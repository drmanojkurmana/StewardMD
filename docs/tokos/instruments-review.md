# Tokós Instruments Review Notes

Items for clinical review by an obstetrician covering the 29 obstetric and gynaecological instruments in `tokos/decks/instruments.json`.

The dataset was compiled and rewritten from an owner-supplied departmental practicals sheet. Image licence is currently unconfirmed (owner-supplied temporary base). All clinical text is English-only and marked `ai_drafted` pending obstetrician sign-off in the Review Desk.

---

## Items Requiring Obstetrician Verification

1. **Uterine sound normal length:**
   - The figure of 7.5 to 8 cm (normal adult uterine cavity length) in `in-07` is taken from the source sheet. Reviewer to confirm standard clinical reference values (e.g. 6 to 8 cm vs 7.5 to 8 cm in parous women).

2. **Omitted eponymous sound tests:**
   - The source sheet listed eponymous tests with the uterine sound: "De Jakartha / D'Acosta test" and "Clark's test" (for endometrial carcinoma / friability). These were deliberately omitted from the deck as unverified and obsolete in modern evidence-based practice. Reviewer to confirm omission.

3. **Manual Vacuum Aspiration (MVA) syringe:**
   - Syringe capacity (typically 60 mL) and negative vacuum pressure (typically 60 to 66 cmHg / 26 inHg) were left out of `in-27`. The stated gestational age limit of 8 weeks follows the source sheet; WHO and national guidelines permit MVA up to 12 or 14 weeks depending on cannula size. Reviewer to verify whether the gestational threshold should be updated to 12 weeks.

4. **Anterior vaginal wall retractor:**
   - In `in-03`, the sheet claimed that the retractor "may be used as a curette after delivery" (to remove membranes or clots). This is retained as a source sheet claim but warrants clinical review regarding safety and contemporary practice.

5. **Omitted Foley catheter uses:**
   - Three historical uses listed on the source sheet were deliberately excluded:
     - Extra-amniotic ethacridine lactate instillation for second-trimester termination (obsolete).
     - "To push the bag of membranes" during cervical encirclage (unsupported in standard operative guidelines).
     - Diagnosis of cervical incompetence (non-standard).
   - Reviewer to confirm these omissions.

6. **Corrected instrument names:**
   - Eponyms and typographical errors on the source sheet were corrected:
     - *Smellie's perforator* (corrected from "Smellie perforator" / spelling variants).
     - *De Lee mucus extractor* (corrected from "Dee Lee" / "Dee's").
     - *Wrigley's outlet forceps* (corrected from "Wrigleys").
     - *Curette* (standardised from "uterine curette" / "curet").

7. **Mucus sucker photograph:**
   - The photograph in `in-23` depicts a bulb-type infant mucus extractor/sucker rather than a traditional trap-bottle catheter De Lee assembly. The text reflects both the bulb type pictured and the classic De Lee catheter extractor. Reviewer to confirm appropriateness of the image or recommend replacement.

8. **Image provenance and licensing:**
   - All 29 instrument photographs are from the owner-supplied practicals sheet. Manufacturer marks, vendor catalogue numbers, and one watermark have been removed. The image licence is unconfirmed and flagged as temporary. Replacement with CC BY or newly generated original images is planned prior to public release.
