4. Verify every drug, dose and interaction

  • Always confirm the drug, dose, route, frequency, duration, contraindications, warnings, and interactions against the current statutory product label and an authoritative prescribing reference before prescribing or administering.
  • The interaction checker is a non-exhaustive screening aid built from open data sources; the absence of an alert is not evidence of safety. Severity grades are indicative only.
  • Draft or provisional values (including renal dose bands and brand↔composition mappings) are labelled as such in-app and must be verified locally before use.

Treat all such output as a prompt for your own reasoning, never as an answer to be adopted without independent verification. The app performs no automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on any individual without your involvement.
