Effective 13 July 2026 · StewardMD is operated by Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana.
StewardMD is a clinical decision-support mobile app (iOS and Android) for qualified, registered healthcare professionals. It supports — it does not replace — your clinical judgment. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional evaluation, examination, or treatment.

1. For clinicians only

StewardMD is a mobile application for iOS and Android, distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. It is intended solely for use by qualified, registered healthcare professionals in the course of their practice. It is not a consumer health app and is not intended for patients or the general public. If you are not a healthcare professional, do not rely on this app; consult a doctor.

2. Decision support, not a decision

All output — differentials, confidence scores, empiric antibiotic suggestions, doses, calculators, scores, and any AI-generated text — is provided for informational and educational decision-support purposes only. The treating clinician remains fully responsible for every diagnostic and treatment decision and must independently verify all information against the individual patient, current authoritative references, and local protocols before acting.

3. Verify doses, drugs and interactions

  • Antibiotic and drug recommendations reflect standard adult guidance and must be adjusted for the individual patient — age, weight, renal and hepatic function, allergies, pregnancy, comorbidities, and local resistance/antibiogram.
  • Always confirm dose, route, frequency, duration, contraindications, and interactions against the current product label and an authoritative prescribing reference before prescribing.
  • Renal dose bands and other draft values are labelled as such and must be verified locally.

4. AI & "beta" features

AI-generated explanations and the Clinical Reasoning engine (marked beta) are provisional and may be incomplete or incorrect. Confidence scores are transparent rule-based indicators, not validated probabilities. Nothing in the app is a confirmed diagnosis. Treat all such output as a prompt for your own reasoning, never as an answer to be followed.

5. Knowledge sources

Clinical content is reproduced and adapted from standard references (including Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine and national/international guidelines such as ICMR, WHO and IDSA) and is provided for reference. It may not reflect the very latest evidence or your local guidance. Where content is under review, this is indicated in-app.

6. Emergencies

StewardMD is not for use in an emergency as a substitute for appropriate clinical care. In an emergency, follow your institution's protocols and established standards of care.
