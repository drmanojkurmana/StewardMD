# 🏢 16. Real Estate, Architecture & Interior UI

*This module covers aesthetics translated directly from physical building design into 2D interfaces.*

---

## 1. Parametricism / Generative Architecture (Zaha Hadid)
**Psychological Intent:** Bleeding-edge future, organic flow, defying gravity.
*   **Colors:** White concrete, silver titanium, sweeping glass reflections.
*   **Typography:** Fluid, ultra-modern extended Sans-Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Completely rejects straight lines. Flowing, algorithm-generated curves.
*   **Negative Space:** Aerodynamic and sweeping.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** 3D rendered sweeping curves, ribbed structures.
*   **Animations:** Flowing path animations.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG curved paths, Three.js.

---

## 2. High-Tech Architecture (Pompidou Center)
**Psychological Intent:** Radical transparency, industrial function over form.
*   **Colors:** Exposed steel gray, bright primary colors (red pipes, blue vents).
*   **Typography:** Industrial stencils.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** The "guts" of the website are exposed. 
*   **Negative Space:** Filled with scaffolding and wireframes.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Exposed grids, technical blueprints.
*   **Animations:** Mechanical sliding.
*   **Tech Stack:** Visible CSS grid lines (`border: 1px solid`).

---

## 3. Postmodern Architecture
**Psychological Intent:** Playful, ironic historical references, rejecting minimalism.
*   **Colors:** Bright, clashing pastels and neons on classical structures.
*   **Typography:** Mixing 80s geometric fonts with classical Roman serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Chaotic, deliberate misalignment.
*   **Negative Space:** Filled with geometric primitives (spheres, pyramids).
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Neon columns, giant geometric forms.
*   **Animations:** Bouncy and ironic.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS `clip-path` for geometric masks.

---

## 4. Prairie School (Frank Lloyd Wright)
**Psychological Intent:** Harmony with nature, extreme horizontality.
*   **Colors:** Warm natural woods, autumn leaves, terracotta.
*   **Typography:** Craftsman-style lettering.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Extreme horizontal emphasis. Wide, sweeping banners.
*   **Negative Space:** Organic integration.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Geometric stained glass patterns.
*   **Animations:** Slow, horizontal panning.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS Flexbox (horizontal wrapping).

---

## 5. Vernacular Architecture
**Psychological Intent:** Indigenous wisdom, earthy, localized logic.
*   **Colors:** Mud-brick red, clay, dried grass.
*   **Typography:** Hand-drawn or rough textures.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Organic, irregular.
*   **Negative Space:** Textured with plaster or mud.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Woven textures, raw materials.
*   **Animations:** Static.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG filters for rough edges.
