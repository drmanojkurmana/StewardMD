# 🧵 28. Textiles, Materials & Patterns

*This module focuses on the micro-textures that make up our physical world. It brings tactile sensations into UI design.*

---

## 1. Kintsugi
**Psychological Intent:** Healing, resilience, honoring flaws.
*   **Colors:** Matte black/white ceramics with Gold veins.
*   **Typography:** Delicate Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Fractured, separated by gold lines.
*   **Negative Space:** Vast.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Cracked ceramic textures.
*   **Animations:** Gold lines drawing themselves.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG path animations (`stroke-dasharray`).

---

## 2. Terrazzo / Memphis
**Psychological Intent:** Playful, architectural.
*   **Colors:** Muted backgrounds speckled with highly saturated chips.
*   **Typography:** Architectural Sans-Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** White cards over chaotic terrazzo backgrounds.
*   **Negative Space:** Inside the cards.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Seamless SVG terrazzo patterns.
*   **Animations:** Chips parallax shifting.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG `<pattern>`.

---

## 3. Kinfolk Aesthetic
**Psychological Intent:** Slow living, artisan curation.
*   **Colors:** Raw linen, natural wood, earthy neutrals.
*   **Typography:** Highly refined Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Curated, messy wooden table layouts.
*   **Negative Space:** Linen textures.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Artisan pottery, coffee, plants.
*   **Animations:** Very slow.
*   **Tech Stack:** High-res texture overlays.

---

## 4. Shibori / Tie-Dye
**Psychological Intent:** Bohemian, handmade, Japanese tradition.
*   **Colors:** Indigo blue and stark white.
*   **Typography:** Minimalist to balance the busy pattern.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Flowing.
*   **Negative Space:** Formed by the white folds of the dye.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Repeating folded dye patterns.
*   **Animations:** Ink bleeding effects.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS blending modes.

---

## 5. Ikat
**Psychological Intent:** Global artisan, fuzzy geometry.
*   **Colors:** High contrast woven colors.
*   **Typography:** Bold.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Woven grids.
*   **Negative Space:** Minimal.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Fuzzy, feathered-edged geometric patterns.
*   **Animations:** Horizontal shifting (like a loom).
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG filters to blur horizontal edges.

---

## 6. Tartan / Plaid
**Psychological Intent:** Tradition, winter warmth, Scottish heritage.
*   **Colors:** Deep reds, greens, navy interlocking.
*   **Typography:** Traditional Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Strict orthogonal interlocking grids.
*   **Negative Space:** None.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Woven wool textures.
*   **Animations:** Static.
*   **Tech Stack:** Multiple `repeating-linear-gradient` in CSS.

---

## 7. Houndstooth
**Psychological Intent:** High fashion, classic tailoring.
*   **Colors:** Strictly Black and White.
*   **Typography:** Fashion-forward (Didot).
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Jagged check patterns.
*   **Negative Space:** Woven into the pattern.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** The iconic broken check shape.
*   **Animations:** Optical illusion vibrations on scroll.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG `<pattern>` fills.

---

## 8. Toile de Jouy
**Psychological Intent:** French pastoral luxury, aristocratic.
*   **Colors:** Crisp white background, single-color intricate scenes (Blue or Red ink).
*   **Typography:** Elegant scripts.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Repeating pastoral scenes.
*   **Negative Space:** Crisp white fabric.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Intricate monochrome pastoral scenes.
*   **Animations:** None.
*   **Tech Stack:** Background image repeating.

---

## 9. Damask
**Psychological Intent:** Goth, baroque, heavy luxury.
*   **Colors:** Deep purple, black, rich crimson.
*   **Typography:** Gothic lettering.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Reversible figured fabric patterns.
*   **Negative Space:** Symmetrical floral voids.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Heavy, luxurious, repeating floral motifs.
*   **Animations:** Shimmering silk effects.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS `background-blend-mode`.

---

## 10. Paisley
**Psychological Intent:** 1960s psychedelic rock, Persian heritage.
*   **Colors:** Vibrant, clashing psychedelic colors.
*   **Typography:** Rubber-band 60s fonts.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Swirling teardrop motifs.
*   **Negative Space:** Chaotic.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** The teardrop motif with curved upper ends.
*   **Animations:** Swirling, hypnotic rotation.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG animations.

---

## 11. Suminagashi (Paper Marbling)
**Psychological Intent:** Chaos, liquid flow, bookbinding tradition.
*   **Colors:** Black ink floating on water, or vibrant Turkish Ebru colors.
*   **Typography:** Classic book Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Fluid, zero straight lines.
*   **Negative Space:** Delicate concentric rings.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Liquid chaotic patterns.
*   **Animations:** Real-time fluid simulation.
*   **Tech Stack:** WebGL fluid shaders.

---

## 12. Dazzle Camouflage
**Psychological Intent:** Confusion, optical illusion, WWI history.
*   **Colors:** High-contrast Black and White.
*   **Typography:** Blocky military fonts.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Intersecting geometric lines designed to break silhouettes.
*   **Negative Space:** Disorienting.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Sharp, unpredictable diagonal stripes.
*   **Animations:** Dizzying parallax.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS Grid heavily skewed and rotated.

---

## 13. Quilling
**Psychological Intent:** Delicate craftsmanship, patience.
*   **Colors:** Vibrant colored paper strips.
*   **Typography:** Round, curled scripts.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Circular, rolled shapes.
*   **Negative Space:** The shadows cast by the 3D paper strips.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Thin strips of paper rolled and glued together.
*   **Animations:** Unrolling spirals.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG path drawing with heavy drop shadows.
