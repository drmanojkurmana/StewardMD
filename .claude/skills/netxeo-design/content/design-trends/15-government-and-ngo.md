# 🏛️ 15. Government, NGO & Institutional UI

*This module covers public infrastructure, mass communication, and geopolitical aesthetics.*

---

## 1. Gov.uk / Civic Brutalism
**Psychological Intent:** 100% accessibility, zero confusion, absolute utility.
*   **Colors:** High-contrast White, Black, and standard Link Blue. No gradients.
*   **Typography:** GDS Transport (or highly legible system fonts). Huge text.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Single column. Left-aligned everything.
*   **Negative Space:** Used to separate distinct steps in a legal process.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Zero decorative images. Only functional SVG icons.
*   **Animations:** None.
*   **Tech Stack:** Pure semantic HTML, extreme WCAG AAA accessibility.

---

## 2. Non-Profit / NGO (The "Charity" Aesthetic)
**Psychological Intent:** Empathy, urgency, hope, and direct action.
*   **Colors:** Earth greens, warm sun yellows, or urgent reds (Red Cross).
*   **Typography:** Friendly, humanistic Serifs paired with urgent bold Sans-Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Story-driven layouts leading directly to a massive "Donate" button.
*   **Negative Space:** Used to focus on the eyes of the subjects in the photography.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Emotional photojournalism (looking directly at the camera).
*   **Animations:** Progress bars filling up (fundraising goals).
*   **Tech Stack:** Stripe integration, fast loading times.

---

## 3. Constructivism (Soviet/Propaganda Art)
**Psychological Intent:** Revolution, industrial power, collective action.
*   **Colors:** Pure Red, Pure Black, stark White.
*   **Typography:** Heavy, blocky, geometric (Cyrillic-inspired). Diagonal text.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Aggressive diagonal lines (30-degree slants).
*   **Negative Space:** Used to emphasize massive, monumental figures.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Workers, gears, factories, pointing fingers. High-contrast photomontage.
*   **Animations:** Hard, snapping transitions.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS `clip-path` for aggressive diagonal slashes across the screen.

---

## 4. Solarpunk / Eco-Utopian
**Psychological Intent:** Radical optimism, sustainable future, post-capitalism.
*   **Colors:** Lush canopy greens, glowing solar golds, clear sky blues.
*   **Typography:** Art Nouveau inspired, organic curves.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Flowing, integrated with nature.
*   **Negative Space:** Sun rays, stained glass patterns.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Skyscrapers covered in forests, windmills, glass domes.
*   **Animations:** Gentle wind swaying leaves.
*   **Tech Stack:** WebGL lighting mimicking sunlight through leaves.
