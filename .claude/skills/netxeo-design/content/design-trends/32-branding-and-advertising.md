# 🏷️ 32. Branding, Advertising & Layout UI

*This module covers marketing communication, product framing, and campaign aesthetics.*

---

## 1. Corporate Guidelines (Brand Bible)
**Psychological Intent:** Strict rulebooks, objective truth.
*   **Colors:** Pantone-specific precision.
*   **Typography:** Strict typographic scales.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Modular grids, visible safe zones.
*   **Negative Space:** Mathematical.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Grid lines over logos.
*   **Animations:** None.
*   **Tech Stack:** Native CSS Grid.

---

## 2. Out of Home (OOH) / Billboard
**Psychological Intent:** Rapid comprehension at 60mph.
*   **Colors:** Ultra-high contrast.
*   **Typography:** Massive, heavy fonts. Less than 7 words.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** One singular massive focal point.
*   **Negative Space:** Eradicated to make the font bigger.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Bold photography.
*   **Animations:** Scrolling tickers.
*   **Tech Stack:** Viewport-relative typography (`vw`).

---

## 3. Minimalist Packaging (Apothecary)
**Psychological Intent:** Transparency, clinical luxury (Aesop style).
*   **Colors:** Amber glass, stark white.
*   **Typography:** Stark black Sans-Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Grid of ingredient lists.
*   **Negative Space:** 80% blank.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Minimal text.
*   **Animations:** Static.
*   **Tech Stack:** Clean CSS.

---

## 4. Clay / Isometric 3D Mockups
**Psychological Intent:** Focus purely on the UI, abstracting the hardware.
*   **Colors:** Matte pastel environments.
*   **Typography:** UI elements only.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Floating 30-degree angles.
*   **Negative Space:** Studio lighting backdrops.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Clay iPhones/Laptops without screens.
*   **Animations:** Hover to tilt.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS 3D transforms (`rotateX`).

---

## 5. Guerrilla Marketing / Street Aesthetics
**Psychological Intent:** Anti-corporate, raw, rebellious.
*   **Colors:** High contrast black/white with neon spray paint.
*   **Typography:** Stencil graffiti, wheat-paste ripples.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Overlapping layers.
*   **Negative Space:** Brick textures.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Sticker bombing, ripped posters.
*   **Animations:** Flashing strobe.
*   **Tech Stack:** `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.

---

## 6. Infomercial / Direct Response Ad
**Psychological Intent:** Pure conversion psychology. "Ugly but sells."
*   **Colors:** Bright red text, yellow highlights.
*   **Typography:** Massive, screaming headlines.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Long scrolling sales letters.
*   **Negative Space:** None. Packed with testimonials.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Massive countdown timers, flashing arrows.
*   **Animations:** Urgency timers ticking.
*   **Tech Stack:** Sticky "Buy Now" buttons.
