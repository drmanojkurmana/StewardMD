# 🍔 12. Food, Beverage & Dining UI

*This module stimulates appetite, curates cravings, and sells the culture of dining, from Michelin stars to fast food.*

---

## 1. High-End Gastronomy (The "Michelin" Aesthetic)
**Psychological Intent:** Exclusivity, art on a plate, and intimidating pricing.
*   **Colors:** Pitch black or stark white. No "food" colors like red or yellow.
*   **Typography:** Elegant, microscopic Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Asymmetrical, massive negative space. The dish is treated like a sculpture.
*   **Negative Space:** 90% of the screen.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Moody, overhead spotlight photography of tiny food.
*   **Animations:** Glacial fades.
*   **Tech Stack:** Simple HTML, massive high-res imagery.

---

## 2. Fast Food Maximalism
**Psychological Intent:** Immediate craving, high energy, cheap speed.
*   **Colors:** Ketchup Red and Mustard Yellow (scientifically proven to trigger hunger).
*   **Typography:** Thick, chunky, bouncy Sans-Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Cramped. Combos and deals flashing everywhere.
*   **Negative Space:** None.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Glossy, impossible-looking burgers. Floating fries.
*   **Animations:** Bouncy spring physics. Food dropping from the top of the screen.
*   **Tech Stack:** Framer Motion, Parallax overlapping elements.

---

## 3. Diner / Americana
**Psychological Intent:** 1950s nostalgia, comfort food, jukeboxes.
*   **Colors:** Checkerboard black-and-white, cherry red, and chrome.
*   **Typography:** Neon scripts and retro cursive.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Menu-board style layouts.
*   **Negative Space:** Diamond checkerboard patterns.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Milkshakes, classic cars, neon signs.
*   **Animations:** Flickering neon.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS `text-shadow` for neon glowing.

---

## 4. Cafe Chalkboard / Artisan Bakery
**Psychological Intent:** Hand-crafted, local, warm, smells like bread.
*   **Colors:** Flour-dusted white, slate gray, baked brown.
*   **Typography:** Hand-drawn chalk fonts, rough scripts.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Organic, center-aligned.
*   **Negative Space:** Slate chalkboard textures.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Flour smudges, coffee rings.
*   **Animations:** Sketching animations.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS `mask-image` for chalk dust effects.

---

## 5. Third-Wave Coffee (Minimalist Roaster)
**Psychological Intent:** Scientific approach to coffee, hipster aesthetics.
*   **Colors:** Matte black, craft paper brown, stark white.
*   **Typography:** Utilitarian Sans-Serifs or Monospace (looks like stamped labels).
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Strict, package-design layouts.
*   **Negative Space:** Vast and clean.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Flat-lays of coffee beans, scientific brewing equipment (Chemex).
*   **Animations:** Smooth, slow scrolls.
*   **Tech Stack:** E-commerce optimized (Shopify headless).
