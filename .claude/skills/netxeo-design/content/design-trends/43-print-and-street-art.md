# 🎨 43. Print, Street Art & Abstract Movements

*This final module covers raw physical art techniques, optical illusions, and historical abstract movements.*

---

## 1. 1990s MTV / Nickelodeon Aesthetic
**Psychological Intent:** Punk-rock attitude applied to kid's media, loud rebellion.
*   **Colors:** Slime green, hazard orange, loud checkerboards.
*   **Typography:** Ransom note, jagged, comic.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Extreme camera angles (fisheye lens), skewed grids.
*   **Negative Space:** Chaotic magazine cutouts.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Slime splatters, distorted faces.
*   **Animations:** Stop-motion jitter.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG filters for slime drips.

## 2. Op Art (Optical Illusion Art)
**Psychological Intent:** Dizzying, mind-bending geometry (Victor Vasarely).
*   **Colors:** Strictly Black and White.
*   **Typography:** Hidden within the illusions.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Undulating, mathematically warped grids.
*   **Negative Space:** The illusion of 3D depth on a 2D plane.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Shapes that seem to move or vibrate when you look at them.
*   **Animations:** Hypnotic scrolling effects.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS repeating radial gradients.

## 3. Suprematism / Absolute Geometric Abstraction
**Psychological Intent:** The supremacy of pure feeling (Kazimir Malevich).
*   **Colors:** Pure primary colors and black.
*   **Typography:** None.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Floating, weightless.
*   **Negative Space:** Massive white backgrounds.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Floating squares, crosses, and circles. Total rejection of drawing real objects.
*   **Animations:** Static perfection.
*   **Tech Stack:** Basic CSS geometry.

## 4. Fauvism
**Psychological Intent:** "Wild Beasts". Pure emotional expression through color.
*   **Colors:** Extreme, unnatural, highly saturated colors (e.g., blue trees, red skies).
*   **Typography:** Expressive brush scripts.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Flat, un-modeled space.
*   **Negative Space:** Painted aggressively.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Painterly landscapes with jarring color clashes.
*   **Animations:** None.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG turbulence filters.

## 5. Deconstructivism
**Psychological Intent:** Anxiety, tension, breaking the rules.
*   **Colors:** Grayscale, stark red.
*   **Typography:** Purposefully broken apart, sliced in half, layered haphazardly.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Shattered, fragmented layouts.
*   **Negative Space:** Awkward angles.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Torn images, misaligned slices.
*   **Animations:** Glitch hover effects.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS `clip-path` slicing.

## 6. Bardcore / Medieval Revival
**Psychological Intent:** Irony, plague-era humor.
*   **Colors:** Parchment, faded tapestry dyes.
*   **Typography:** Old English / Blackletter.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Flat perspective, tapestry layouts.
*   **Negative Space:** Illuminated manuscript borders.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Modern themes painted as medieval tapestries (e.g., knights fighting snails).
*   **Animations:** Scrolling tapestry.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS parchment background patterns.

## 7. Tachisme / Art Informel
**Psychological Intent:** Pure spontaneity, European abstract expressionism.
*   **Colors:** Raw pigments.
*   **Typography:** Messy handwriting.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Chaotic splatter.
*   **Negative Space:** Raw canvas.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Spontaneous brushwork, drips, blobs of paint straight from the tube.
*   **Animations:** Splattering on load.
*   **Tech Stack:** Canvas API random drips.

## 8. Sticker Bombing
**Psychological Intent:** Street culture, claiming space.
*   **Colors:** Overwhelming chaos of colors.
*   **Typography:** Graffiti tags.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Completely overlapping, zero grid.
*   **Negative Space:** Eradicated by stickers.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Every inch of a surface covered in vinyl stickers (laptops, street poles).
*   **Animations:** Sticking sounds/animations on click.
*   **Tech Stack:** Absolute positioning with random `rotate`.

## 9. Airbrush T-Shirt Art
**Psychological Intent:** 1990s mall kiosks, pure nostalgia.
*   **Colors:** Neon gradient sunsets (pink to yellow to blue).
*   **Typography:** Thick cursive names with highlights.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Centered on a black or white "shirt".
*   **Negative Space:** Smooth fading spray paint.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Palm trees, hearts, hyper-smooth gradients.
*   **Animations:** Static.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS `radial-gradient`.

## 10. Pin-Up Art (1940s/50s)
**Psychological Intent:** Flirtatious, idealized retro glamour.
*   **Colors:** Vibrant reds, soft pastels.
*   **Typography:** 1950s scripts.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Poster layouts.
*   **Negative Space:** Soft lighting studio backdrops.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Gil Elvgren style painted illustrations, soft lighting.
*   **Animations:** None.
*   **Tech Stack:** Vintage CSS filters.

## 11. Trompe L'oeil
**Psychological Intent:** "Deceive the eye".
*   **Colors:** Photorealistic palettes.
*   **Typography:** Painted to look carved into stone.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Architectural framing.
*   **Negative Space:** Imitating 3D depth on a flat wall.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** 2D paintings so incredibly realistic they appear to be 3D objects popping out of the wall.
*   **Animations:** Parallax trickery.
*   **Tech Stack:** Complex CSS shadowing.

## 12. Stencil Art (Banksy/Street)
**Psychological Intent:** Political messaging, rapid execution.
*   **Colors:** Monochrome black/white with a single red pop color.
*   **Typography:** Spray-painted stencils.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Brick wall framing.
*   **Negative Space:** The raw wall texture.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Images broken down into 2 or 3 solid color layers, separated by necessary "bridges".
*   **Animations:** Spray paint fading in.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG masks imitating stencils.

## 13. Jungle / Drum & Bass Flyers (90s)
**Psychological Intent:** Underground raves, bio-mechanical futures.
*   **Colors:** Dark greens, silvers, laser glows.
*   **Typography:** 3D rendered alien chrome text, practically unreadable.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Dense, overlapping layers.
*   **Negative Space:** Outer space or dark metallic corridors.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Futuristic bio-mech suits, abstract 3D shapes.
*   **Animations:** Strobe flashing.
*   **Tech Stack:** WebGL chrome shaders.

## 14. Neon Sign / Tube Lighting Art
**Psychological Intent:** Nightlife, retro diners, motels.
*   **Colors:** Highly saturated glowing colors (neon pink, blue, red).
*   **Typography:** Cursive tube lettering.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Brick wall backgrounds.
*   **Negative Space:** Dark alleys.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Glowing gas tubes with a physical glass tube center and buzzing lights.
*   **Animations:** Flickering, buzzing on load.
*   **Tech Stack:** Intense CSS `text-shadow` layers.

## 15. Line Art / Monoline
**Psychological Intent:** Simplicity, modern icon design.
*   **Colors:** Single color (usually black or white).
*   **Typography:** Clean sans-serifs matching the line weight.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Extremely clean, lots of whitespace.
*   **Negative Space:** The primary element.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Illustrations drawn entirely with a single, unvarying line weight.
*   **Animations:** SVG line drawing (`stroke-dashoffset`).
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG strokes.
