# 🚗 11. Automotive, Transport & Mobility UI

*This module covers high-speed mobility, luxury cars, and futuristic transport. The UI must feel physical, engineered, and extremely responsive.*

---

## 1. High-Performance Dashboard (The "Porsche" Aesthetic)
**Psychological Intent:** Precision engineering, adrenaline, and complete control.
*   **Colors:** Pitch black (`#000000`), stark white, and "Racing Red" or "Speed Yellow" for critical actions.
*   **Typography:** Technical, extended Sans-Serifs (Porsche Next, Microgramma). It must feel like an analog speedometer.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Dashboard grid. Wide, panoramic aspect ratios.
*   **Negative Space:** Dark voids surrounding glowing instrumentation.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** High-res 3D car models rotating 360 degrees. Glowing RPM gauges.
*   **Animations:** Needle-sweep animations on load. Instant response times.
*   **Tech Stack:** Three.js for 3D configurators, WebGL for glowing gauge shaders.

---

## 2. EV / Clean Mobility (The "Tesla" Aesthetic)
**Psychological Intent:** Silent speed, environmental purity, and software-defined hardware.
*   **Colors:** Clinical white, soft grays, and electric blue (`#00FFFF`).
*   **Typography:** Thin, minimalist Sans-Serifs.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Massive, empty layouts dominated by a single car image.
*   **Negative Space:** Vast and sterile.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Wireframe overlays on car bodies showing battery packs.
*   **Animations:** Smooth, silent transitions. No aggressive shaking or bouncing.
*   **Tech Stack:** React, fluid CSS transitions.

---

## 3. Decopunk / 1930s Streamline Moderne
**Psychological Intent:** Romanticized luxury travel, the Golden Age of airplanes and trains.
*   **Colors:** Polished chrome, brass, and deep burgundy leather.
*   **Typography:** Art Deco fonts (Peignot, Broadway).
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Symmetrical, aerodynamic curves.
*   **Negative Space:** Framed by parallel chrome speed-lines.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Zeppelins, steam locomotives, aerodynamic bullet shapes.
*   **Animations:** Gliding, smooth linear motion.
*   **Tech Stack:** CSS multiple borders to create chrome striping.

---

## 4. Dieselpunk
**Psychological Intent:** Gritty, oil-stained, heavy machinery. 1940s war-tech.
*   **Colors:** Rusted steel, oily black, military olive drab.
*   **Typography:** Stencil fonts, distressed industrial lettering.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Heavy, bolted-on panels.
*   **Negative Space:** Filled with exhaust smoke and grime.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** Rivets, exposed engines, pressure gauges.
*   **Animations:** Mechanical, clanking, shuddering.
*   **Tech Stack:** Complex SVG filters for rust and grime.

---

## 5. Transit / Wayfinding UI
**Psychological Intent:** Absolute clarity under stress. Subway maps and airport signage.
*   **Colors:** High-contrast wayfinding colors (Vignelli NYC Subway colors).
*   **Typography:** Helvetica. Period.
*   **Spacing & Grid:** Strict grid of colored lines and dots.
*   **Negative Space:** White space used to group stations logically.
*   **Assets & Graphics:** The iconic angled transit map lines (45 and 90 degrees only).
*   **Animations:** Flashing "You Are Here" dots.
*   **Tech Stack:** SVG mapping libraries.
