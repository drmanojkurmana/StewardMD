# ✍️ 04. Anti-AI Copywriting Laws

*This module eradicates the "AI Tone of Voice". AI assistants naturally write generic, verbose, and soulless marketing copy. You must strictly follow these rules to sound like a human copywriter.*

---

## I. THE VOCABULARY VIRUS (Words to Ban)

**1. The "Elevate" Virus**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Elevate your business to the next level."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Grow your business." Use simple, concrete verbs.

**2. The "Unleash" Cliché**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Unleash the power of our platform."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Use our platform to do X." Be literal.

**3. The "Seamless" Trap**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Experience seamless integration."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Connects in one click."

**4. The "Robust" Filler**
*   **The AI Tell:** "A robust and dynamic solution."
*   **The Human Solution:** "A fast, crash-free app." Describe what it actually does.

**5. The "Revolutionize" Exaggeration**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Revolutionize the digital landscape."
*   **The Human Solution:** State the exact impact. "Save 10 hours a week."

**6. "In today's fast-paced world"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Starting introductions with this phrase.
*   **The Human Solution:** Delete the introduction. Start directly with the problem.

**7. "Delve into"**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Let's delve into the features."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Here are the features." Or just show them.

**8. "Unlock your potential"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Meaningless motivational fluff.
*   **The Human Solution:** Promise a tangible result. "Increase revenue by 20%."

**9. "Cutting-edge" or "Next-gen"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Relying on buzzwords instead of specs.
*   **The Human Solution:** Name the technology used. "Built on Rust and WebGL."

**10. "Comprehensive"**
*   **The AI Tell:** "A comprehensive guide."
*   **The Human Solution:** "The complete guide" or "Everything you need to know."

## II. SYNTAX & PUNCTUATION ERRORS

**11. The Bracketed Text `[Text]`**
*   **The AI Tell:** Writing `[Click Here]`, `[Submit]`, or `[Your Brand]` as buttons or placeholders.
*   **The Human Solution:** Write the text normally without brackets. Brackets are for code, not copy.

**12. The Hyphen/Dash Overload**
*   **The AI Tell:** Using hyphens to connect random words (e.g., "user-centric-platform").
*   **The Human Solution:** Never use dashes unless literature/grammar strictly requires it.

**13. The "Whether you are..." Construction**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Whether you are a startup or an enterprise..."
*   **The Human Solution:** "From startups to enterprise." Keep it short.

**14. Title Case Abuse in Sentences**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We Provide The Best Services For You."
*   **The Human Solution:** Use standard sentence case. "We provide the best services."

**15. Monotonous Sentence Length**
*   **The AI Tell:** Every sentence is 12 words long. It sounds robotic.
*   **The Human Solution:** Vary lengths. One long sentence. Then a short one. This creates rhythm.

**16. Exclamation Point Fatigue**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Sign up today! It's free! You'll love it!"
*   **The Human Solution:** One exclamation point per page. Let the words carry the energy.

**17. The "Not only... but also" Loop**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Not only is it fast, but it is also secure."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Fast and secure." Be concise.

**18. Overuse of Commas**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Our product, which is new, helps you, the user, to win."
*   **The Human Solution:** Remove commas by writing direct sentences.

**19. Passive Voice Default**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Your data is protected by our system."
*   **The Human Solution:** Use active voice. "Our system protects your data."

**20. The "Imagine if..." Intro**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Imagine if you could save time..."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Save time." Don't ask them to imagine.

## III. TONE, POSITIONING & ETHICS

**21. Legal & Competitor Defamation (CRITICAL)**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We are better than Competitor X." "Competitor Y is terrible."
*   **The Human Solution:** NEVER defame or attack a competitor directly (legal risk). You can mention them for tech parity ("Same encryption as Stripe") but never to insult. Focus on your own strengths.

**22. The Neutral Trap**
*   **The AI Tell:** Trying to sound polite and pleasing to absolutely everyone.
*   **The Human Solution:** Have an opinion. A luxury brand speaks less. A brutalist brand speaks aggressively. A fintech brand speaks with authority. Adapt the directness strictly to the Brand Identity defined in Step 3.

**23. Fake Excitement**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We are absolutely thrilled to announce..."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Introducing [Feature]." Confidence is quiet.

**24. The Apologetic Tone**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We're sorry for the wait, but..."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Thanks for your patience." Frame positively.

**25. Over-explaining the Obvious**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Click this button to go to the next page where you can register."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Register."

**26. The "We" vs "You" Ratio**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We built this tool. We want to help. Our system is great."
*   **The Human Solution:** Flip to "You". "Save your time. Grow your audience. Your data is safe."

**27. Forced Empathy**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We know how hard it is to manage emails. We feel your pain."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Managing emails takes too long. Fix it."

**28. Artificial Urgency**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Hurry! Offer ends immediately!"
*   **The Human Solution:** State facts. "Offer ends Friday."

**29. Vague Promises**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Experience incredible results."
*   **The Human Solution:** Define the result. "Load pages in 0.4 seconds."

**30. The "Solution" Crutch**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We are an HR solution."
*   **The Human Solution:** "We are HR software." Call things what they are.

## IV. FORMATTING & STRUCTURE

**31. Emoji Abuse**
*   **The AI Tell:** 🚀 Starting 💡 every 📈 bullet point 🎯 with an emoji.
*   **The Human Solution:** Use emojis strictly if the Brand DA calls for it. Otherwise, use clean spacing and real CSS icons.

**32. The Robotic Bullet Point**
*   **The AI Tell:** Five bullet points that all start with the same verb and are the same length.
*   **The Human Solution:** Make bullet points scannable. Use bolding for the first two words.

**33. The "In Conclusion"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Ending a section with "In conclusion, our product..."
*   **The Human Solution:** Websites aren't essays. Just end the section.

**34. Bolding Random Words**
*   **The AI Tell:** "We provide **the best** service in **the entire** industry."
*   **The Human Solution:** Bold only key metrics or actionable takeaways.

**35. Wall of Text**
*   **The AI Tell:** Paragraphs with 6+ lines.
*   **The Human Solution:** Keep paragraphs to 2-3 lines max. Web users scan, they don't read.

**36. Orphan Words**
*   **The AI Tell:** Leaving one word alone on the last line of a paragraph.
*   **The Human Solution:** Use non-breaking spaces `&nbsp;` to balance typography.

**37. The Fake Testimonial**
*   **The AI Tell:** "I love this product!" - John Doe, CEO of Business.
*   **The Human Solution:** If generating placeholder testimonials, use realistic details: "Migrating from Vercel to Block95 saved us $4k/mo." - Sarah Jenkins, DevOps.

**38. Redundant Subtitles**
*   **The AI Tell:** H2: "Features". Subtitle: "Here are our features."
*   **The Human Solution:** Let the H2 stand alone or use the subtitle to add new information.

**39. Overuse of "Top" and "Best"**
*   **The AI Tell:** "The best platform for the top creators."
*   **The Human Solution:** "The platform for creators."

**40. Using "Click Here"**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Click here to read more."
*   **The Human Solution:** Make the button text the action. "Read the Report."

## V. UX WRITING & MICRO-COPY

**41. Error Messages (Robotic)**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Error 404: Page not found."
*   **The Human Solution:** "We can't find that page. Let's get you back home."

**42. Loading States**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Loading..."
*   **The Human Solution:** Give context. "Encrypting your data..."

**43. Empty States**
*   **The AI Tell:** "No data available."
*   **The Human Solution:** "It's quiet here. Create your first project."

**44. Success Messages**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Form submitted successfully."
*   **The Human Solution:** "You're in. Check your email."

**45. Placeholders in Inputs**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Enter your email address."
*   **The Human Solution:** Example data. "e.g., hello@netxeo.com"

**46. Tooltips**
*   **The AI Tell:** Repeating the label text in the tooltip.
*   **The Human Solution:** Provide *why* or *how*, not *what*.

**47. Unsubscribe Links**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Click here if you no longer wish to receive communications."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Unsubscribe."

**48. Pricing Tiers**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Basic / Standard / Premium"
*   **The Human Solution:** Name them by the target audience (e.g., "Hobby / Startup / Enterprise").

**49. Checkbox Copy**
*   **The AI Tell:** "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions."
*   **The Human Solution:** Keep it standard but ensure it's visually aligned. No generic robotic text.

**50. The Final CTA**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Ready to get started? Sign up now!"
*   **The Human Solution:** One clear command. "Start building."

**56. "Treat yourself"**

**58. "Hurry, while stocks last!"**

## IX. TRUST & SECURITY OVERCOMPENSATIONS

**81. "100% Secure and Safe"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Sounds like a scam site.
*   **The Human Solution:** "Secured with AES-256."

**82. "Your privacy is our top priority"**
*   **The AI Tell:** The standard PR apology intro.
*   **The Human Solution:** "We never sell your data. Read our strict privacy policy."

**83. "Trusted by millions"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Hallucinated metrics.
*   **The Human Solution:** Only use if verifiably true. Otherwise: "Used by 1,000+ teams."

**84. "Military-grade encryption"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Overused marketing buzzword.
*   **The Human Solution:** State the exact encryption protocol used.

**85. "We take your security seriously"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Platitude.
*   **The Human Solution:** "End-to-end encrypted servers in Switzerland."

**86. "Industry-leading standards"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Vague.
*   **The Human Solution:** "SOC2 and HIPAA Compliant."

**87. "Guaranteed results"**

**88. "No hidden fees"**

**89. "Risk-free trial"**

**90. "Unbeatable prices"**

## X. AI-SPECIFIC SYNTACTIC TICS

**91. The "Let's dive in"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Never start a section with this.
*   **The Human Solution:** Just start the section directly.

**92. The "Furthermore" / "Moreover"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Websites aren't academic essays.
*   **The Human Solution:** Remove the transition words. Use short, punchy sentences.

**93. The "As a reminder"**
*   **The AI Tell:** If they need a reminder, the UX/flow is bad.
*   **The Human Solution:** Redesign the page so the context is obvious.

**94. The "It's important to note that"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Fluff intro.
*   **The Human Solution:** Just state the important fact directly.

**95. The "At its core"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Banned filler.
*   **The Human Solution:** "Fundamentally," or just delete it entirely.

**96. The "Navigating the complexities of"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Over-dramatizing a simple problem.
*   **The Human Solution:** "Managing X is hard. We make it easy."

**97. The "A testament to"**
*   **The AI Tell:** "This is a testament to our quality."
*   **The Human Solution:** "Proof of quality."

**98. The "Bespoke"**
*   **The AI Tell:** Calling an API integration "bespoke".
*   **The Human Solution:** Only use if genuinely custom-tailored luxury (like a suit). Use "Custom" instead.

**99. The "Unprecedented"**
*   **The AI Tell:** "Unprecedented growth." Rarely true.
*   **The Human Solution:** "Record growth."

**100. The "In the realm of"**
*   **The AI Tell:** "In the realm of web design..."
*   **The Human Solution:** "In web design..."
