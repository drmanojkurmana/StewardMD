# PrepReels pilot protocol (pre-registered, protocol only)

Status: protocol only. No results are claimed. Recruiting, consent and analysis need human owners.

## Design

- 30 adult learners, 4 weeks, across NEET-PG, INI-CET, NEET-SS, USMLE and FMGE profiles.
- Matched topics: each learner studies matched topic pairs, one through normal PrepNucleus practice and one through PrepReels Study Feed, with baseline ability recorded first. Topic order counterbalanced.
- Cadence arms: 2:1, 3:1 and 5:1 (MCQs to one micro-lesson or video plus a retrieval check), assigned before the pilot starts. Default arm is 3:1.

## Outcomes (pre-registered before the pilot starts)

- Primary: 7-day delayed recall on concepts met in PrepReels versus the matched normal-practice topics.
- Secondary: 30-day delayed recall, matched mock-test performance change, correct answers per minute of active retrieval, proportion of video exposures followed by a correct retrieval check, mission completion with actual learning actions, saved content later revised.
- Guardrails: total session duration and unplanned overrun, passive video minutes without retrieval, disputed-content exposure, learner-reported distraction, pressure or guilt, battery and data use, accessibility failures.

## Analysis

Pre-register the primary outcome, the matching rule, exclusion rules and the analysis before any learner starts. Compare delayed recall on matched topics, adjusting for baseline ability. Do not claim causation from engagement analytics (views, swipes, dwell) alone. Expand only if learning outcomes improve and guardrails stay acceptable.

## Ethics

Finite sessions, visible progress, a clear stopping point and a Finish session action at all times. No autoplay chain, no guilt, shame, fake urgency or punitive reminders. Break timer is a user aid, never a lock. Notifications off by default for PrepReels engagement prompts. Measurement events are learning outcomes only, local and consent-aware; no listening history, no watch history, no social graph.

## OPEN

Learner recruitment, consent wording, Hindi/Telugu reviewer availability, and the analysis owner are human tasks. This file does not enrol anyone and reports no data.
