Four things you are holding at once.

None of them are hard on their own. The cost is holding all four in your head, at 2am, while somebody waits for an answer.

  1. 01

    The organism, and what it is likely to be

    The culture is not back. You are reasoning from syndrome, site and exposure.

  2. 02

    This patient's kidneys, right now

    Clearance changes the safe dose of a large share of what you might reach for. It also changes hour to hour.

  3. 03

    What is actually resistant in your hospital

    National guidance is written for a country. The organism in front of you is local.

  4. 04

    Which guidance your unit follows when those disagree

    They do disagree. Knowing which one wins is most of the decision, and it is the part nobody writes down.

Source hierarchy

Every line the app shows you knows where it came from.

  1. 01Harrison, 22e. The disease reference floor.
  2. 02ICMR and NCDC. National guidance.
  3. 03Society guidelines for the syndrome.
  4. 04Your hospital's own overlay and antibiogram.Governs

When they disagree, the local overlay wins, and the app says so on the line itself. That is the whole design: not one more opinion, but a stated order of authority you can audit.
