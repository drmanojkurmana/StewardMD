feat(P0.4): the safety inbox - what needs a person, across the ward

NOT A NEW SOURCE OF TRUTH AND NOT A DASHBOARD. Every item already existed somewhere: an
open critical-result loop in critical-results.js, an unsigned note in note-cosign.js, an
undecided medicine in med-reconciliation.js, an overdue reassessment in risk-assessment.js
and the rest of chart-completion.js's detectors, which are imported and reused rather than
reimplemented.

Why it had to exist: those facts were reachable ONE PATIENT AT A TIME, which is the wrong
shape for the question actually being asked. A doctor coming on shift does not ask "is
patient 7's note signed"; they ask "what needs me, now, anywhere on this ward". Answering
that by opening thirty charts is how the answer stops being looked for.

ROLE-TAILORED, NEVER ROLE-HIDDEN. Ordering is by the hospital's own `responsibleRole` on
the rule, not a judgement made here. But the filter is a convenience and never a
permission: what a person may READ is the record service's grant as everywhere else, and a
patient a reader may not read never enters this file. Filtering can only narrow what the
reader was already entitled to see. An item nobody owns is shown to EVERYBODY rather than
to nobody - an unowned safety item appearing in no inbox is the exact failure this removes.

THE MOST IMPORTANT BEHAVIOUR IS ABOUT INCOMPLETENESS. A safety inbox that quietly dropped
the patients it could not read, or stopped at its scan cap without saying so, reads as a
quiet ward - worse than having no inbox at all. So:
  - patients whose charts could not be read are NAMED, never skipped silently
  - a ward past the cap says "this list is incomplete - do not read it as a quiet ward"
  - the warning renders ABOVE the list, asserted by a test on the ordering
  - an empty-but-incomplete list says "the check was incomplete" and is forbidden by test
    from saying "nothing outstanding"
  - a failed LOAD sets the same sentence rather than leaving an empty screen
  - every role sees the WARD total alongside their own, so a quiet inbox is never mistaken
    for a quiet ward

Critical results are listed independently of the hospital's chart-completion config: a
hospital with an empty config still has critical results, and that must not depend on
somebody having configured a rule.

It leads to the action. Acknowledging a critical result is done from the row, and now
reloads whichever list the person is actually looking at - doing it from the inbox used to
reload the criticals board and leave the acted-on row sitting there.

Tests: 25 - 15 on the module (role ownership, ordering, the cap, named failures, an
unowned item reaching everybody) and 10 rendering the screen, including that an incomplete
check can never render as all-clear.
