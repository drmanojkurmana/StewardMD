feat: save a whole consultation in one go, and tell the truth when it half-fails

Adapted from Bahmni's BahmniEncounterTransaction, which has had this right for years: a
consultation is one act of care, so it should be one save.

The old way was five buttons, five separate saves, all reporting into the same single
error line on screen - so whichever reply landed last won. A refused prescription could
be painted over by a note that saved fine, and the doctor would walk away believing the
drug was ordered. This session's bug hunt kept finding that same shape.

_wardsynq/consultation.js takes the vitals, the diagnosis, the prescription, the test
and the note together. It holds no authority of its own: every piece goes through the
exact writer its own route has always called, so the same validation, governance, audit
and safety engine apply, and the formulary and advisories stay org content rather than
becoming caller-supplied just because the call arrived bundled.

Half a consultation is the thing to fear, and there is no transaction across records to
prevent it, so the common failure is caught before it can happen: the actor is resolved
ONCE up front and every requested piece is checked against their grant, and if any one
piece is outside it the whole save is refused with nothing written. A nurse who may
record vitals but not prescribe is told that before her vitals are saved, not after.

What can still fail mid-way is a write that passes permission and then fails on its own
merits. That is reported, never hidden: the reply names what reached the chart, where it
stopped, and what was never attempted, and the screen renders that literally instead of
collapsing it to "saved" or "failed".

Also: each piece gets its own derived idempotency key. Sharing the screen's single key
across five writes would have made the second piece look like a repeat of the first and
be silently dropped - which is how a resend quietly loses a prescription.

The new Consultation screen on the chart is what makes this reachable; the reachability
checker added in the previous commit caught the route as orphaned within a second of it
being written, which is precisely what it is for. The five individual buttons all still
work - a nurse recording observations should not have to open a consultation.

Tests: 10 new, covering chart order, the all-or-nothing permission refusal, the partial
save report, a writer that throws, and per-piece indexing. Suite 5743 passing, 0 failing.
