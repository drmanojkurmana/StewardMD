feat(P0.2): search the history, and open the record behind any line

Two of the three gaps left on the timeline. "When did we start the co-amoxiclav" is asked
far more often than any filter answers, and on a three-week stay it is unanswerable by
scrolling. Search looks at what the reader can actually SEE - the line, who did it, and
the words behind it - rather than the raw record, because a search that matched hidden
fields would return rows whose match nobody can find.

The drill-down is the bigger one. The timeline is a projection: it says "dr.mehta
prescribed Co-amoxiclav 1.2g IV TDS", which is what a reader wants ninety-nine times in a
hundred. The hundredth time somebody needs to know exactly what was written, by whom, when
it was written as against when it took effect, and whether it was later corrected - and
there was nowhere to go and look.

_wardsynq/record-detail.js adds no authority and no second copy. It reads the same
governed record through the same actor's own read scope and returns its versions; a reader
outside a type's read scope is refused here exactly as anywhere else.

WHAT WAS SUPERSEDED IS SHOWN, NOT HIDDEN. "This was prescribed and corrected an hour
later" and "this stood all week" are different facts, and a chart showing only the latest
version makes them look identical. wardsynq-temporal.js already works out which versions
were ever the live belief and which were overtaken before taking effect; that verdict is
now on screen, along with which fields moved between versions.

Two bugs found while building:
  - historyOf returns each version spread FLAT with its verdict flags alongside, not
    wrapped. Reading it as a wrapper silently yielded the version NUMBER where the record
    should be, so every field came back undefined. Caught by the first test written.
  - `isCurrentBelief` answers a narrower question than its name suggests - "was this the
    live belief at its OWN effective time" - and is true of several versions at once, so
    it marked every version current. The version the store actually returns is the latest
    recorded one; that is what is marked now, and it matches the record in the response.

Also: `changed` skips meta, version and writtenBy, which differ on every version by
construction, and historyOf's own three verdict flags. Listing them would drown the one
field that actually moved.

Tests: 15 new - 8 on the version logic, 7 rendering the search and the panel, including a
version overtaken before it took effect and a record the reader may not open. Suite 5861
passing, 0 failing.

Still open on P0.2: unread/unacknowledged state, and referrals and documents, which cannot
appear on a timeline until they exist.
