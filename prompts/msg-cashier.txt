The cashier's own screen refused itself before she ever opened it

open() called loadWard() unconditionally, even when the shell asked for one
specific hospital-wide board. The ward roster read needs the same record scope
as the ward list, and plenty of roles - cashier, pharmacy, the front desk - do
not hold it. So a cashier opening "Billing and cashier" watched the ward roster
fail first ("cash.01@demo.wardsynq.test may not read Encounter"), which set the
one shared error banner every view reads from, and then landed on her own
working cashier screen with somebody else's refusal still printed across the
top of it. Her screen was fine; the request she never made was not.

Every hospital act already loads itself (loadBoard, loadEd, cashierOpen,
loadReports, and the rest), so the roster read is only needed when no act is
given - opening the plain ward list. loadWard() now runs only in that case.

Found by looking up a patient as the cashier and watching the refusal from a
request she never made appear over her own working screen.

ALSO: the cashier's patient card printed the raw record id
("opd-pat-smd-6teqzm-00025") under the patient's name. A cashier checks a
person against the number on their bill or their card, and that id is neither -
it is the system's own internal key. It now shows the MRN she just typed to
find them, which costs nothing extra since she already has it.
