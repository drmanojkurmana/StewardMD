The laboratory can open the laboratory

The bench had a board and could not use it. lab holds queue.view and lab.result;
the board's reads were gated emr.view alone, which the role deliberately does not
have because resulting a potassium needs the order and not the consultation
notes. So the one department screen the laboratory exists to work from was the
one screen it could not open.

THREE NARROW CHANGES, each matching a pattern already in the codebase:

1. lab.result is now an alternative authority for collections and pending-tests,
   exactly as order.verify already is for criticals and lab.result already is for
   specimen-outcome. It grants the narrow record scope in actor.js and nothing
   more, so it opens no other route.

2. The laboratory's record scope gains READ on Patient. Without it every row of
   its own worklist named a specimen by record id, the one identifier that cannot
   be checked against a tube, a form or a wristband, which is the entire purpose
   of the specimen subsystem. The imaging worklist settled the identical question
   already, on the stated grounds that a worklist with no identity on it is worse
   than no worklist.

3. A tile may name more than one way in, because some screens honestly have two.
   The laboratory board is opened by a ward with emr.view and by the bench with
   lab.result, the same pair the server now accepts, and a single capability
   would have had to lock out one of the two departments.

THIS MOVES A PINNED BOUNDARY, so it is recorded and not absorbed. The role test
asserted "and never reads the chart" over Patient and ClinicalNote together. That
assertion is now split: Patient is readable, and the test asserts explicitly that
ClinicalNote, Encounter, Condition and MedicationOrder are not, and that the lab
never WRITES an identity. A laboratory still cannot read the chart. emr.view was
tried on the role first and reverted the same day because it opened the discharge
summary and the ward's critical-results list.
