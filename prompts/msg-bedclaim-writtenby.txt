Every bed-claim row in the audit log had a blank WHEN, forever

Following the table-wrapping fix, the audit log's Record changes list was
readable enough to notice a pattern: every _wardsynq_bed_claim row showed no
WHEN at all, while every Encounter row next to it did. Not one blank cell -
every single one, on every bed claim ever written.

The store stamps writtenBy (id, kind, tier, at) on every record it writes,
but only for writes that go through its own put()/putMany() - the governed
path that also runs authoriseWrite. claimBed() (the self-healing reconciler
that keeps a bed's claim in sync with whichever Encounter currently sits in
it) calls svc.repository.append() directly instead, correctly skipping
authoriseWrite - a bed claim isn't a clinical entity a human or AI is
writing, it's this file's own bookkeeping - but that also skipped the stamp
that comes with it. The audit trail was accurately reporting a fact this
file had genuinely never recorded, not misreading one that existed.

claimBed() now stamps its own writtenBy, using the same shape every other
record carries: kind: KIND.SERVICE ("internal machinery such as the
escalation monitor" - wardsynq-actors.js's own words for exactly this kind
of writer), so the audit log can finally tell a bed claim apart from a
person's edit instead of showing neither a time nor an author for either.

Found by reading the audit log as supervisor, right after fixing how it
rendered.
