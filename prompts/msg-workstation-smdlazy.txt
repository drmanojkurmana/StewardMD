The Order safety workstation broke itself the instant a drug was typed

Signed in as doctor and opened Order safety workstation. It looked fine at first - rules loaded,
drug field enabled - but typing anything into the drug field threw "smdLazy is not defined" and
the whole screen locked: the drug field went permanently disabled, the banner said "ordering is
disabled", and the patient roster showed only "Unavailable." check() (which runs on every
keystroke in the order form, and once synchronously during boot() as soon as the first patient is
selected) called smdLazy('/interaction-rules.js?v=gold363') - StewardMD's own native app has a
function by that name for lazily loading its interaction-rules bundle, but this file is not that
app and never defines or loads it. Every call was a guaranteed ReferenceError. Called during
boot()'s own patient-selection step, that throw propagated all the way out and aborted boot()
itself, which is why the roster never rendered at all - not a load failure, a crash one keystroke
in that looked identical to one.

There was nothing left to lazy-load: the rule pack is already built and handed to the engine
synchronously at the top of boot() (buildRulePack + `new SafetyEngine(...)`), before check() can
ever run. Removed the smdLazy wrapper and evaluate directly - the precondition it was gating had
already been met every single time.

Verified live end to end as doctor: typed a drug and dose, findings rendered, sign stayed
correctly enabled/disabled by the engine's own verdict, roster and patient identity rendered
normally. No test in this repo exercised this file's check() (test/run-interactions.mjs, which
does reference smdLazy, is StewardMD's native app's OWN interaction test - a different app,
already defensive about smdLazy possibly being absent - so this defect had no automated coverage
at all until this walkthrough found it live).
