A view that does not look right? The C-arm angles and whether you were looking at the left or right coronary artery are the most useful things to note.

Using AngioLAB

  • Drag on the angiogram, or move the two sliders under Controls, to turn the C-arm.
  • Tap a standard view, such as Spider or LAO cranial, to go straight to it.
  • Left, Right and Both choose which coronary artery is injected.
  • Labels and Color name the segments. Tap a vessel to pick it out.
  • The angle pad is a map of every angle; tap it to send the C-arm there.
  • Tap "For educational purposes" for credits and sources.

What it is

AngioLAB is an educational simulation of one generic, normal coronary tree. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or any clinical decision.
