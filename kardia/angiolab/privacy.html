AngioLAB

Privacy

Effective October 5, 2026

AngioLAB collects no personal information.

The app

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and it does not connect to the internet: the anatomy and everything else it needs are inside the app. Nothing you do in it leaves your device.

The app remembers a few display choices on your device, such as whether a panel is folded. Deleting the app removes them.

The website

The website sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics. It remembers the same display choices in your browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time.

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers receive your IP address and browser type when they send you the page, and may keep them briefly in their logs to operate and protect the service. I do not use those logs to identify visitors.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Because AngioLAB collects no personal information, there is none to share, sell, keep or delete, and no consent to withdraw.

Children

AngioLAB is an educational tool for clinicians and students. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
