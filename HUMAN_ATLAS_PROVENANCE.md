# Human Atlas / BodyParts3D provenance for the RadioAnatome 3D layer

This file is the provenance manifest for every third-party asset behind the RadioAnatome
"3D Anatomy" layer (`atlas3d.js`, `atlas/3d/`). It exists so that the upstream copyright,
attribution, source links, licence notices and adaptation notices are preserved exactly, and
so that third-party assets stay clearly separated from StewardMD's own code and from the
other RadioAnatome sources (Visible Human, TotalSegmentator dataset, OpenNeuro), which are
recorded in `atlas-pipeline/sources.json`.

Machine-readable copy of the checksums, rejects and modifications: `atlas/3d/provenance.json`
(regenerated by `atlas-pipeline/bp3d_import.py --write`).

### Required attribution (verbatim, as mandated by the licence page)

> BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International

The licence page states that this exact wording must be used when distributing part or
whole of the database or any adapted material. It is rendered in the app on the 3D Anatomy
"About" screen (`atlas3d.js` `infoHtml()`), together with the licence URL, the dataset name and
a statement that changes were made. It must not be paraphrased or removed.

Licence verification note: the OBJ files inside the upstream archive carry an older
"CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan" comment. The current official licence page (linked above) supersedes that
legacy text and licenses the database under CC BY 4.0. Human Atlas's `ATTRIBUTION.md` records
the same finding. The current page was fetched and read directly during this integration.

## 3. Modifications made by StewardMD (adaptation notice)

Applied by `atlas-pipeline/bp3d_import.py` on top of the Human Atlas packaging:

1. **Repacked** the 15 upstream chunks into 27 per-system chunks (`atlas/3d/<system>-N.bin.gz`)
   so the mobile viewer streams only the systems that are visible. Vertex data is merged per
   chunk with a uint16 part-index attribute; indices are rebased into chunk space. Geometry
   values are unchanged.
2. **Rejected 7 meshes** that are exact duplicates (same name, identical bounding box) of
   another mesh; the first copy is kept: FJ2011 (dup of FJ1928, superior mesenteric artery),
   FJ2013 (dup of FJ1846, celiac artery), FJ2386 (dup of FJ1916), FJ2394 (dup of FJ1924),
   FJ2553 (dup of FJ1465, tendinous arch of levator ani), FJ2769 (dup of FJ2440, cricoid
   cartilage), FJ3201 (dup of FJ2772, hyoid bone). 2,227 of 2,234 meshes ship.
3. **Corrected 11 display-system labels** in Human Atlas's curated system table that filed
   nervous-system parts under "cardiac" or "sensory" (third, fourth and both lateral
   ventricles, the interventricular foramen, choroid plexus, both flexor retinacula of the
   wrist, both lacrimal bones). The full list is in `atlas/3d/provenance.json`
   (`system_corrections`).
4. **Added** a body-region label per mesh (from FMA container-concept membership, falling back
   to height bands) and the mapping to RadioAnatome canonical ids (`atlas-pipeline/bp3d-map.json`).
5. **Dropped nothing else.** All 3,432 FMA concepts and their element lists are preserved
   (`manifest.concepts`), and every mesh keeps its BodyParts3D `FJ` id and FMA concept id.

Nothing from the upstream credits was modified or removed; this file adds to them.

## 6. Separation from other RadioAnatome sources

| Layer | Source | Licence | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT (head, thorax, abdomen, pelvis, knee, foot, hand, whole body) | Visible Human Project, U.S. National Library of Medicine | US Government work, NLM terms | `atlas-pipeline/sources.json`, each module's `atlas.json.provenance` |
| CT (living torso) | TotalSegmentator dataset (Wasserthal et al.) | CC BY 4.0 | same |
| MRI (brain) | OpenNeuro ds003563 | CC0 | same |
| 3D | BodyParts3D 4.0 via Human Atlas | CC BY 4.0 (data), MIT (packaging code) | this file, `atlas/3d/provenance.json` |

No BodyParts3D geometry is mixed into any CT/MRI module file, and no CT/MRI image is used by
the 3D layer. The only shared artefact is the canonical id vocabulary in
`atlas-pipeline/ontology.json`.
