Golden rule of StewardMD — every recommendation must answer:
  1. Why is this the diagnosis?
  2. Why are antibiotics needed or not needed?
  3. Why are these pathogens likely?
  4. Why were these antibiotics selected?
  5. What organisms do these antibiotics cover?
  6. What is NOT covered?
  7. When should therapy be narrowed?
  8. When should therapy be stopped?
If any answer is missing, no antibiotic recommendation should be made. This tool is a clinical reasoning aid built from guideline references — it does not replace clinical judgment, local antibiograms, or specialist input.

About StewardMD
StewardMD

A bedside clinical decision engine for antibiotic selection and antimicrobial stewardship, built by a practising clinician for clinicians.

What StewardMD does

StewardMD analyses clinical findings, patient context, risk factors, and vital signs to generate guideline-aligned antibiotic recommendations grounded in an 8-point stewardship framework. Every recommendation must answer why the diagnosis fits, why antibiotics are needed, which pathogens are likely, why these agents were chosen, what they cover, what they miss, and when to de-escalate or stop.

Who built it

StewardMD was created by Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana, MD, an internal medicine physician, PubMed-indexed researcher, JACC-published presenter, and Stanford-certified antimicrobial stewardship practitioner. The clinical content is built from IDSA, WHO, AHA, Surviving Sepsis, and Harrison's guidelines.

Medical Disclaimer
⚠️ For qualified medical professionals only
AI-summarised drug & medicine information

The drug, dosage, interaction, and monograph content in StewardMD's drug database is AI-summarised and structured from official sources (openFDA / FDA, EMA, SmPC, BNF, WHO). Doses are reproduced from the source labels but may contain transcription or summarisation errors. Always verify every dose, indication, contraindication, and interaction against the primary source before prescribing. Do not rely on it as the sole basis for any prescribing decision.

Educational purpose only

StewardMD is an educational clinical reasoning aid built from published guideline references for self-study and teaching discussion. It has not been clinically validated or peer-reviewed as a direct patient care tool.

Not a replacement for clinical judgment

StewardMD does not replace clinical judgment, local antibiograms, institutional antibiotic policies, or specialist input. Always verify any recommendation independently before applying it to any patient. If a result conflicts with your clinical assessment, trust your assessment.

Limitation of liability

Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana, MaiKnowledge, and any contributors to StewardMD accept no liability for clinical decisions made using this tool. Use of StewardMD constitutes acceptance of this disclaimer in full.

Intended users

StewardMD is intended for use by qualified medical professionals only — doctors, residents, and clinical pharmacists with the ability to independently evaluate antibiotic recommendations in context.

Guideline currency

Clinical guidelines evolve. Always cross-reference with the most current version of the relevant guideline before clinical application.

Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Effective 5 July 2026 · Operated by Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana

StewardMD is a clinical decision-support tool for qualified healthcare professionals — not a consumer app. This is a summary; please read the full Privacy Policy.

What we collect & why

Only what is needed to run the service: your account (name, email, photo) if you sign in with Google; saved cases you choose to save (in the cloud when signed in, on your device as a guest); optional AI content you submit; a notification identifier if you enable alerts; and app preferences stored only on your device. Necessary technical/security data is processed by our hosting provider to deliver and protect the service.

What we do NOT do

We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting trackers/SDKs; we do not sell or rent your data or build ad profiles; and we do not use your clinical inputs or patient data to train AI models.

AI features (optional, off by default)

"Explain" sends the content you submit to Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini) only to generate the response; it is not stored by us and not used to train models. "AI Vision" (app only) reads the photo entirely on your device — the image never leaves it — and only the recognised text, after obvious identifiers are automatically redacted, is sent to structure the values. You review every value.

Ward Sync (institutional)

Where offered (GITAM/GIMSR users), Ward Sync relays clinical data from your institution's system using your own institutional login. The institution is the data controller; StewardMD is a secure conduit that does not store that patient data or your institution password.

Providers, storage & retention

We use Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Vertex AI) and Cloudflare (hosting/delivery). Cloud data is stored in India (asia-south1, Mumbai). We keep your account and saved cases until you delete them; on-device data stays until you erase it; AI request content is not retained by us.

Your rights

Delete everything in-app (Account → Delete account & data). You may request access, correction, or erasure, withdraw consent (turn off AI/notifications), and — under India's DPDP Act, 2023 — nominate another person to exercise your rights. Grievance Officer: Dr. Manoj Kumar Kurmana; you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

Patient data — your responsibility

Any patient information you enter, you process as the treating clinician and data fiduciary. Please minimise identifiers and ensure you have the authority/consent required.
