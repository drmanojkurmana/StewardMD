WHO growth tables in this directory: who-growth-2006.json and who-growth-2007.json

Used by wardsynq/wardsynq-growth.js for growth z-scores and centiles.

who-growth-2006.json (0 to 5 years, by day; by 0.1 cm for weight-for-length/height)
  WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group. WHO Child Growth Standards: length/height-for-age,
  weight-for-age, weight-for-length, weight-for-height and body mass index-for-age: methods and
  development. Geneva: World Health Organization; 2006.
  https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards
  Head circumference-for-age: WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group. WHO Child Growth
  Standards: head circumference-for-age, arm circumference-for-age, triceps skinfold-for-age and
  subscapular skinfold-for-age: methods and development. Geneva: World Health Organization; 2007.
  Source files: https://github.com/WorldHealthOrganization/anthro, data-raw/growthstandards/
  weianthro.txt, lenanthro.txt, wflanthro.txt, wfhanthro.txt, bmianthro.txt, hcanthro.txt

who-growth-2007.json (5 to 19 years, by month; weight-for-age to 121 months only)
  de Onis M, Onyango AW, Borghi E, Siyam A, Nishida C, Siekmann J. Development of a WHO growth
  reference for school-aged children and adolescents. Bull World Health Organ. 2007;85:660-667.
  https://www.who.int/tools/growth-reference-data-for-5to19-years
  Source files: https://github.com/WorldHealthOrganization/anthroplus, data-raw/growthstandards/
  wfawho2007.txt, hfawho2007.txt, bfawho2007.txt

Transformation: the tab-separated rows (sex, age or length, L, M, S) were converted to JSON rows
[x, L, M, S] per sex, values unchanged. Six published WHO values (WHO z-score and centile xlsx files)
are reproduced from these rows in test/wardsynq-growth.test.mjs.
