Please check the labels, module by module

Ten modules of NLM Visible Human imagery in the StewardMD viewer. Every pin has been checked by machine to sit inside the region it points at. What nobody has checked is whether that region is the structure it is named — that is the judgement being asked for here. Verdicts and notes save to the server as you go.

Not live to students. The atlas is merged but switched off in the app (smd_atlas) until this sheet is signed. Nothing here has reached a user.

What to look for

  • Is each label on the right structure? That is the whole question. The machine check only proves a pin is inside some region.
  • Sides are never claimed. Nothing says left or right anywhere — two anatomical landmarks disagreed on this cadaver, so no side is asserted. If you see a side named anywhere, that is a bug.
  • Levels are never claimed. Ribs and vertebrae are generic, not numbered.
  • Lungs are not lobed. This cadaver’s lungs measure −540 to −570 HU against −700 to −850 in a living aerated lung, so the fissures are not resolvable. “Lung” is deliberately whole-organ.
  • No liver, spleen or kidney in the abdomen — without contrast they share one 25–90 HU band and cannot be separated. Their absence is intentional.
  • The brain has images and no labels. Automatic segmentation was tried and failed; it is listed so you can say whether the images are worth keeping.
Reject anything you cannot personally vouch for. A missing label is a gap we can fill. A wrong label teaches wrong anatomy, which is the one failure this must not have.
