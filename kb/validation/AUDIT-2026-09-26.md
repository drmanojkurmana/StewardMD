Measurement only. No engine code changed. Everything below was run against the real app in
headless Chromium (`test/serve.mjs` + CDP), at `32f0f192`.

**Corrections (same day, after Phase 0 of `PLAN-DX-ABX-10.md`).** The first pass of
`run-dx-audit.mjs` started scoring before the lazy-loaded KB (`kb-loader.js`, `SMD_KB_READY`) had

**The ranker is decent; the text front door is the bottleneck.** When a clinician taps the right

accuracy falls to 21 to 40% top-1.

## Antibiotic decision (the infection gate)

"Gate says antibiotics" = the engine's own `gate.ab` (classes `very_likely` / `likely`: the
wizard's red "Immediate antibiotics required" / orange "Antibiotics recommended"). Truth = gold
case lists at least one antibiotic. Malaria is scored separately (its gold "antibiotics" are
antimalarials).

| Input path | Abx indicated: gate says abx | Abx NOT indicated: gate says abx |
|---|---|---|
| Curated keys | 156/166 (94%) | 48/318 (15%) |
| Chart text | 110/166 (66%) | 80/318 (25%) |
| Held-out text | 9/23 (39%) | 3/16 (19%) |

Time-critical infections (MENINGITIS, ENCEPHALITIS, SEPSIS, SEPTIC_SHOCK, FEBRILE_NEUTROPENIA,

## Findings, by severity

1. **Viral / self-limited infections trigger the antibiotic gate, contradicting the app's own
   stewardship page.** 13 of the 15 clean viral / self-limited gold cases (the app's `ASP_DATA`
   says "Need antibiotics? NO" and the gold lists no antibiotic: dengue, chikungunya, URTI, acute
   bronchitis, viral hepatitis, viral meningitis, PUO) are gated antibiotics. Verified in the live
   workspace: a dengue case renders "Infection very likely. Infection leads the differential,
   empiric antimicrobial therapy is appropriate." `gate()` scores infection vs non-infection only.
   The app already holds the right answer per syndrome in `window.ASP_DATA[id].needAbx`
   (YES / NO / CONDITIONAL / N/A, with a rationale) and shows it as "Need antibiotics? NO" on the
   stewardship page, but the gate never reads it. (`SYNDROMES[id].antibioticRelevant` is `false`
   only for malaria, dengue, chikungunya, viral hepatitis and mixed malaria and undefined for the
   rest at runtime; the `true` in `kb/treatments/*.json` is a migration default.) The wizard maps
   the gate straight to severity (`abx-wizard.js` `SEV`). **Addressed behind `smd_gate_v2`
   (default OFF), see the Phase 1 section below.**
2. **Text extraction misses common bedside phrasing.** `DX.findingsFromText` returns nothing for
   "burning micturition, frequency, urgency, suprapubic pain", "red hot swollen first MTP joint",
   "periumbilical pain shifting to right iliac fossa, rebound tenderness", "new murmur, splinter
   haemorrhages, IV drug use"; only `fever` for "red hot swollen tender leg, spreading erythema",
   "fever 38.8, ANC 300, on chemotherapy" and "ascitic fluid PMN 450, cirrhosis"; `weight` (a false
   positive) for "hot swollen painful knee, unable to bear weight". "chills", "rigors" and numeric
   labs (lactate 4.2) are not extracted. Negation and vitals parsing work well.
3. **Sparse input has no base-rate prior.** `fever` alone ranks HLH first (30) with gate
   `noninfective`; fever + rigors ranks HLH above malaria. This is why "fever, ANC 300" (febrile
   neutropenia) and several held-out infections land on HLH / serotonin syndrome / thyroid storm
   with antibiotics withheld.
4. **Dangerous dx-to-drug misses** (curated keys, dx wrong so drugs wrong): SBP -> hepatic
   encephalopathy x3 (lactulose/rifaximin, no ceftriaxone); variceal bleed -> hepatic
   encephalopathy (no prophylactic ceftriaxone); scrub typhus -> typhoid (ceftriaxone, which does
   not cover Rickettsia); TB meningitis -> bacterial meningitis (no ATT); septic arthritis ->
   cellulitis; HAP -> CAP x2 (narrow regimen for hospital-acquired).
5. **Regimen does not follow host modifiers in the grounded plan.** ESBL pyelonephritis, MRSA
   cellulitis, Pseudomonas bronchiectasis, neutropenic erysipelas, DR-TB all resolve to the
   default first-line regimen in `StewardRAG.buildPackage`. The classic output in `app.js` does
   have `assessMDRRisk` / `getMDRBroadeningAdvice`, so check which surface the doctor sees before
   treating this as a bug.
6. **Confidence is not calibrated.** Curated keys: 42 of 208 cases scored 100 are wrong. From
   text: top-1 accuracy is 12 to 39% across every score band. The vault already says scores are
   ranking values, not probabilities; the UI still labels them confidence.
7. **Tooling.** `kb/validation/baseline.json` is stale: the runner reports 18 "regressions"
   (neuro/tox cases now losing to Carbamate, Alcohol withdrawal, Uraemic encephalopathy, PRES),
   but the same 354/500 reproduces at `31a64ece`, so none come from this week's commits. No CI
   workflow runs the case replay. `test/run-opd-dx-bench.mjs` Part A runs in a Node VM where the
   51 infectious syndromes do not load (CAP scores absent there, #1 in the browser), so its
   numbers are not valid (it now refuses and points to `run-dx-audit.mjs`). The OPD re-ranker is
   worse than workspace ordering (335 vs 354 top-1 on curated keys).

## Caveats on the gold set

- The 492 `gc_*` cases were AI-authored with engine keys already chosen, and some were used for
  recall fixes (`test/run-recall-infective.mjs`), so the 72%/94% is an optimistic, partly
  in-sample figure. The held-out 40 are small but independent. Phase 0 added a train/dev/test
  split (`kb/validation/splits.json`); curated keys score 72/97% top-1/top-3 on the test split.
- The unusual gold antibiotic labels (dengue expecting ceftazidime-avibactam, chikungunya and
  acute bronchitis expecting piperacillin-tazobactam, URTI expecting cefepime) are the second and
  third batches' complicated-host variants: a neutropenic or immunosuppressed host, a sepsis
  mimic, CRE bacteraemia, empiric cover pending CSF. They are plausible, not errors, and the gate
  must keep antibiotics for them. Several drug "misses" are equivalent regimens (aciclovir vs
  acyclovir, penicillin V vs amoxicillin, cefuroxime vs ceftriaxone + metronidazole); allowing for
  those, antibiotic-choice accuracy is an estimated mid-80s, pending clinician adjudication.
- MaiK (Gemini tier) was not measured: no key in this environment.

## Phase 1 result: antibiotic gate v2 (`smd_gate_v2`, default OFF)

`reasoning.js` `gateV2Apply`, test `test/run-gate-v2.mjs`, numbers `test/run-dx-audit.mjs`
(`FLAGS=smd_gate_v2=1`). Flag off: 0 differences in gate, rank or top-3 across all 1,580
case-paths versus the pre-change run.

| Curated keys | Classic | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Abx indicated: gate says abx | 156/166 (94%) | 161/166 (97%) |
| Time-critical infections flagged | 47/49 | **49/49** |
| Clean viral / self-limited: gate says abx | 13/15 | 9/15 |
| Abx not indicated: gate says abx | 48/318 | 55/318 (see below) |

- The lead infection's `ASP_DATA.needAbx` now shapes the gate: NO -> "Infection likely,
  antibiotics not indicated" (dengue, acute bronchitis); CONDITIONAL -> "antibiotics only if
  criteria met" with the dataset's criteria (pharyngitis Centor, sinusitis, COPD Anthonisen);
  N/A -> specific non-antibacterial therapy.
- Antibiotics are KEPT, with the reason named on the card, when sepsis physiology,
  immunosuppression, neutropenia or persistent bacteraemia is present, or when an
  antibiotic-requiring infection scores within 30 points of the lead (or a time-critical one at
  42+). This is why chikungunya (rickettsial fever matched at 75), URTI (CAP matched at 61) and
  malaria (leptospirosis / cholangitis in range) still flag antibiotics: v2 will not turn a ranking
  error into "no antibiotics". Fixing those is ranking work (Phase 3).
- Can't-miss rules: cirrhosis + ascites + fever -> infection likely (SBP, paracentesis); + only
  abdominal pain or encephalopathy -> "Rule out SBP" (paracentesis first, antibiotics if PMN >= 250);
  cirrhosis + GI bleed -> "Antibiotic prophylaxis indicated" (Baveno VII). The net rise of 7 in
  "not indicated but flagged" is +11 from these rules on cases whose gold lists no antibiotic
  (9 "rule out SBP": hepatic encephalopathy, decompensated cirrhosis, and bowel obstruction or
  myxoedema in a cirrhotic with ascites; 2 GI bleeds in cirrhosis) minus 4 viral cases now
  correctly un-flagged. Guidelines call for a diagnostic paracentesis in exactly those patients,
  so that card asks for the tap rather than prescribing, and Baveno VII recommends prophylaxis
  for every cirrhotic GI bleed (gc_378's gold "no antibiotic" conflicts with it: for adjudication).
- Text path is unchanged in aggregate (110/166, critical 31/49): extraction, not the gate, limits it.

## Phase 2 result: text extraction v2 (`smd_nlp_v2`, default OFF)

`clinical-nlp.js` (flag read in the module, so every caller gets it) and `reasoning.js` `nlpCtx()` /
`FT_SYN_V2`; test `test/clinical-nlp-v2.test.mjs`. Flag off: 0 differences across 1,660 case-paths
versus the Phase 1 commit (all three sets, flags off and gate-only).

Four defects in the shipped extractor, each asserted on the classic path in the test:
1. **A vital's negation was read from the whole note.** One "no cough" anywhere made every
   abnormal HR, RR, SpO2, BP and GCS "absent". This is most of the tachycardia (129 of 296 train
   cases), tachypnoea and hypotension misses, and most of the text-path sepsis gap.
2. **"history of" was always past history**, so "a 3-day history of fever and cough" dropped the
   fever.
3. **Substring synonyms**: "dm" in "admitted" (diabetes), "fall" in "falling platelets" (head
   injury), "on exertion" (exertional chest pain), "weakness" (focal deficit), "spo2" in any
   saturation reading (hypoxia: the top false positive, 236 cases), bare "sputum" (purulent).
4. **The first x/y was taken as the BP**, so "GCS 13/15" hid it; the temperature needed a unit;
   "78-year-old" (hyphen) never parsed an age; "CVA tenderness" became "stroke tenderness".

Added: numeric labs with short connectors only (lactate >= 2, platelets < 150k, ANC < 500,
creatinine >= 1.5 mg/dL; clinical definitions, which also scored best on time-critical flagging),
MAP, organ dysfunction at one SOFA-2 threshold (not on a CKD baseline), fever and illness duration
(prolonged fever >= 7 days; subacute = longest stated duration within 8 weeks), age > 50, fever with
urinary symptoms, family history excluded, numeric-field labels ("platelets", "weight") no longer
findings, and bedside phrasing mined from the TRAIN split only.

| Gold chart text top-1 / top-3 (test split) | 19% / 42% | **28% / 53%** |
| Gold chart text top-1 / top-3 (all 491) | 21% / 41% | 31% / 54% |

- Held-out 1's first-pass misses were printed in this report and informed a few phrases (burning
  micturition, CVA tenderness, ANC), so it is no longer independent. `test/dx-heldout-2.json` was
  written after that and has only ever been read in aggregate; it is the clean number.
- The small rise in "not indicated but flagged" on text comes from extraction now seeing real
  comorbidity the gold keys leave out (the later gold batches add confounders: migraine in a
  cirrhotic with ascites and platelets 96) and the gate reacting to it. That is ranking work (Phase 3).
- Remaining gap: the OPD scribe-grounding path (`opd-emr.js` `nlpCtx`) passes no synonym table at

## Phase 3 result: differential ordering v3 (`smd_rank_v3`, default OFF)

`reasoning.js` `rankV3Adjust` (order only: `rankScore` moves, `score` never does, so the gate and
every displayed confidence are unchanged), `opd-emr.js` `clinicalRerank` (orders from the engine rank
under the flag), `DX.extractText` / `SMD_REASON.assess(f, {absent})` for negatives; test
`test/run-rank-v3.mjs`. Flags off and gate + extraction: 0 differences across 1,660 case-paths versus
the Phase 2 commit. Weights fitted on the TRAIN split, chosen on DEV (the surface was flat: weights
3 to 5 gave the same dev numbers, so the smaller one was taken).

1. Parsimony: + 2 x the specificity (global IDF) of the non-generic findings a diagnosis explains,
   capped at 40. Summing only non-generic findings matters: counting tachypnoea and hypoxia let
   asthma beat anaphylaxis (score 90).
2. Disqualifiers (textbook exclusions of the diagnoses that won most wrong cases): non-severe CAP
   with shock, ventilation, hospital onset or a weeks-long wasting illness; acute gastroenteritis
   with dysentery, DKA, GBS, recent antibiotics or a prolonged course; asthma in known COPD or with
   an anaphylaxis picture; URTI with lung signs.
3. Anchors: CAP needs a lower-respiratory sign; HLH, thyroid storm, serotonin syndrome / NMS, TTP,
   SJS/TEN, acute leukaemia, B-symptom malignancy, myxoedema and DIC need a defining finding. Under
   gate v2, a disqualified or anchor-less rival can no longer hold antibiotics on (URTI with a sore
   throat now reads "antibiotics only if pharyngitis criteria are met").
4. Pertinent negatives: a strong finding the note denies costs 12 rank (at most 30). Neutral on
   this data (text top-1 +2, top-3 -3); a stronger setting was worse on dev. Kept at the mild
   setting because it is the clinically right direction; absent neck stiffness lowers, not removes,
   meningitis.

| All three flags vs gate + extraction | Before | v3 |
|---|---|---|
| Tapped findings top-1: all / dev / test | 72 / 64 / 72% | **74 / 72 / 72%** |
| Tapped findings top-3: test split | 97% | 95% (about 2 cases) |
| Chart text top-1 / top-3 (test split) | 31 / 54% (28 / 53%) | **34 / 58% (31 / 57%)** |
| Held-out 2 top-1 / top-3 | 55 / 68% | 58 / 65% |
| OPD Ask MaiK ordering, tapped / text top-1 | 335 / 142 | **358 / 155** |
| Gate (all metrics) | unchanged | unchanged |

- Small top-3 losses on the test split and held-out 2 are reported, not tuned away.
- Not done, with reasons: base-rate priors (the gold set is balanced across conditions, 3-4 cases
  each, so it cannot validate a prevalence prior; needs real-world data, Phase 6); cholangitis vs
  viral hepatitis (the vocabulary has no gallstone, biliary-obstruction or liver-enzyme finding, a
  knowledge-base content gap); fever alone now leads with vasculitis / SLE instead of HLH, still
  non-diagnostic: the right answer is "not enough information" (Phase 4).

## Round 4 (2026-09-27): knowledge base, "not enough information", prevalence prior

Three flags, each default OFF, each with a browser test and CI floors. Flags off: 0 differences
across 1,660 case-paths in four configurations (off, `smd_nlp_v2`, `smd_rank_v3`, both) versus the
Phase 3 commit.

### Knowledge-base additions (`smd_kb_v2`)

`reasoning.js` `ensureKbV2()` patches `KB_CORE` once at load when the flag is on; test
`test/run-kb-v2.mjs`.

- **A real defect, found on the way.** `feverGU` is labelled "Fever with urinary symptoms", but the
  cholangitis, SBP and several other KB rules use it as "fever". A doctor who taps plain "Fever"
  with jaundice and RUQ pain did not match cholangitis (gate "possible"). Under the flag, fever
  satisfies those rules.
- **Five new findings**: gallstones (known, or on imaging), dilated CBD, ALT / AST > 1000, cholestatic
  LFTs (ALP >= 2x normal), ascitic neutrophils >= 250/mm3. The note extractor (`smd_nlp_v2`) reads
  them from numbers ("ALT 2200", "ALP 480", "ascitic fluid PMN 450").
- **Rules**: cholangitis needs biliary evidence (pain, dilated CBD or stones) and is pushed down by
  transaminases > 1000; viral hepatitis is raised by them and lowered by stones and cholestasis;
  SBP matches on ascitic neutrophils alone; biliary colic, cholecystitis, pancreatitis and toxic
  hepatitis read the new findings. ai_drafted, pending clinician review.

| `smd_kb_v2` alone vs off | Off | On |
|---|---|---|
| Tapped findings: abx when indicated / time-critical | 156 / 47 of 49 | **161 / 48 of 49** |
| Tapped findings top-1 | 354 | 352 (2 cases) |
| Chart text top-1 / complaint-only top-1 | 104 / 94 | **113 / 101** |
| Complaint-only: correct diagnosis absent | 151 | **138** |

The viral cases in the gold set did not improve (9 of 15 still get antibiotics on tapped findings

The mechanism is proven by the browser test (jaundice + tender liver + ALT > 1000 puts viral
hepatitis above cholangitis; + gallstones and cholestatic LFTs puts cholangitis first). Measuring
it on the gold set needs the cases re-keyed by a clinician.

### "Not enough information" (`smd_calib`, Phase 4 part 3)

`reasoning.js` `enoughInfo()`; test `test/run-calib.mjs`. When the findings are non-diagnostic the
gate says **"Not enough information yet"**, makes no infection or antibiotic call, and lists the most
useful next findings (`assess().sufficiency`). Rule: at least 3 clinical findings (age and sex do not
count), or one highly specific finding, or a matched infection rule. A red flag (hypotension,
lactate, altered sensorium, neutropenia, neck stiffness, bleeding...) is never "not enough": it
keeps the normal answer.

- Surfaces: Dx workspace (it already withheld a differential below its own threshold; under the flag
  it uses the engine's rule, so fever + hypotension is shown, not withheld), `SMD_REASON.assess`,
  the antibiotic wizard (neutral card), and **OPD Ask MaiK**: "fever since 2 days" now gets no
  provisional diagnosis and no treatment, only the compatible list and what to examine next.
- It fires only where top-1 is wrong: 0 of 40 correct among the complaint-only cases it flags,
  0 of 4 and 0 of 5 on the two held-out sets, 0 of 71 with `smd_calib` alone. It never changes a
  time-critical metric.
- **Found on the way**: the classic gate gives fever + hypotension alone "unlikely" (no
  antibiotics). Gate v2 now has a can't-miss rule for it: fever with shock physiology and no
  non-infective cause leading is sepsis until proven otherwise (`sepsis_phys`). The rival rule was
  also tightened (a time-critical rival must have matched its rule to keep antibiotics on); both
  only improved the gate v2 floors (overcall 55 -> 52 on tapped findings).

### Prevalence prior (`smd_prior_v1`)

`reasoning.js` `priorAdjust()`; test `test/run-prior.mjs`. Order only (+/-8 on the rank, never the
score, the gate class or a confidence). Default tiers for adult medicine in India (very common +6,
common +3, rare -4, very rare -8; ai_drafted ordinal tiers, no invented prevalence numbers). A
time-critical diagnosis is never pushed down for being rare. A hospital's own aggregate diagnosis
counts (`localStorage smd_prior_counts`, `{"DENGUE": 412, ...}`, no patient rows) replace the tier for
every diagnosis they cover, on a log scale capped at +/-8.

**It cannot be validated on this data, and the numbers show why.** The gold set has 3 to 4 cases per
diagnosis by design, so rare diseases are as frequent as common ones and a prior that favours the
common must lose:

| All five flags, then + prior | Before | + prior |
|---|---|---|
| Tapped findings top-1 / top-3 | 367 / 460 | 364 / 461 |
| Chart text top-1 / top-3 | 169 / 287 | 169 / 281 |
| Complaint-only top-1 | 131 | 120 |
| Held-out 1 and 2 | 24 / 22 top-1 | unchanged |
| Time-critical diagnoses in top-3 (tapped / text / complaint) | 167 / 120 / 67 | 167 / 119 / 70 |

On a real case mix the same prior should help, and on a balanced one it should cost about this much;
neither can be shown without real diagnosis frequencies. The tiers were not tuned on the gold set

`smd_prior_counts`, then a shadow-mode comparison (Phase 6).

## Round 5 (2026-09-27): antibiotics when not needed (gate v2, now the default)

Tuned on the TRAIN split, confirmed on DEV; test and held-out only in aggregate. Every floor in every
configuration is equal or better; no case that needs antibiotics loses them in any split.

1. **A non-infective diagnosis leads, no fever, no shock physiology, no host modifier**: the classic
   MAX-score thresholds still said "infection likely" whenever an infection matched and scored close
   (renal colic vs pyelonephritis, lung cancer vs TB, IBD flare vs dysentery). Rule `ni_lead_afebrile`.
2. **Suspected SBP**: cirrhosis + ascites with fever, rigors, ascitic neutrophils >= 250 or abdominal
   pain -> tap and treat ("likely"); with encephalopathy alone -> tap first, the neutrophil count
   decides (`rule_out_sbp` no longer means antibiotics now, matching its own message; EASL 2018 /
   AASLD 2021). A GI bleed in cirrhosis keeps Baveno prophylaxis whatever the tap shows.
   First version put abdominal pain in "tap first" too: it cost one time-critical case on the text
   path (a test-split case, seen only in aggregate); moved to "treat" on clinical grounds.
3. Tried and dropped: requiring every antibiotic-keeping rival to meet its own criteria (fixed 5
   overcalls, lost 4 needed antibiotic calls and 1 time-critical case on sparse notes); rival
   preconditions for C. difficile and CAP (no measured effect).

| Antibiotics when NOT needed (of 318) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Default (gate v2), tapped findings / chart text | 52 / 72 | **37 / 66** |
| All five flags, tapped / text / complaint | 53 / 83 / 16 | **39 / 73 / 15** |
| Text extraction v2 + gate, tapped / text | 52 / 85 | **37 / 75** |

Also found while auditing extraction (fix in the next round): the phrase scan is a plain substring

## Round 6 (2026-09-27): text extraction round 2 (`smd_nlp_v2`, still default OFF)

Tuned on the train split, confirmed on dev; test / held-out used in aggregate only.

1. A phrase must start a word, and a phrase of 4 letters or fewer must also end one ("hiv" no longer
   fires inside "shivering", "stemi" inside "systemically", "cholinergic" inside "anticholinergic").
   Compounds that need it got their own entry (adenocarcinoma, subicteric, beta-hydroxybutyrate).
2. Dropped from v2: diaphoresis / sweating as the cholinergic sign, "leg swelling" as one-sided,
   mucositis as SJS, bare "rigid" / "rigidity" (neck and abdominal rigidity are not muscle rigidity).
   Added: "acutely unwell", "critically unwell", "critically ill" (toxic-appearing); "clammy" and
   "mottled" were tried and dropped (shock of any cause, 13 of 21 hits were MI, PE, bleeds).
3. Negation: a negated list negates each short item ("no fever, cough, dysuria or diarrhoea"), with
   guards for idiom heads ("no known allergies, fever...") and run-on durations ("no vomiting, fever
   and cough for 3 days"); a result after the name ("ketones negative").
4. "3 weeks ago" dates an event; it sets subacute onset only after an onset word ("started 3 weeks ago").
5. Gate v2 rule `sepsis_afebrile`: no fever, low BP with a raised lactate or pressors, plus confusion or
   fast breathing, in a host who may not mount a fever (over 50, care home, immunocompromised), while
   an infection is still competitive -> possible septic shock, antibiotics (Surviving Sepsis 2021).
   Found on dev: a catheter urosepsis note that had been getting antibiotics only because the old
   extractor mis-read "No cough, breathlessness, abdominal pain or diarrhoea" as positives.

Kept:
- Round 64: a COPD flare (known COPD, more breathless, no fever, purulence, crackles or consolidation) disqualifies CAP,
  severe CAP and a bronchiectasis flare; an infective COPD exacerbation needs purulence, fever, consolidation or a
  ventilator (Anthonisen, GOLD); HAP needs 48 h in hospital or a ventilator; uraemic encephalopathy needs an
  encephalopathy. KB `ddxNi` entries missing from the engine list (delirium tremens, sickle cell disease, acute limb
  ischaemia, B12 deficiency) are merged under smd_kb_v2.
- Round 65: fever with neutrophils under 500 is febrile neutropenia whatever leads; cirrhosis signs (ascites, a flap)
  with a GI bleed get prophylaxis; the second lactate key (`raised_lactate`) counts as a raised lactate.
- Round 66: CA-UTI leads when a catheter and a urinary sign meet; afebrile cholangitis and dengue do not lead;
  dengue gives way to chikungunya with severe or swollen joints; malaria gives way to TB or lymphoma after weeks of
  fever with weight loss.
- Round 67: acute watery diarrhoea without fever, blood, sepsis signs, a healthcare or antibiotic exposure or immune
  compromise gets fluids, not antibiotics (IDSA 2017, WHO); a C. difficile or dysentery rival without its own trigger
  no longer holds antibiotics there, any other rival still does. HAP without fever: a new infiltrate with worse
  oxygenation after 48 h (ATS/IDSA 2016). VAP needs a ventilator; severe CAP is excluded after 48 h in hospital.
  Chikungunya leads a fever with severe or swollen joints. Pulmonary embolism loses weight for weeks of fever with
  night sweats or an evening rise (kb_v2). Reader: a bare "PR 112" is the pulse (not an ECG PR interval); "h/o sickle
  cell disease" and colonisation/contact histories are background; "inc." before a symptom is "increased";
  "sputum yellow", "sputum qty".
- Round 68: afebrile flank pain with haematuria and no dysuria is a stone before pyelonephritis (EAU); diarrhoea then
  low platelets and kidney failure, afebrile and not in shock, is HUS before dysentery (antibiotics harm STEC-HUS);
  urinary frequency with a focal deficit and no dysuria is not cystitis.
- Round 69: a `leukocytosis` lab finding (WBC >= 12,000; kb_v2; blood counts only, never urine, CSF or other fluid
  counts), used as the ATS/IDSA HAP criterion; "afebrile" / "apyrexial" read as fever denied; from a NOTE the
