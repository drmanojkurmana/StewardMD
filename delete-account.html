Delete your account & data

StewardMD · Operated by MAIKNOWLEDGE LLP (LLPIN ADA-6560) · Effective 16 August 2026
This page explains how to permanently delete your StewardMD account and the data associated with it. You do not need to be signed in or have the app installed to make a deletion request — see the email option below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open StewardMD and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account & data and confirm.

This permanently removes your account and your saved cases from both your device and our cloud. If you use the app as a guest (not signed in), use Erase all data on this device in Account to clear everything held locally.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@stewardmd.in from the address associated with your account (or include your registered email) and ask us to delete your account and data. We verify the request and action it within the timeframe required by applicable law.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (name, email, authentication identifier, profile)Permanently deleted
Saved cases (cloud & on-device)Permanently deleted
On-device data (preferences, guest cases, recent history, cached content)Removed from your device
Push subscription / device tokenDeleted (alerts stop)

What may be retained, and for how long

After deletion your data is erased from our live systems and removed from backups within a reasonable backup cycle. We may retain a limited amount of data only where and for as long as the law requires (for example, security or legal-compliance records), after which it is erased. AI request content and any verification credential are never stored by us — they are handled transiently to produce a result or a verification decision.

StewardMD does not store data pulled through Ward Sync (institutional/GHIS) — that patient data is relayed to your device and not written to our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on that account.

For full details on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. Questions: privacy@stewardmd.in.
