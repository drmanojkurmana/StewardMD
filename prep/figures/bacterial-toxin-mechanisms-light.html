What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • Toxin mechanisms shown. Diphtheria toxin ADP-ribosylates elongation factor 2, stopping protein synthesis, with pseudomembrane and myocarditis. Pertussis toxin ADP-ribosylates Gi, raising cAMP and driving lymphocytosis. Cholera toxin locks Gs on, raising cAMP and opening chloride channels so salt and water leave the gut. Shiga toxin cleaves 60S ribosomal RNA, injuring endothelium and causing haemolytic uraemic syndrome. C. difficile toxins glucosylate Rho GTPases, collapsing the cytoskeleton and causing colitis. Tetanus and botulinum toxins are proteases that cleave SNARE proteins and block vesicle release. This is standard teaching in microbiology and pathology texts and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
  • Treatment ideas shown. Antitoxin neutralises toxin that has not yet entered cells, antibiotics reduce the bacteria making toxin, supportive care protects the airway, heart, gut and kidneys meanwhile, and vaccination prevents disease for toxins with effective toxoid or related vaccines. They are teaching toggles only.

What was simplified. Toxin uptake routes in molecular detail, receptor names beyond the targets shown, subunit structures, dose and timing, toxin-specific complications beyond the headline effects, and which antibiotic or antitoxin suits which toxin are left out. Counts and speeds are illustrative, and the figure does not say how any patient should be treated.
