What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • Where each class acts. Benzodiazepines increase how often GABA-A channels open, barbiturates increase how long they stay open, and flumazenil is a benzodiazepine-site antagonist. This is standard teaching in the pharmacology texts the PrepNucleus lessons cite (Katzung; KD Tripathi) and were not checked against a linked source here, so they are flagged for review.

What was simplified. Z-drugs, alcohol, anaesthetics at GABA-A, direct channel opening by barbiturates at high levels, receptor subunits, tolerance and dependence, withdrawal, respiratory depression, flumazenil limits and adverse effects are left out. Neuron firing is drawn as a teaching score. Levels and the size of each effect are illustrative. The drug choices are teaching toggles of a class: no named drug, dose or schedule is shown or implied, and the figure does not say who should or should not take any of them.
