What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • How paracetamol eases pain and fever. Paracetamol is a central analgesic and antipyretic with weak peripheral cyclo-oxygenase effect. Teaching describes central cyclo-oxygenase inhibition, activation of serotonergic descending inhibitory pathways, and action through the cannabinoid system by its product AM404. Action in the hypothalamus lowers the raised temperature set point in fever. Peripheral anti-inflammatory effect is minimal, so swelling at an inflamed site changes little. This is standard textbook teaching and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
  • Overdose and rescue. A small fraction of paracetamol is converted to the reactive product NAPQI, normally detoxified by glutathione. In overdose glutathione is depleted, free NAPQI accumulates and injures hepatocytes. N-acetylcysteine restores glutathione and helps detoxify NAPQI. Activated charcoal in the gut, given early, reduces absorption. This is standard textbook teaching and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
