What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • Where each class acts. Nitrates raise nitric oxide and cGMP, PDE5 inhibitors slow cGMP breakdown, minoxidil-type drugs open potassium channels, and nitrates with PDE5 inhibitors together can cause a profound fall in pressure. This is standard teaching in the pharmacology texts the PrepNucleus lessons cite (Katzung; KD Tripathi) and were not checked against a linked source here, so they are flagged for review.

What was simplified. Hydralazine and diazoxide mechanisms, calcium channel blockers (own figure), beta-blockers and ranolazine in angina, tolerance to nitrates, reflex tachycardia, headache, pulmonary hypertension use, erectile dysfunction, adverse effects and contraindications are left out. Vessel tone is drawn as a teaching score. Levels and the size of each effect are illustrative. The drug choices are teaching toggles of a class: no named drug, dose or schedule is shown or implied, and the figure does not say who should or should not take any of them.
