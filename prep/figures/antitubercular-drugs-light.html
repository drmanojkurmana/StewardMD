What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • Where each class acts. Isoniazid blocks mycolic acid synthesis, ethambutol blocks arabinogalactan synthesis, rifampicin blocks RNA polymerase and pyrazinamide disturbs membrane energy in acid. This is standard teaching in the pharmacology texts the PrepNucleus lessons cite (Katzung; KD Tripathi) and were not checked against a linked source here, so they are flagged for review.

What was simplified. Streptomycin, second-line and newer drugs, resistance and how combinations stop it, intensive and continuation phases, doses and durations, adverse effects and monitoring (hepatitis, neuropathy, optic neuritis), drug interactions and latent infection are left out. Bacterial growth is drawn as a teaching score. Levels and the size of each effect are illustrative. The drug choices are teaching toggles of a class: no named drug, dose or schedule is shown or implied, and the figure does not say who should or should not take any of them.
