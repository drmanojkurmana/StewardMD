What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • Where each class acts. Aminoglycosides, tetracyclines, macrolides, lincosamides, chloramphenicol and linezolid each bind the bacterial 30S or 50S subunit and block initiation, tRNA binding, peptide bond formation or translocation. This is standard teaching in the pharmacology texts the PrepNucleus lessons cite (Katzung; KD Tripathi) and were not checked against a linked source here, so they are flagged for review.

What was simplified. Aminoglycoside uptake and oxygen dependence, concentration-dependent killing, post-antibiotic effect, the difference between bacteriostatic and bactericidal drugs, ribosome protection and methylation, efflux, tigecycline, streptogramins, aminoglycoside toxicity, and the differences within each class are left out. Levels and the size of each block are illustrative. The drug choices are teaching toggles of a class: no named drug, dose or schedule is shown or implied, and the figure does not say who should or should not take any of them.
