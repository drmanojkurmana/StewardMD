What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • How type 2 inflammation runs. Allergen picked up by dendritic cells wakes Th2 helper cells. Th2 cytokines split the work: IL-4 pushes B cells to switch to IgE and grows the Th2 response, IL-5 calls in eosinophils and keeps them alive, IL-13 drives mucus, twitchy airway muscle and skin barrier loss. IgE arms mast cells in airway and skin, and allergen cross linking fires them to release histamine and other mediators. This is standard teaching in allergy and dermatology texts and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
  • What the biologics do. Anti-IgE binds free IgE so mast cells are less well armed. Anti-IL-5 removes IL-5 so eosinophils fall. Anti-IL-4 receptor blocks the shared alpha chain for IL-4 and IL-13, calming mucus, barrier loss and IgE switching. Injection reactions can occur with each, rare anaphylaxis is a watch out with anti-IgE, and eye irritation with anti-IL-4 receptor. These are teaching toggles only.
