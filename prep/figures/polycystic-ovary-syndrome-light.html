What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • How PCOS develops. In polycystic ovary syndrome the pituitary releases LH in stronger pulses while FSH is relatively low. Insulin resistance in muscle and fat raises circulating insulin, and insulin acts with LH on ovarian theca cells to increase androgen production. Androgen excess and the altered hormone pattern arrest follicle growth, so many small follicles collect in the ovary, ovulation is infrequent, and periods are irregular. Without regular ovulation the womb lining is not shed regularly and can build up. Androgens acting on skin and hair follicles cause acne and hirsutism. Insulin resistance also raises longer term metabolic and diabetes risk. Not everyone has every feature, and the balance differs between people. This is standard teaching in endocrinology and gynaecology texts and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
  • Treatment ideas shown. Lifestyle change and weight loss where relevant, and metformin, mainly improve insulin resistance and downstream androgen excess. Ovulation induction aims to restore ovulation when pregnancy is wanted, which also lets the womb lining shed. Combined care does all three at once and so improves the most gauges. They are teaching toggles only and the figure does not choose between them.
