What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • How vincristine works. Vincristine caps tubulin so microtubules cannot grow. With no spindle, chromosomes cannot line up or separate, so dividing tumour cells arrest in metaphase and die. This is standard teaching in oncology and pharmacology texts and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
  • Why the side effects happen. Nerves, gut and vein all rely on microtubules. Capped tubulin stalls axon transport so feet tingle and go numb with weak grip and a slow bowel; leaked drug burns skin it touches; drug in spinal fluid is fatal; and marrow is largely spared so counts stay steady. Laxative bowel care and a safe IV line are teaching toggles only.
