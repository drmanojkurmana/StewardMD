What was simplified. Flucytosine and griseofulvin, resistance, fungal species and which drug fits which infection, drug interactions (notably the azoles and cytochrome P450), adverse effects and monitoring are left out. Fungal growth is drawn as a teaching score. Levels and the size of each effect are illustrative. The drug choices are teaching toggles of a class: no named drug, dose or schedule is shown or implied, and the figure does not say who should or should not take any of them.
