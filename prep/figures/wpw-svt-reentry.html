What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • Pre-excitation and WPW. An accessory pathway (bypass tract) connects atrium to ventricle outside the AV node, so it bypasses the normal AV nodal delay. In sinus rhythm part of the ventricle is activated early (pre-excitation): the PR interval is short, the QRS starts with a slurred delta wave and is widened by fusion with conduction through the AV node and His-Purkinje system. Many people with a delta wave have no symptoms; WPW syndrome means pre-excitation with tachyarrhythmia symptoms. This is standard teaching in medicine and cardiology texts and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
  • Re-entrant SVT. In orthodromic atrioventricular re-entrant tachycardia the impulse goes antegrade down the AV node and His-Purkinje system (narrow QRS, no delta wave during the tachycardia) and retrograde up the accessory pathway, in a repeating circuit. In antidromic forms it goes down the pathway and up the AV node (wide QRS). AV nodal re-entrant tachycardia (AVNRT) instead uses dual pathways, a fast and a slow pathway, inside and around the compact AV node, with near simultaneous atrial and ventricular activation. Focal atrial tachycardia and junctional forms are other supraventricular mechanisms. Recurrent episodes, their forms and inferior and coronary sinus clues on the ECG guide catheter ablation of the pathway or the slow pathway. This is standard teaching and was not checked against a linked source here, so it is flagged for review.
  • Treatment ideas shown. Vagal manoeuvres and adenosine increase AV nodal block: adenosine administration transiently blocks the AV node, so it terminates SVT that needs the AV node (orthodromic AVRT and AVNRT) and can unmask atrial activity. In AF with pre-excitation, blocking the AV node does not stop the atria and can shunt more impulses down the accessory pathway to a very fast ventricular response, so AV nodal blockers are avoided and urgent rhythm treatment is sought. Catheter ablation destroys the accessory pathway (or the slow pathway in AVNRT), removes pre-excitation and prevents recurrence. These are teaching toggles only.
