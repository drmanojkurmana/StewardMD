What the figure shows, and how sure we are

  • Phases and drug classes. Antimetabolites act in S phase, topoisomerase inhibitors in S and G2, bleomycin in G2, vinca alkaloids and taxanes in M, alkylating and platinum agents in any phase, and targeted kinase inhibitors on the growth signal. This is standard teaching in the pharmacology texts the PrepNucleus lessons cite (Katzung; KD Tripathi) and were not checked against a linked source here, so they are flagged for review.

What was simplified. Checkpoint biology (p53, CDK and cyclins), apoptosis, hormonal, immunological and antibody-based treatment, differences between cycle-specific and cycle-nonspecific action in detail, resistance, the effect on normal fast-dividing tissue (marrow, gut, hair) and the main toxicity of each class are left out. Levels and the size of each block are illustrative. The drug choices are teaching toggles of a class: no named drug, dose or schedule is shown or implied, and the figure does not say who should or should not take any of them.
