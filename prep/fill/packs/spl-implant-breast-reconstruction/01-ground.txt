# Neligan Plastic Surgery 5th Edition (2024): Comprehensive Reference for Implant-Based Breast Reconstruction

The clinical discipline of Implant-Based Breast Reconstruction addresses complex reconstructive and aesthetic surgical problems across craniofacial, microsurgical, breast, trunk, hand, and integumentary domains.

## Implant Reconstruction: Prepectoral vs Subpectoral and ADM Use
Citations and Chapter Sources: Plastic Surgery 5E Vol 5 Ch 4 (Implant-based breast reconstruction)

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Two-stage implant-based breast reconstruction utilizes a submuscular or prepectoral tissue expander exchanged for a permanent silicone gel implant after completing expansion.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: two
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: tissue expander, two-stage, silicone implant

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Direct-to-implant DTI reconstruction places a permanent breast implant immediately at the time of mastectomy, requiring pristine, well-perfused mastectomy skin flaps verified by ICG angiography.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: direct-to-implant, DTI, ICG angiography

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Prepectoral implant reconstruction places the device above the pectoralis major muscle, eliminating animation deformity and muscle spasm associated with subpectoral placement.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: prepectoral, pectoralis major, animation deformity

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Acellular dermal matrix ADM serves as an internal sling or pocket complete wrap, stabilizing the implant position and providing lower pole coverage.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: acellular dermal matrix, ADM, internal sling

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Breast implant-associated anaplastic large cell lymphoma BIA-ALCL is a CD30-positive, ALK-negative T-cell lymphoma associated primarily with textured implant surfaces.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: BIA-ALCL, CD30, ALK-negative, textured implant

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: BIA-ALCL presents classically with a delayed peri-implant seroma occurring at a median of 8 to 10 years following implant placement.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 8, 10
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: delayed seroma, peri-implant seroma

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Diagnostic workup for suspected BIA-ALCL mandates ultrasound-guided aspiration of peri-implant seroma fluid for CD30 immunohistochemistry and cytology.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: CD30, immunohistochemistry, seroma aspiration

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Definitive curative oncological treatment for localized BIA-ALCL is complete en bloc bilateral or ipsilateral capsulectomy with implant removal.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: en bloc capsulectomy, implant removal


Comprehensive Textbook Context:
Surgical planning in Implant-Based Breast Reconstruction requires meticulous anatomical grounding, precise knowledge of blood supply, innervation, fascial planes, and tissue biomechanics.
Operative interventions balance functional restoration of speech, swallowing, mastication, sensory discrimination, and locomotion with aesthetic symmetry and minimal donor site morbidity.
Preoperative evaluation integrates acoustic Doppler, computed tomography angiography, indocyanine green fluorescence imaging, and patient-specific risk factor optimization including smoking cessation, strict glycemic control, and blood pressure management.
Postoperative care prioritizes early mobilization, monitoring of capillary refill, tissue turgor, pinprick bleeding, active rehabilitation, and vigilant surveillance for hematoma, seroma, infection, or microvascular thrombosis.
