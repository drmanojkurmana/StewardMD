# IgG4-related disease (grounding reference: Kelley & Firestein, Harrison, ACR/EULAR criteria)

Rheumatology topic srh-igg4: overview of pathophysiology, diagnostic criteria, clinical features, and management guidelines.

IgG4-related disease (IgG4-RD) is a fibroinflammatory condition characterized by mass-forming tumefactive lesions, tissue-infiltrating IgG4-positive plasma cells, and progressive storiform fibrosis, capable of involving nearly every organ system.

The 2019 ACR/EULAR classification criteria for IgG4-RD establish a three-step validation process: mandatory entry criteria (typical clinical/radiologic organ involvement), exclusion criteria, and a weighted scoring system requiring >=20 points.

The definitive histopathologic triad of IgG4-RD consists of: (1) dense lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate rich in IgG4+ plasma cells; (2) storiform fibrosis (cartwheel-like whorled collagen pattern); and (3) obliterative phlebitis (venous lumen obliteration by inflammatory cells).

Immunohistochemical criteria require an absolute count of IgG4-positive plasma cells exceeding organ-specific cutoffs (typically >10 to 50 per HPF) AND a ratio of IgG4-positive to total IgG-positive plasma cells exceeding 40%.

Serum IgG4 concentrations are elevated (>135 mg/dL) in up to 60% to 70% of active cases; however, elevated serum IgG4 is neither sensitive nor specific, and normal serum IgG4 does not exclude the diagnosis.

Classic organ manifestations of IgG4-RD include Type 1 autoimmune pancreatitis (painless jaundice, sausage-shaped pancreas with hypoattenuating rim), Mikulicz disease (lacrimal and salivary gland enlargement), and Riedel thyroiditis.

Retroperitoneal fibrosis in IgG4-RD manifests as a fibroinflammatory soft tissue mantle encasing the infrarenal abdominal aorta and iliac vessels, characteristically producing medial ureteral deviation, hydronephrosis, and post-renal azotemia.

Systemic glucocorticoids (oral prednisone 0.6 to 0.8 mg/kg/day) are the standard first-line remission induction therapy, producing rapid tumefactive regression; for refractory disease or maintenance, B-cell depletion with rituximab is the targeted biologic of choice.
