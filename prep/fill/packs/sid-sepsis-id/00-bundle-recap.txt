Early recognition, cultures, antimicrobials within 1 hour for suspected septic shock or probable sepsis, 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypoperfusion, noradrenaline to a MAP of 65 mmHg, and urgent source control.
Norepinephrine (first agent), vasopressin (second agent), epinephrine (third agent); hydrocortisone 200 mg daily if ongoing vasopressor requirement; consider adding dobutamine to norepinephrine with decreased LVEF.
