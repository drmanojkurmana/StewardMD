Antenatal hydronephrosis is graded postnatally by the multidisciplinary Urinary Tract Dilation system into UTD P1 low risk, P2 intermediate risk and P3 high risk with parenchymal thinning. The first postnatal renal ultrasound waits until after 48 to 72 hours of life because transient neonatal oliguria underestimates dilatation severity. Vesicoureteric reflux on voiding cystourethrography follows International Reflux Study grading 1 to 5: grade I ureter only, grade II pelvis without dilatation, grade III mild dilatation and blunting, grade IV moderate tortuosity, grade V gross hydroureteronephrosis. Posterior urethral valves show the keyhole sign, distended thick walled bladder with dilated posterior urethra, oligohydramnios and bilateral hydroureteronephrosis, and voiding films show abrupt calibre change with hypertrophied bladder neck and trabeculated wall. Ureteropelvic junction obstruction is the commonest congenital hydronephrosis cause, with pelvicalyceal dilatation ending at a normal ureter. MAG3 furosemide renogram half time under 10 min excludes obstruction, 10 to 20 min is indeterminate, over 20 min confirms obstruction. Multicystic dysplastic kidney has non-communicating cysts, dysplastic stroma, atretic ureter and contralateral reflux in 20 to 30 percent.
 Neuroblastoma arises from the adrenal medulla or sympathetic chain, crosses the midline, encases vessels, calcifies in most cases and metastasises to bone and liver, while Wilms tumour arises inside the kidney with a claw sign of stretched parenchyma, displaces vessels and extends into the renal vein and inferior vena cava. Antenatal hydronephrosis also follows vesicoureteric reflux, duplex collecting systems with ectopic ureterocele obeying the Weigert-Meyer rule, and posterior urethral valves needing urgent catheter drainage and later valve ablation. Renal ultrasound Doppler resistive indices, dimercaptosuccinic acid scans for cortical scars after pyelonephritis, and micturating cystourethrography timing after urinary infection resolution complete the paediatric urinary imaging pathway taught for examinations. Horseshoe kidney, crossed fused ectopia and renal agenesis with compensatory hypertrophy are incidental variants that imaging must recognise and report clearly for surgical planning.
 Technetium-99m MAG3 is the tracer, written 99m, paired with furosemide diuresis.
