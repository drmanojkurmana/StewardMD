Ischaemia and small-bowel tumour anchors.
Acute mesenteric ischaemia: superior mesenteric artery embolism about 50%. Pain out of proportion to examination.
CT angiography: filling defect, thin wall, stranding, pneumatosis intestinalis.
Non-occlusive mesenteric ischaemia: spasm in shock. papaverine infusion after output is restored.
Chronic mesenteric ischaemia equals intestinal angina. Pain near 30 minutes after meals. Stenosis of at least 2 of the 3 visceral arteries.
neuroendocrine tumours of ileum cause desmoplastic mesenteric retraction.
Carcinoid syndrome needs escape from hepatic first-pass metabolism. Flush, diarrhoea, wheeze, tricuspid valve.
Small bowel adenocarcinoma: duodenum about 50%. Syndromes Lynch, FAP, Peutz-Jeghers, plus coeliac disease.
68Ga-DOTATATE PET-CT. Sensitivity over 90%. somatostatin receptor subtype 2.
Agent: papaverine.
Digits: 50, 30, 2, 3, 90, 68, 1.
