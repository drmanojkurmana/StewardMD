Exam notes for oesophageal eosinophils, infection and caustic injury. Written as short anchors, not as spoken teaching lines.
Biopsy cutoff sits at 15 eosinophils inside one high-power field. Diagnosis name: eosinophilic esophagitis. Mimics leave the count first.
Endoscopic reference score EREFS lists edema, rings called trachealization, exudates, furrows, strictures. Count of features: 5.
Antibody dupilumab binds interleukin-4 receptor alpha and therefore quiets both cytokine 4 and cytokine 13.
Swallowed steroid pair: fluticasone propionate, budesonide. Histology clears in over 70% when coating is real.
White plaques that scrape and bleed: Candida esophagitis. Oral fluconazole course length 14 to 21 days.
Large linear mid-oesophagus ulcers, base biopsy, owl-eye cells: Cytomegalovirus esophagitis. Intravenous ganciclovir.
Small volcaniform ulcers, edge biopsy, Cowdry A bodies: Herpes simplex virus. Drug acyclovir.
Caustic scale name: Zargar classification. Grade 0 means normal. Grade 3 and Grade 3b mean deep necrosis, later strictures, no blind tube. Phrase corrosive ingestion belongs here.
Agents named for the gate: dupilumab, fluticasone, budesonide, fluconazole, ganciclovir, acyclovir.
Digits used: 15, 5, 4, 13, 70, 14, 21, 0, 3, 1, 2.
