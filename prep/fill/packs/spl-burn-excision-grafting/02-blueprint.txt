## Tangential Excision, Dermal Matrices and Burn Critical Care
Citations and Chapter Sources: Plastic Surgery 5E Vol 4 Ch 17 (Burn wound excision, grafting, and coverage)

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Early tangential excision of deep burns within 48 to 72 hours of injury significantly decreases bacterial translocation, systemic sepsis, and overall mortality.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 48, 72
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: tangential excision, sepsis, mortality

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Tangential excision removes sequential thin layers of non-viable eschar with a Watson or Goulian knife until uniform punctate capillary bleeding is reached.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: tangential excision, Watson knife, punctate bleeding

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Topical antimicrobial silver sulfadiazine SSD 1% causes transient leukopenia and delayed eschar separation, and is contraindicated in patients with sulfa allergy.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 1%
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: silver sulfadiazine, leukopenia, sulfa allergy

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Mafenide acetate 8.5% cream penetrates deeply into thick burn eschar and cartilage but inhibits carbonic anhydrase, causing metabolic acidosis and hyperventilation.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 8.5%
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: mafenide acetate, carbonic anhydrase, metabolic acidosis

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Acellular dermal matrix Integra consists of a bovine collagen-chondroitin-6-sulfate dermal replacement layer covered by a temporary protective silicone epidermal sheet.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Integra, collagen, silicone

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Integra vascularisation requires 14 to 21 days before the silicone layer is peeled and replaced with an ultra-thin split-thickness skin graft.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 14, 21
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Integra, split-thickness skin graft

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Cultured epithelial autografts CEA provide expansive coverage in massive burns exceeding 70% TBSA but suffer from fragile anchoring and late mechanical shear blister formation.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 70%
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: cultured epithelial autografts, CEA

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Burn hypermetabolism drives severe protein catabolism with caloric demands reaching double basal rates, countered by early enteral feeding within 24 hours.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 24
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: hypermetabolism, enteral feeding
