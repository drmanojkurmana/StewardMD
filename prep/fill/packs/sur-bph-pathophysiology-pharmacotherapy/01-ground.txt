Benign prostatic hyperplasia grows only in the transition zone around the urethra. Intracellular dihydrotestosterone, abbreviated DHT, grips androgen receptors with 5-fold higher affinity than testosterone, driving stromal and epithelial nodules that compress the channel early. The enzyme 5-alpha reductase makes DHT; its Type 2 isoenzyme dominates prostatic stroma while Type 1 works in liver and skin, so selective blockade hits the gland hardest. Pharmacotherapy of BPH starts with alpha-1 antagonists: tamsulosin 0.4 mg once daily and silodosin 8 mg relax prostatic smooth muscle within days through alpha-1A selectivity, without shrinking the gland, at the price of dizziness and retrograde ejaculation.

The 5-alpha reductase inhibitors finasteride 5 mg and dutasteride 0.5 mg deliver true prostate volume reduction of 20% to 25% and halve PSA by 50%, preventing retention and surgery in glands over 30 cc, though full effect needs six months and libido may dip. Combination therapy pairs a blocker with a 5-ARI: the MTOPS trial of doxazosin plus finasteride and the CombAT trial of tamsulosin plus dutasteride proved fewer retentions and operations, best when volume tops 30 cc or PSA tops 1.5. Tadalafil 5 mg daily, a PDE-5 inhibitor raising cGMP through the nitric oxide pathway, eases storage symptoms together with erectile dysfunction in one tablet. Intraoperative floppy iris syndrome, abbreviated IFIS, billows the iris during cataract surgery in men on tamsulosin, so the ophthalmologist must be warned, as late stopping changes nothing. The surgical indications BPH are refractory retention, recurrent gross haematuria, recurrent infections, bladder stones, and renal failure from bilateral hydronephrosis; delay fibroses the detrusor. Testosterone itself drives little growth until converted, which is why the Type 2 isoenzyme, not circulating testosterone level, predicts response. Counsel that ejaculate volume falls on 5-ARI therapy and reverses after stopping, and that PSA must be doubled for interpretation once finasteride or dutasteride has halved it. Reassess symptom scores and flow rates yearly, stepping up from monotherapy to combination therapy as glands enlarge.
