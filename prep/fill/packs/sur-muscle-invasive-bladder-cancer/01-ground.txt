Muscle invasive bladder cancer is treated with cisplatin doublets before surgery. Accelerated MVAC, built from methotrexate with vinblastine, doxorubicin, and cisplatin, or gemcitabine with cisplatin lifts absolute survival by 5 to 8 percent at 5 years. Renal function with hearing and performance is checked for cisplatin fitness, because carboplatin substitution weakens the gain and cystectomy follows promptly.

Radical cystectomy in men removes bladder with prostate, seminal vesicles, distal ureters, and regional nodes, while women undergo anterior pelvic exenteration clearing bladder, urethra, uterus, ovaries, and anterior vaginal wall. Pelvic node dissection spans external iliac, internal iliac, and obturator packets to yield at least 10 to 15 nodes for staging.

The ileal conduit, called Bricker diversion, isolates a 15 to 20 cm ileal segment taken 15 cm above the ileocecal valve for an external bag, suiting every fitness level. The orthotopic neobladder in Studer or Hautmann design detubularizes 50 to 60 cm of ileum into a reservoir joined to the native urethral stump for motivated fit patients. Bowel diversions reabsorb ammonium and chloride while losing bicarbonate and sodium, so hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis is corrected with oral alkali plus bicarbonate and vitamin D checks.

Solitary T2 tumours without hydronephrosis or extensive CIS may take trimodal therapy: maximal TURBT followed by concurrent chemoradiotherapy with lifelong watched bladders. Locally advanced or metastatic disease starts with enfortumab vedotin, a Nectin-4 antibody drug conjugate, plus pembrolizumab, improving progression free and overall survival with skin, nerve, and glucose monitoring.

Counselling covers continence, sexual function, and stoma versus neobladder choice before the operation, since reconstruction is planned together. Salvage cystectomy stays ready for trimodal recurrence, and mapped nodal positivity guides adjuvant therapy after surgery. Smoking cessation is enforced at diagnosis, because tobacco drives recurrence in preserved and reconstructed bladders alike. Night leakage after neobladder is managed with timed voiding and pelvic floor drills. Parastomal hernia with stomal stenosis is watched at every conduit review, and peristomal skin is protected with fitted appliances.
