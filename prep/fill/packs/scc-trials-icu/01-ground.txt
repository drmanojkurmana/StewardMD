ICU trials sheet: what each study tested and where the primary landed. Figures referenced: 6, 12, 30, 16, 150, 81, 108, 180, 90, 7, 10, 4, 28, 200, 33, 60, 50, 3, 80, 5, 19. Agents referenced: hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, hydroxyethyl starch, albumin, saline, Ringer acetate, balanced crystalloid, oxygen.

Lung protective ventilation won in ARMA within ARDSNet: breaths scaled to predicted mass at six millilitres per kilo beat twelve, with end inspiratory plateau capped at thirty of water, cutting deaths. Body estimate comes from stature, never bedside weight.

French investigators proned severely hypoxaemic patients (ratio under one fifty) for sixteen plus hours daily atop small breaths in PROSEVA, and survival improved. Positioning functioned as therapy, not rescue theatre. Nasal high flow in FLORALI trimmed 90 day deaths in hypoxaemic failure, yet day 28 tube rates barely moved overall.

Glucose strategy went moderate after NICE-SUGAR: the 81 to 108 band raised 90 day deaths and lows versus staying at or under 180. Blood thrift survived TRICC, where a 7 gram trigger matched 10 grams among stable ICU patients, favouring the lower cut in milder illness. Colloid hopes died in SAFE: four percent albumin equalled saline on 28 day deaths, with brain injured patients faring worse.

Starch resuscitation failed in 6S, raising 90 day deaths and machine dependence versus acetate Ringer in sepsis. Salt choice mattered in SMART, where balanced solutions beat saline on a 30 day bundle of death, fresh dialysis, and lasting renal harm. Volume dose did not in CLOVERS: tight versus generous strategies after early resuscitation tied on 90 day deaths. Reasonable balanced fluids, no floods.

Mandated Rivers style bundles fell thrice: ProCESS, ARISE, and ProMISe each found no survival edge for protocolised goal therapy once background care already delivered prompt antimicrobials and lactate review.

Steroid stories split. ADRENAL infused 200 mg hydrocortisone daily through septic shock without 90 day gain, though pressure support lifted sooner. HYPRESS could not halt progression from severe sepsis toward shock. Dexamethasone at 6 mg to ten days in RECOVERY lowered 28 day deaths, but solely among COVID cases requiring oxygen or machines; room air cases gained nothing.

Post arrest cooling lost in TTM2: holding 33 degrees matched fever control on six month survival while rocking haemodynamics. Kidney timing lost in STARRT-AKI: hurrying the machine changed no 90 day outcome and stranded extra survivors on dialysis, though crashing potassium, acid, lung water, poisons, and uraemic injury still command action. ECMO in EOLIA crossed over freely and missed its 60 day mortality target in profound ARDS (entries under fifty beyond three hours or under eighty beyond six), proving neither futility nor universal indication.

Read endpoints before slogans. A missed primary stays missed despite rosy subsets, and crossover muddies control arms.
