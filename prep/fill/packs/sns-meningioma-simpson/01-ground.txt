Cover tumours, not brain tumours. Meningiomas are dural-based with tail, even enhancement and thick bone, unlike gliomas; small ones watch.

Grade rules radiation. Grade 1 benign, grade 2 atypical with 4 to 19 mitoses or invasion, grade 3 anaplastic with 20 plus; grade outranks size.

Simpson scores removal. Grade I clears tumour, dura and bone for cure of convexity lumps, down to grade V biopsy, with quoted recurrence per grade.

Spare nerves at the base. Olfactory, sphenoid wing and petroclival tumours get planned subtotal plus radiosurgery, never carotid injury.

Radiate leftovers and wild grades. Grade 2 subtotal or recurrent plus all grade 3 get rays per ROAM and 22042; grade 1 total gets scans.

Spine favours thoracic women. Psammoma bodies mark them, posterior Simpson I cures pain and myelopathy, and bladder signs rush surgery.

Many lumps mean NF2. Bilateral hearing tumours with merlin loss trigger gene tests, family scans, counselling and yearly child checks.

Never cut sheath tumours. Optic sheath lumps with tram tracks get rays not resection, since cutting blinds; vision is charted each visit.
