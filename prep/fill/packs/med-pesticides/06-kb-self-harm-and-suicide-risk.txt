# Self-harm and suicide risk (adult)
Adults
Emergency department, ward, primary care
Treat the injury or poisoning and start a psychosocial assessment at the same time; do not use risk scales or low, medium or high labels to predict suicide or decide discharge; agree a needs-based plan and written safety plan, restrict access to means, and arrange aftercare within 48 hours if concerns remain.
Aliases: deliberate self-harm; DSH; suicide attempt; attempted suicide; suicidal ideation; suicide risk assessment; safety plan.
# In the emergency department
Treat the injury or poisoning without delay (see Acute poisoning: general approach (adult) and specific poisoning protocols). In any overdose, check a paracetamol level because early features may be absent.
At triage establish: severity of injury and urgency, emotional state and distress, immediate safety concerns, safeguarding issues, and willingness to accept medical and mental health care.
Ask what means were used and whether the person still has access to them (tablets, pesticides, weapons, ligatures); discuss removing them collaboratively.
