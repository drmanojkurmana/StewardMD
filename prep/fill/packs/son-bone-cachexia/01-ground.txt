Bone-modifying agents and cancer cachexia: grounding notes

Bisphosphonate action on bone
- Nitrogen-containing bisphosphonates have a strong affinity for mineral exposed on bone surfaces. Bone mineral is a crystalline calcium phosphate, with hydroxyapatite as its main form.
- Resorbing osteoclasts absorb the agent as they dissolve that surface.
- Inside the cell, the agent inhibits farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase in the mevalonic acid route. This reduces protein prenylation, interferes with protein trafficking, and may push the osteoclast toward apoptosis.

Skeletal-related events
- A skeletal-related event (SRE) groups bone pain needing opioids, radiotherapy or surgery aimed at bone, high blood calcium, pathological fracture, and spinal cord compression.
- Bone-directed agents lower the chance of these events but do not treat the tumor itself.
- Treatment is recommended from the time bone metastases are diagnosed and continues alongside systemic cancer therapy.
- In randomized trials of castration-resistant prostate cancer, zoledronic acid given at 3 to 4 week intervals reduced events by 25%.
- Bone-supportive therapy showed no change in overall survival. Denosumab trials found survival and investigator-reported progression unchanged versus zoledronic acid.

Kidney limits
- Intravenous aminobisphosphonates are contraindicated when the glomerular filtration rate (GFR) falls below 35 mL/min per 1.73 m2.
- Zoledronic acid and pamidronate both fall into this class, so the same limit applies to each.
- Above that threshold, doses still need adjustment for renal function.
- For hypercalcemia, pamidronate is given intravenously at 60 to 90 mg over 2 to 4 hours.
- Denosumab does not adversely affect renal function and is the preferred option when renal impairment is significant.

Denosumab
- A fully human monoclonal antibody that binds RANKL, the ligand driving osteoclast formation and activity.
- Cleared by the reticuloendothelial system instead of the kidney.
- In a phase 3 comparison with zoledronic acid, grade 3 or higher hypocalcemia occurred in 5% on denosumab and 1% on zoledronic acid.
- Vitamin D deficiency, low calcium intake, and advanced chronic kidney disease raise the risk of low calcium with antiresorptive therapy.

Cancer cachexia
- Cachexia may develop in as many as 80% of patients with advanced malignancy.
- Clinically important unintentional weight loss means more than 5% of body weight over 6 to 12 months.
- Three stages are recognized. Pre-cachexia, with weight loss up to 5%, is the best window for prevention.
- The middle stage is set by weight loss beyond 5%, or a body mass index under 20 with more than 2% loss, and comes with inflammation.
- Refractory cachexia means therapy-resistant cancer, poor performance status, and a life expectancy under 3 months.

Cytokines
- Cytokines and inflammatory factors drive cachexia, though reduced intake also contributes.
- Tumor necrosis factor alpha increases lipolysis, muscle breakdown, and glucose turnover. Most evidence for this comes from animal models.
- Interferon gamma enhances lipolysis and reduces protein synthesis.
- Interleukin 1 causes anorexia, peripheral proteolysis, and release of interleukin 6.
- Interleukin 6 causes severe wasting and is measurable in serum, unlike tumor necrosis factor.

Leptin
- Leptin is an adipocyte hormone that controls fat stores by lowering appetite and raising resting energy expenditure through hypothalamic neuropeptides.
- Its levels remain persistently low in cachectic patients, which suggests tumors can mimic leptin and sustain anorexia and weight loss.

Anabolic steroids
- Androgens act through the androgen receptor, a ligand-activated transcription factor in the steroid hormone nuclear receptor family.
- Testosterone increases fiber size and satellite cell numbers in muscle and promotes myogenic differentiation.
- In men with human immunodeficiency virus and weight loss, testosterone improved lean body mass and muscle strength.
- Oxandrolone is an oral 17-alpha-alkylated androgen with hepatotoxicity risk.
- In cancer, anabolic steroids have no proven nutrition benefit and may ease some symptoms.
- Testosterone is contraindicated in men with prostate or breast cancer.
