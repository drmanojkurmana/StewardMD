Squamous cell carcinoma causes over 95 percent of penile cancers, with high-risk HPV 16 and 18 driving 30 to 50 percent; circumcision, hygiene, cessation, and boyhood vaccination lower risk. Erythroplasia of Queyrat of glans and prepuce with Bowen disease of shaft are both carcinoma in situ Tis, biopsied and treated topically or by laser when small.

Low-risk T1 disease is controlled by wide excision, glansectomy with skin graft, or Mohs surgery with clear margins of 3 to 5 mm, while invasive T2 disease takes partial penectomy preserving a 2 to 3 cm stump with spatulated neo-meatus for upright voiding. Inguinal lymphadenectomy ILND is mandatory from T1G3 and T2 upward since palpation misses occult micrometastases in 20 to 25 percent, and dynamic sentinel node biopsy DSNB with technetium-99m nanocolloid plus blue or ICG dye spares the 30 to 50 percent wound morbidity of full clearance.

Two or more positive inguinal nodes or extracapsular extension pN2 or pN3 adds bilateral pelvic lymphadenectomy of iliac and obturator packets. Fixed ulcerated or bulky nodes start with TIP paclitaxel, ifosfamide, and cisplatin neoadjuvant chemotherapy for three to four courses before consolidation surgery, with palliation for non-responders.

Lymphatic mapping with blue dye traces first landing nodes, and failed mapping repeats staging rather than guessing clearance. Wound breakdown with lymphoedema after groin dissection is managed with elevation, compression, and antibiotics. Sexual counselling with prosthetic and reconstructive options follows every amputation discussion. Surveillance pairs groin examination with cross-sectional imaging, because early nodal relapse still cures with salvage dissection. Smoking status with HPV testing is recorded at diagnosis to refine risk counselling. Groin ultrasound with fine needle sampling triages palpable nodes before formal dissection. Smoking cessation with HPV vaccination counselling continues at every follow up visit. Lymphoedema physiotherapy with fitted garments follows extensive bilateral groin surgery. Psychosexual support with partner counselling completes rehabilitation after penectomy.
