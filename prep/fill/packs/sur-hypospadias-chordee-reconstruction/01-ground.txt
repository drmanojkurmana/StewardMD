Hypospadias presents the classic hypospadias triad: a proximal ventral meatus displaced down the underside, ventral chordee bending the shaft, and a dorsal prepuce hooding the glans while the ventrum lies bare. Severity follows meatal position and curvature together, with distal variants suited to single-stage repair and proximal ones demanding staged work.

Neonatal circumcision is an absolute contraindication to circumcision in hypospadiac boys, because preputial skin furnishes dorsal hood flaps and inner grafts for reconstruction. Birth teams must preserve the entire prepuce and counsel parents against ritual cutting before surgical review.

Repair timing targets 6 and 18 months, the accepted hypospadias age window before genital awareness and durable operative memory. Infant tissues handle finely and heal supplely, while deferral stiffens skin and loads the child with recall of dressings and catheters.

The Snodgrass repair, TIP urethroplasty, tubularizes distal and mid-shaft defects by a midline relaxing incision that hinges the urethral plate into a tension-free tube without graft harvest. A supple plate is prerequisite; the reward is a terminal slit meatus with minimal dissection.

Where the plate lies flat and inelastic, the Mathieu repair flips a perimeatal flap of ventral shaft skin upward over the defect, closing distal holes reliably on hostile tissue at the price of a rounded meatus.

Proximal hypospadias with chordee beyond 30 degrees after degloving requires plate division for straightening, and the resulting gap mandates the two-stage Bracka technique: first grafting with inner prepuce or buccal mucosa, then tubularizing the matured graft months later.

Urethrocutaneous fistula complicates 5% to 15% of repairs as the leading morbidity, and a dartos barrier flap of dartos or tunica vaginalis interposed between neourethra and skin sharply curtails leakage through waterproof double coverage.

Meatal stenosis prevention in TIP surgery means halting tubularization 2 mm to 3 mm short of the glans tip with loose distal closure, preserving a broad meatus that streams freely instead of scarring to a dilator-dependent pinhole.
