Acute severe colitis needs rescue infliximab or ciclosporin on day 3 by Travis criteria, and colectomy on days 4 to 7 if stool frequency, CRP and albumin fail to improve. Steroids alone beyond this window raise perforation risk without benefit.

Restorative proctocolectomy with J-pouch ileal pouch-anal anastomosis is the standard for fit young patients, giving continence without a permanent stoma. Emergency or steroid-loaded cases are staged, starting with subtotal colectomy and ileostomy, with the pouch built months later.

Pouchitis with urgency, bleeding and fever responds to 2 weeks of ciprofloxacin with metronidazole. Chronic antibiotic-dependent cases step up to probiotics or biologics, and surgical revision is reserved for mechanical failure.

Dysplasia in flat mucosa or strictures in longstanding pancolitis over 8 years mandates proctocolectomy, because surveillance misses synchronous cancer in scarred colons.

Ileorectal anastomosis preserves fertility and avoids pelvic dissection, but leaves rectal cancer risk needing lifelong surveillance. It suits older or poor-sphincter patients who accept the trade.

Continent Brooke ileostomy with Kock pouch suits failed pouches and poor sphincters, using a nipple valve that needs intubation 4 to 6 times daily. Valve slippage is the commonest mechanical failure.

Primary sclerosing cholangitis with ulcerative colitis raises colorectal cancer and cholangiocarcinoma risk, mandating annual colonoscopy with chromoendoscopy from diagnosis.

Laparoscopic colectomy matches open surgery for cancer clearance with faster recovery, but toxic megacolon with caecal diameter over 9 cm needs urgent open subtotal colectomy before perforation. Travis Oxford criteria count stools with CRP above 45 and albumin below 30 on day 3 to trigger rescue. The Kock nipple valve is fashioned by intussuscepting ileum and stapling the collar, which explains why desusception causes incontinence. Staged pouch surgery spaces colectomy, pouch build and ileostomy closure across three admissions to let steroids wean and nutrition recover. Postoperative surveillance after ileorectal join uses flexible sigmoidoscopy with biopsies every year.
