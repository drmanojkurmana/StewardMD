Watchful drugs sheet. Figures referenced: 2, 4, 8, 12, 10, 3, 2016, 5, 5.0, 0, 50. Agents referenced: methotrexate, leflunomide, hydroxychloroquine, tofacitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib, tocilizumab, sarilumab, tacrolimus, cyclosporine.

Methotrexate demands baseline blood counts with creatinine and liver enzymes (AST, ALT), rechecked every two to four weeks while escalating and every eight to twelve weeks when stable; transaminases beyond two to three times normal shelve the drug. Counts with kidneys and liver set the baseline rhythm.

Leflunomide pushes pressures: check blood pressure every single visit, since sympathetic firing hypertenses up to ten percent, with liver panels monthly to quarterly. Every visit needs a cuff.

Hydroxychloroquine threatens retinas slowly: twenty sixteen eye academy rules demand baseline fundus with SD-OCT inside year one, then yearly ten-dash-two fields with SD-OCT after five years (sooner when daily dose tops five point zero milligrams per kilo or kidneys falter). Baseline scans with yearly fields guard sight.

JAK blockers (tofacitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib) wear boxed warnings from ORAL Surveillance: heart attacks and strokes, venous clots to legs and lungs, deep infections, and cancers led by lymphoma and lung tumors. Boxed warnings bundle clots with cancers.

Interleukin six blockers (tocilizumab, sarilumab) fatten lipids dose-dependently, dip neutrophils transiently, and rarely burst diverticula of the lower gut; check lipids at baseline and periodically, and scan every acute belly urgently. Lipids rise while bowels rarely burst.

Tumor necrosis factor blockers must dodge failing hearts: moderate-to-severe failure (NYHA class three or four) or ejection fraction below fifty percent forbids them, since trials counted extra heart failure admissions and cardiac deaths. Failing pumps forbid anti-TNF.

Tumor necrosis factor blockers must also dodge demyelination: personal or first-degree family multiple sclerosis or central demyelinating disease forbids them, since they spark or worsen optic neuritis, transverse myelitis, and fresh demyelination. Myelin history forbids anti-TNF.

Calcineurin blockers (tacrolimus, cyclosporine) need blood level steering by twelve-hour troughs with creatinine and potassium watches; afferent arteriolar squeeze injures kidneys acutely, scars them chronically, and lifts potassium. Troughs with creatinine steer narrow drugs.
