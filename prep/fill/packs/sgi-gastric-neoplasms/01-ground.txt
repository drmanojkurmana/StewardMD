Anchors for gastric tumours. Different wording from the spoken steps.
Lauren classification: gland-forming intestinal pattern versus discohesive signet-ring cells. Digit 1 appears because type labels use 1.
Hereditary diffuse gastric cancer: germline CDH1 mutations, E-cadherin gone, prophylactic total gastrectomy.
Gastrointestinal stromal tumours start in interstitial cells of Cajal. Stain CD117 in over 95%. DOG1 backup. Drivers KIT or PDGFRA.
Imatinib mesylate is the tyrosine kinase inhibitor for unresectable, metastatic, or high-risk resected GIST.
Gastric neuroendocrine tumours: Type 1 gastric NET with autoimmune gastritis, type 2 with gastrinoma and MEN1, type 3 sporadic and aggressive. Digits 1, 2 and 3.
Linitis plastica equals leather bottle, rigid from desmoplasia.
early gastric cancer that is mucosal differentiated: endoscopic resection. Ulcerated lesions only to 3 cm without vessel invasion.
FLOT chemotherapy drugs: fluorouracil, leucovorin, oxaliplatin, docetaxel.
Named agents: imatinib, docetaxel, fluorouracil, leucovorin, oxaliplatin.
Digits: 1, 117, 95, 2, 3.
