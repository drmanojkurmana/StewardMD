Takayasu arteritis, the pulseless disease, is a chronic granulomatous panarteritis of the aorta and its great branches (subclavian, carotid, renal) plus pulmonary arteries, striking young females under forty. Big vessels inflame in young women.

Twenty twenty two ACR EULAR classification demands onset at sixty or below plus imaged large vessel vasculitis as entry, then five or more points from pulse loss, claudication, bruits, arm pressure gaps above twenty, and imaging damage (narrowing, occlusion, wall thickening). Young onset with imaged damage classifies.

Hata angiography sorts six patterns: arch branches alone, ascending arch disease with or without descending thoracic aorta, thoracoabdominal with renal arteries, abdominal with renal arteries alone, and combined extensive disease (the commonest). Maps run from arch to abdomen.

Renal artery narrowing hits thirty to fifty percent, usually ostial or proximal, firing renin-angiotensin-aldosterone toward severe renovascular hypertension that hides when cuffs wrap pulseless claudicating arms. Kidney ischemia raises pressure silently.

Hearts decide survival: inflammatory root dilatation brings severe aortic regurgitation in up to twenty five percent, ascending aortitis narrows coronary ostia, failure follows, and branch pulmonary vasculitis breeds pulmonary hypertension. Roots leak, ostia narrow, hearts fail.

Modern imaging (MR angiography, CT angiography, eighteen-F FDG PET-CT) has replaced catheter angiography; MR spots early mural edema (concentric wall thickening with delayed mural enhancement) before fixed stenosis scars the lumen. Walls swell before lumens narrow.

Induction uses high-dose prednisone at one milligram per kilogram daily, but relapses strike fifty to seventy percent during tapering, so steroid-sparing drugs (methotrexate, azathioprine, leflunomide, or mycophenolate mofetil) start early. Steroids induce; sparers sustain.

Refractory disease yields to biologics: anti-TNF monoclonals (infliximab, adalimumab) or interleukin six blockade with tocilizumab spare steroids and freeze lesion progression. Surgery and stenting wait strictly until inflammation burns out. Biologics freeze lesions; knives wait for quiet.
