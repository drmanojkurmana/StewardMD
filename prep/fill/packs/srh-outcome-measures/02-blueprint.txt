Disease activity and outcome measures: DAS28 with CDAI, SDAI, ACR responses, HAQ-DI, BASDAI with ASDAS, SLEDAI-2K, BVAS, psoriatic MDA.

In rheumatoid arthritis, composite disease activity indices guide treat-to-target adjustments: DAS28, evaluating 28 joints with ESR or CRP plus patient global, defines remission as below 2.6 and low disease activity as 3.2 or less; Clinical Disease Activity Index, called CDAI, which excludes laboratory tests entirely, defines remission as 2.8 or less and low disease activity as 10.0 or less. Visits count joints formally, never by impression.

Simplified Disease Activity Index, called SDAI, incorporates 28 tender and swollen joint counts, patient and physician global assessments on 0 to 10 cm visual analogue scales, and C-reactive protein measured in mg per dL; an SDAI score of 3.3 or less defines stringent remission, aligning closely with the ACR/EULAR Boolean 2.0 remission definition. SDAI plus CDAI need no calculator.

ACR20, ACR50, and ACR70 response criteria are standardized clinical trial endpoints requiring at least 20 percent, 50 percent, or 70 percent improvement in both tender and swollen joint counts, plus matching improvement in at least 3 of 5 core variables: patient global, physician global, pain VAS, HAQ-DI disability, and acute-phase reactants. Trials report all three rungs together.

The Health Assessment Questionnaire Disability Index, called HAQ-DI, assesses functional physical disability across 20 activities in 8 categories from dressing to walking, scored from 0, meaning no disability, to 3, meaning completely disabled; a change of at least 0.22 points represents the universally accepted minimal clinically important difference, called MCID. Function matters as much as swelling.

In axial spondyloarthritis, disease activity is measured by the Bath Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity Index, called BASDAI, a 0 to 10 scale with 4 or more defining active disease, and the Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity Score, called ASDAS, incorporating CRP or ESR, where ASDAS below 1.3 denotes inactive disease, 1.3 to below 2.1 moderate activity, 2.1 to 3.5 or less high activity, and above 3.5 very high activity. NSAID and biologic starts follow ASDAS bands.

In systemic lupus erythematosus, SLEDAI-2K evaluates 24 weighted organ items over the preceding 30 days, where a score of 6 or more denotes clinically active disease requiring therapy modification, and a score of 4 or less is required to fulfill the Lupus Low Disease Activity State, called LLDAS. Serology with complements rides alongside the score.

In systemic necrotizing vasculitis, the Birmingham Vasculitis Activity Score, BVAS version 3, evaluates 64 items across nine organ systems, distinguishing new or worse persistent manifestations from chronic irreversible damage, which is independently tracked using the Vasculitis Damage Index, called VDI. Activity and damage are scored separately at every visit.

In psoriatic arthritis, Minimal Disease Activity, called MDA, is defined as fulfilling at least 5 of 7 outcome criteria: tender joints 1 or less, swollen joints 1 or less, PASI 1 or less or body surface area 3 percent or less, patient pain VAS 15 mm or less, patient global VAS 20 mm or less, HAQ-DI 0.5 or less, and tender entheseal points 1 or less. Five of seven is the treat-to-target bar.
