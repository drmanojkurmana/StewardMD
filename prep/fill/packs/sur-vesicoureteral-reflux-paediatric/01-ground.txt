Vesicoureteral reflux, abbreviated VUR, is graded I to V on voiding cystourethrography, from urine flicking into the ureter to Grade V tortuosity with caliceal blunting, always graded by the worst film since reflux varies. Primary reflux comes from a short congenital submucosal ureteral tunnel: the healthy length to diameter ratio of 5:1 shutters flap-valve competence during filling, and only surgical lengthening restores the plumbing, never drugs.

The 99mTc-DMSA scan, dimercaptosuccinic acid cortical scintigraphy, is the gold standard for renal scarring, mapping focal photon defects and split function after febrile infections, with yearly repeats tracking new scars that steer surgery. Deflux, dextranomer hyaluronic acid gel, injected by the STING technique under the orifice with the HIT procedure of tunnel hydrodistension, cures 75% to 85% of Grades II to IV as a day case, mounding the orifice into a coapted tunnel. Cohen reimplantation tunnels the ureter cross-trigonal across the bladder in a fresh submucosal tunnel, succeeding in 95% to 98% open or robotically for high grade or post Deflux reflux, since diagonal length guarantees the valve.

Leadbetter-Politano reimplantation passes the ureter through a fresh higher neohiatus inside the opened bladder, keeping straight anatomy for later endoscopy, closing the old hiatus behind. Secondary reflux from posterior urethral valves or neurogenic bladder is managed by treating the outlet first, since reimplantation into a hostile high pressure bladder fails. Open surgery stays preferred for grossly dilated tortuous ureters needing tapering. Lich-Gregoir extravesical reimplantation splits detrusor to bare mucosa without entering the lumen, minimising bleeding and spasms and suiting bilateral single sitting repair, with brief stenting for tight tunnels. Bladder and bowel dysfunction, BBD with holding and constipation, halves spontaneous resolution and doubles failure, so constipation, timed voiding, and regularity come before any reimplant; low grades often vanish, persistent high grades are corrected. Vesicoureteral reflux care lengthens tunnels and calms bowels.
