# Oesophageal cancer: squamous cell carcinoma and adenocarcinoma
# Source: StewardMD gastroenterology knowledge base

## Epidemiology and risk factors
Squamous cell carcinoma (SCC) of the oesophagus arises in the proximal and mid thoracic oesophagus. Risk factors include tobacco smoking, alcohol consumption, hot beverages, nutritional deficiencies (riboflavin, vitamin A), achalasia, and Plummer-Vinson syndrome. Tylosis (Howel-Evans syndrome) carries a 95% lifetime risk of SCC.

Adenocarcinoma arises in the distal oesophagus and gastro-oesophageal junction, typically from Barrett oesophagus (intestinal metaplasia). Risk factors include chronic GERD, obesity, smoking, and male sex.

## Genetic syndromes
Tylosis (Howel-Evans syndrome): caused by RHBDF2 mutations on chromosome 17q25, autosomal dominant (AD) inheritance, characterised by focal palmoplantar keratoderma, requiring surveillance from age 20, and a 95% lifetime risk of oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma.
Plummer-Vinson (Paterson-Kelly) syndrome: cervical esophageal webs combined with microcytic iron deficiency anemia and koilonychia (spoon-shaped nails). Associated with postcricoid and upper oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma risk in up to 10% of cases.

## Staging investigations
EUS (endoscopic ultrasound): most accurate tool for T-staging (90% accuracy) and regional N-staging (nodal assessment). Visualises layers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 of the wall and allows fine-needle aspiration of peri-oesophageal and mediastinal lymph nodes.
PET-CT: preferred for distant metastasis assessment (M-staging).

## Neoadjuvant treatment
CROSS trial: neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy regimen of carboplatin plus paclitaxel chemotherapy with 41.4 Gy of radiation over 4 to 5 weeks, followed by surgical resection. This is the standard for locally advanced resectable oesophageal cancer.

## Adjuvant immunotherapy
CheckMate 577 trial: adjuvant nivolumab (anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody) after neoadjuvant chemoradiation and resection in patients with residual disease (non-pCR). Nivolumab significantly doubled median disease-free survival from 11.0 months to 22.4 months (up to 1 year of treatment).

## Endoscopic resection
ESD (endoscopic submucosal dissection): indicated for superficial neoplastic lesions confined to mucosa (T1a) without lymphovascular invasion. Achieves en bloc resection rates exceeding 90%.

## Palliation
SEMS (self-expanding metal stents): covered or partially covered designs for palliation of dysphagia in unresectable or metastatic obstructing oesophageal cancer. Restores luminal patency for swallowing.

## Chemotherapy drugs
Carboplatin (platinum analogue), paclitaxel (taxane), nivolumab (anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor).
