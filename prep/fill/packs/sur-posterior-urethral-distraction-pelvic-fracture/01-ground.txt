Pelvic fracture urethral distraction defects, abbreviated PFUDD, avulse the membranous urethra at the bulbomembranous junction, leaving a fibrous distraction defect of scar that displaces the prostatic apex upward. Blood at meatus with inability to void and a palpable bladder from retention mark the injury. Evaluation demands the simultaneous RUG and VCUG up-and-down study: retrograde urethrography up the urethra plus voiding cystourethrography down through a suprapubic tube, measuring the defect and testing bladder neck competence. Gentle pressure matters, since forceful injection drives venous intravasation.

The Webster elaborated approach repairs the gap by a standardised 4-step progressive elaboration, each step added only for residual tension. Step 1 Webster mobilises the anterior bulbar urethra forward to the penoscrotal junction, a wide urethral mobilization gaining 2 cm to 3 cm, with the bulbospongiosus cover fully divided. Step 2 Webster performs crural separation, dividing the midline intercrural septum between the corpora for another 1 cm to 2 cm, staying strictly midline to dodge cavernosal bleeding. Step 3 Webster resects an inferior pubectomy wedge of pubic bone, laying the urethra straight to the apex through a bony trough while dorsal vein bleeders are packed. Step 4 Webster performs supracrural rerouting around a corporal body through the obturator foramen for the longest gaps, confirming a kink-free straight shot before suturing.

Primary realignment at the time of trauma, immediate railroading over wires, is reserved for stable select patients because haemorrhage, neurovascular injury, and erectile dysfunction risk all rise; most cases are diverted suprapubically for delayed anastomotic repair with the best potency and continence. Children with open growth plates and men with prior urethral surgery need individualised planning, since scarred or growing tissues alter each Webster step. Document preoperative erectile function and continence so postoperative change is measurable. Pelvic fracture urethral injuries reward staged evaluation and tension free repair.
