Metabolic surgery is offered at body mass index over 40, or over 35 with comorbidity, and over 30 with uncontrolled type 2 diabetes after failed medical therapy. Every case passes through multidisciplinary assessment with dietetics, psychology and anaesthesia before listing.

Sleeve gastrectomy removes 75 to 80 percent of the stomach over a 36 to 40 French bougie. The pylorus is preserved, which lowers ulcer risk, but reflux worsens in 20 percent because the narrow tube raises intragastric pressure.

Roux-en-Y gastric bypass builds a 30 mL pouch with a 75 to 150 cm alimentary limb and a 50 cm biliopancreatic limb. It gives superior type 2 diabetes remission and reflux control at the cost of internal hernia and marginal ulcer risk.

One-anastomosis gastric bypass uses a 200 cm biliopancreatic limb with a single gastrojejunostomy. Weight loss and diabetes control are effective, but bile reflux and malabsorption need surveillance.

Early leak within 7 days shows tachycardia over 120, fever and shoulder pain and needs urgent washout with drainage. Late strictures at 3 to 6 weeks present with dysphagia and respond to endoscopic dilation.

The Petersen defect between the mesenteries and the jejunojejunal mesenteric defect must both be closed with nonabsorbable suture at bypass surgery, since internal hernia peaks after major weight loss closes the mesenteric gaps paradoxically.

Lifelong multivitamins with calcium, vitamin D, B12, iron and thiamine follow all bariatric procedures, with copper and zinc added after bypass. Deficiencies cause anaemia, neuropathy and Wernicke disease, so bloods are checked every 6 to 12 months.

Dumping follows bypass and sleeve. Early vasomotor flushing starts within 30 minutes of sugars, and late hypoglycaemia from excess insulin secretion strikes at 1 to 3 hours. Small low-glycaemic meals with fluids separated by 30 minutes manage most cases before revision surgery is considered. Follow-up continues annually for life with weight, blood pressure and diabetes review, since regain after 2 years signals pouch dilation or fistula needing endoscopic assessment.
