# Rituximab (HHV8-associated multicentric Castleman disease)
Rituximab (HHV8-associated multicentric Castleman disease), first-line for HHV8-positive MCD, multicentric Castleman disease, HHV8-positive (plasmablastic/plasmacytic); occurs in HIV-1-positive or HIV-1-negative patients.
# Biomarkers
Rituximab (anti-CD20) is the backbone for HHV8-positive multicentric Castleman disease (in HIV-1-positive or HIV-1-negative patients). Occult Kaposi sarcoma (KS) is prevalent in HIV/HHV8+ MCD and may flare after rituximab or prednisone. Per Standard Guidelines, Castleman Disease, v2.2026 (extract lines 178 to 181, 300 to 302; KS caution footnote c, extract lines 187 to 189).
# Premedications
Rituximab premedication, acetaminophen and antihistamine per standard rituximab labeling.
# Supportive care
Screen and monitor for occult/flaring Kaposi sarcoma; continue KS-directed therapy ~3 months post last rituximab.
Hepatitis B screening before rituximab - reactivation risk with immunotherapy.
For HIV-1-positive patients, continue/optimize antiretroviral therapy.
Rituximab-based therapy is time-limited: observe after response and retreat at progression.
# Monitoring
Inflammatory markers and disease response - MCD flare
Kaposi sarcoma surveillance (skin, pulmonary, GI) - flare after rituximab
Hepatitis B viral status - reactivation
CBC and organ function
Clearance checks: HHV8-positive confirmed; hepatitis B status; Kaposi sarcoma baseline screen; CBC/platelets.
# Dose modification rules
Kaposi sarcoma flare, Continue/initiate KS-directed therapy; KS may flare after rituximab. Clinical review required.
hepatitis B reactivation risk
prophylax/monitor
Antiviral prophylaxis and monitoring per hepatitis B status before and during rituximab. Clinical review required.
# Drugs
Rituximab
IV
Represented as days 1, 8, 15, 22 of a 28-day course (4 weekly doses). Rituximab-based time-limited therapy: observe and retreat at progression. +/- prednisone may be used for compressive symptoms. KS CAUTION: occult KS may flare after rituximab; continue KS-directed therapy for ~3 months after the last rituximab dose given rituximab's long action. Screen hepatitis B before rituximab.
