## Hand Fractures: Bennett, Rolando, Scaphoid and Gamekeeper Thumb
Citations and Chapter Sources: Plastic Surgery 5E Vol 6 Ch 5 (Fractures and dislocations of the hand and wrist)

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Bennett fracture is an intra-articular two-piece fracture-subluxation of the first metacarpal base, where abductor pollicis longus APL subluxates the shaft dorsally while the anterior oblique ligament holds the volar beak.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: two
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Bennett fracture, first metacarpal, abductor pollicis longus

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Rolando fracture is a comminuted T-shaped or Y-shaped intra-articular fracture of the first metacarpal base, requiring open reduction and internal fixation or external fixation.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Rolando fracture, T-shaped, first metacarpal

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Boxer fracture is a fracture of the fifth metacarpal neck with volar apex angulation, tolerating up to 40 to 50 degrees of volar angulation due to CMC joint mobility.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 40, 50
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Boxer fracture, fifth metacarpal, volar angulation

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Scaphoid fracture is the most common carpal bone fracture 60% to 70%, with waist fractures at highest risk for avascular necrosis due to retrograde blood supply from radial artery.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 60%, 70%
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: scaphoid fracture, avascular necrosis, retrograde blood supply

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Snuffbox tenderness on physical examination mandates scaphoid series radiographs and prompt thumb spica splinting even when initial plain X-rays appear normal.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: snuffbox tenderness, thumb spica splint

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Gamekeeper or skier thumb is an acute or chronic rupture of the ulnar collateral ligament UCL of the thumb MCP joint.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Gamekeeper thumb, ulnar collateral ligament, UCL

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Stener lesion occurs when the torn UCL retracts superficial to the adductor pollicis aponeurosis, preventing ligamentous healing and requiring prompt surgical repair.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Stener lesion, adductor pollicis

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: PIP joint dorsal dislocation is the most common hand dislocation, treated by closed reduction and active extension block splinting to protect the disrupted volar plate.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: PIP joint dislocation, extension block splinting, volar plate
