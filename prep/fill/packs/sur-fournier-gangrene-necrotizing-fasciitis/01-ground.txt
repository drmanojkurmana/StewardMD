Fournier gangrene is rapidly progressive polymicrobial necrotizing fasciitis of perineal, perianal, and genital planes with aerobic plus anaerobic synergy, suspected with pain beyond skin signs, crepitus, and foul odour. Endarteritis obliterans from toxins drives subcutaneous thrombosis with necrosis racing at 2 cm to 3 cm per hour, so edges are marked with time stamps and re-examined within hours.

Testicular sparing holds because testicular arteries arise from the aorta apart from perineal fascial vessels; exposed gonads rest in thigh pouches for later scrotal reconstruction with Doppler viability checks. Radical surgical debridement to viable bleeding margins with generous dusky borders and daily re-looks is the top survival determinant. FGSI sums 9 parameters (temperature, heart rate, breathing, sodium, potassium, creatinine, haematocrit, leukocytes, bicarbonate) and scores above 9 predict over 75 percent mortality with intensive escalation.

Empiric piperacillin-tazobactam plus vancomycin covers MRSA, coliforms, Pseudomonas, and anaerobes, adding clindamycin for toxin inhibition before narrowing on 48-hour cultures. Urinary diversion by suprapubic or urethral catheter with fecal diversion by colostomy or faecal system keeps wounds clean for later reversal. Urosepsis resuscitation gives 30 mL per kg crystalloids within 3 hours with antibiotics inside 1 hour plus urgent source control, tracking lactate and urine output.

Diabetes with immunosuppression and alcoholism predisposes most cases, so glucose control runs alongside resuscitation. Hyperbaric oxygen is debated as an adjunct where chambers exist, never delaying debridement. Vacuum dressings with staged skin grafting close granulating wounds after infection clears. Nutritional support with protein repletion fuels healing across repeated theatre visits. Tetanus prophylaxis with analgesia and antiemetics completes supportive care. Stoma nursing with wound mapping photographs coordinates prolonged admissions. Scrotal ultrasound with Doppler flow separates cellulitis from early fascial spread. Intensive care with vasopressor support stabilizes septic presenters. Colostomy reversal with stoma closure follows wound healing by months. Skin graft meshing with negative pressure dressings closes granulating perineums.
