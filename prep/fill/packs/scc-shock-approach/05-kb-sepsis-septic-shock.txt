# Sepsis and septic shock: initial management
Adults
Emergency department, ward, ICU
Early recognition, cultures, antimicrobials within 1 hour for suspected septic shock or probable sepsis, 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypoperfusion, noradrenaline to a MAP of 65 mmHg, and urgent source control.
Aliases: septic shock; SSC 2026; SSC 2021; hour-1 bundle; sepsis bundle.
# Recognise
Sepsis: life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection (acute SOFA score rise of 2 or more points).
Septic shock: sepsis needing vasopressors to keep MAP at or above 65 mmHg AND lactate above 2 mmol/L despite adequate fluid resuscitation.
Do not use qSOFA alone as a screening tool; it is specific but not sensitive. Use it with NEWS2, SIRS criteria and clinical judgement.
Hypoperfusion clues: hypotension, lactate 4 mmol/L or more, prolonged capillary refill, mottling, reduced urine output, altered mentation.
