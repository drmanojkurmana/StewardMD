## Dupuytren Disease: Cords, Myofibroblasts, Xiaflex and Fasciectomy
Citations and Chapter Sources: Plastic Surgery 5E Vol 6 Ch 19 (Dupuytren disease)

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Dupuytren disease is a fibroproliferative disorder of the palmar and digital fascia driven by myofibroblasts expressing alpha-smooth muscle actin.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Dupuytren disease, fibroproliferative, myofibroblasts

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Dupuytren diathesis markers for aggressive disease include young age under 50, bilateral involvement, positive family history, and ectopic fibromatoses Ledderhose, Peyronie, Garrod pads.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 50
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Dupuytren diathesis, Ledderhose, Peyronie, Garrod pads

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Pretendinous band forms the pretendinous cord, causing MCP joint flexion contracture; spiral band and lateral digital sheet form the spiral cord, which displaces the neurovascular bundle medially and superficially.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: pretendinous cord, spiral cord, neurovascular bundle displacement

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Four anatomical structures contribute to the pathological spiral cord: pretendinous band, spiral band, Grayson ligament, and lateral digital sheet.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: four
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: spiral cord, Grayson ligament

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Hueston tabletop test is positive when a patient cannot place the palm completely flat on a table, representing an accepted indication for surgical intervention.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Hueston tabletop test

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Surgical intervention is indicated for MCP joint flexion contracture exceeding 30 degrees or any PIP joint contracture.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 30
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: MCP joint contracture, PIP joint contracture

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Limited selective fasciectomy through Brunner zigzag or longitudinal incisions with multiple Z-plasties is the standard surgical treatment.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: limited selective fasciectomy, Brunner incision, Z-plasty

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Collagenase Clostridium histolyticum Xiaflex enzymatic fasciotomy lyses collagen cords, followed by manual finger manipulation 24 to 72 hours later to disrupt the cord.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 24, 72
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: collagenase, Xiaflex, enzymatic fasciotomy
