# Amyloidosis in rheumatology (grounding reference: Kelley & Firestein, Harrison, ACR/EULAR criteria)

Rheumatology topic srh-amyloidosis: overview of pathophysiology, diagnostic criteria, clinical features, and management guidelines.

Amyloidosis encompasses a heterogeneous group of protein-folding disorders in which soluble precursor proteins aggregate into insoluble, unbranched 7.5 to 10 nm cross-beta-sheet fibrils that deposit extracellularly in organs and tissues, causing progressive organ dysfunction.

Histopathological diagnosis requires tissue biopsy (abdominal subcutaneous fat aspirate [sensitivity 70% to 80%], minor salivary gland, rectal, or involved organ) demonstrating pathognomonic bright apple-green birefringence under cross-polarized light microscopy following Congo red staining.

AL (primary) amyloidosis is caused by a clonal plasma cell dyscrasia producing monoclonal immunoglobulin light chains (lambda in 75%, kappa in 25%); clinical hallmarks include nephrotic syndrome, restrictive cardiomyopathy with low ECG voltage, hepatomegaly, macroglossia (pathognomonic, in 15%), and periorbital ecchymoses ('raccoon eyes') caused by amyloid vascular fragility.

AA (reactive/secondary) amyloidosis is driven by chronic, sustained systemic inflammation (rheumatoid arthritis, spondyloarthritis, familial Mediterranean fever, bronchiectasis, chronic osteomyelitis) stimulating hepatic overproduction of acute-phase serum amyloid A (SAA); renal amyloid deposition causes heavy proteinuria, nephrotic syndrome, and end-stage renal disease.

Transthyretin amyloidosis (ATTR) is caused by misfolded transthyretin (TTR), occurring as wild-type ATTR (ATTRwt, formerly senile systemic amyloidosis, affecting elderly men with restrictive cardiomyopathy and carpal tunnel syndrome) or hereditary mutant ATTR (ATTRv, e.g., Val122Ile or Val30Met, causing familial amyloid polyneuropathy and cardiomyopathy).

Cardiac amyloidosis features concentric biventricular wall thickening with small ventricular cavities, severe diastolic dysfunction with restrictive filling pattern, low QRS voltage on ECG despite thickened myocardium on echocardiogram (pathognomonic voltage-to-mass discordance), and apical sparing of longitudinal strain ('cherry on top' pattern).

Bone scintigraphy using 99mTc-pyrophosphate (99mTc-PYP) or 99mTc-DPD demonstrates intense myocardial tracer uptake (Perugini grade 2 or 3) in ATTR cardiac amyloidosis, enabling non-biopsy diagnosis of ATTR cardiomyopathy when monoclonal free light chains are excluded.

Disease-modifying therapies target precursor proteins: AL amyloidosis is treated with anti-plasma cell regimens (daratumumab, bortezomib, cyclophosphamide, dexamethasone [Dara-CyBorD]); AA amyloidosis requires aggressive suppression of underlying inflammation; and ATTR is treated with the oral TTR kinetic stabilizer tafamidis (61 mg daily) or TTR gene-silencing siRNA/antisense oligonucleotides (patisiran, vutrisiran, inotersen).
