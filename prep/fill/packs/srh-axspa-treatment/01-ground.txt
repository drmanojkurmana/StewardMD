Axial SpA management sheet. Figures referenced: 2, 3, 2.1, 4. NSAID trials count: 2 drugs across 4 weeks. Agents referenced: methotrexate, sulfasalazine, leflunomide, infliximab, adalimumab, golimumab, certolizumab, etanercept, secukinumab, ixekizumab, bimekizumab, tofacitinib, upadacitinib.

Full dose oral anti-inflammatories open axial disease control, easing pain and morning rigidity swiftly. Skimped doses merely mask while spine stiffens.

Pill DMARDs (methotrexate, sulfasalazine, leflunomide) miss pure spinal inflammation entirely; sacroiliac or vertebral disease alone never merits them. Limbs differ from spine here.

Sulfasalazine two to three grams daily earns conditional use solely when peripheral joints dominate or bowel inflammation rides along. Isolated spine excludes it.

Stubborn activity (ASDAS at or past two point one, BASDAI at or past four) after pair of NSAID trials across four weeks unlocks biologic or targeted synthetic escalation. Two drugs plus high scores open the gate.

First biologics split evenly: TNF blockers (infliximab, adalimumab, golimumab, certolizumab monoclonals plus etanercept receptor) versus interleukin seventeen A blockers secukinumab and ixekizumab. Equal benefit, differing baggage.

Eye or bowel comorbidity tilts to monoclonal TNF antibodies infliximab, adalimumab, certolizumab; etanercept plus interleukin seventeen blockade lose there. Uveitis and colitis vote monoclonal.

After TNF failure, dual interleukin seventeen A and F blockade bimekizumab plus oral JAK pills tofacitinib and upadacitinib stand approved. New lanes after old roadblocks.

Supervised extension plus chest expansion physio preserves mobility and posture; kyphosis creeps where motion stops. Pills calm fire, movement keeps shape. Smoking cessation plus posture schooling plus hydrotherapy access widen benefit; depression screening catches hidden disability drivers.

Extra anchors: night pain easing with movement separates inflammatory backache from mechanical ache. Alternating buttock pain, dactylitis fingers, and heel enthesitis complete early pictures. HLA-B27 testing supports but never seals diagnosis. MRI sacroiliac edema confirms early disease before plain films change; radiographic sacroiliitis grades progression later.

NSAID is the workhorse abbreviation throughout: trials count two NSAIDs, escalation follows NSAID failure, first-line stays NSAID.
