Rule of Nines carves body surface into nine percent head and neck, nine percent each upper limb, eighteen percent front trunk, eighteen percent back trunk, eighteen percent each lower limb, and one percent perineum. Nines map every region for rapid bedside estimation without charts.

Lund and Browder redraws the map for small children, granting bigger head shares and smaller leg shares to match paediatric proportions. Children wear bigger heads on paper than any adult formula allows.

Parkland resuscitation orders four millilitres of lactated Ringer per kilo per percent second-plus-third-degree burn across the first twenty four hours. Four mils per kilo per percent fills the tank.

Half the Parkland total runs in the first eight hours after injury, the rest across the next sixteen hours, front-loading the leak phase. Half fast, half slow.

Kidney targets steer fluids: hourly urine of zero point five millilitres per kilo in adults and one point zero millilitres per kilo in children under thirty kilos proves perfusion. Half a mil per kilo in grown-ups, one mil per kilo in small children.

Jackson rings the burn in three zones: dead central coagulation, struggling salvageable stasis, and outer reactive hyperemia. Three rings from dead to red.

Smoke kills more than skin: inhalation injury multiplies mortality and demands early proactive intubation when faces burn, sputum soils, or stridor sings. Soot with stridor means tube now.

Carbon monoxide hijacks haemoglobin, dragging the oxyhaemoglobin curve left and starving tissues, yielding only to one hundred percent normobaric, or hyperbaric chamber, oxygen. Left-shifted blood needs pure oxygen.

Tourniquet skin strangles limbs and chests: circumferential full-thickness burns demand emergency escharotomy within twelve hours to free compartments and let chests rise. Cut leathery rings before compartments close.
