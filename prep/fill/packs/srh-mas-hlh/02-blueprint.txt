# Macrophage activation syndrome and haemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis (blueprint grounding: Kelley and Firestein, Harrison; HLH-2004, HScore, MH score)
MAS is life-threatening hyperinflammation from uncontrolled macrophage and T-cell activation with hemophagocytosis in systemic JIA, adult-onset Still disease, and SLE.
Unlike active Still disease with leukocytosis and thrombocytosis, MAS shows falling counts: platelets at or below 181,000 per microliter, leukocytes at or below 4,000, and abrupt ESR fall from consumed fibrinogen.
Ferritin is the screening test: hyperferritinemia from above 10,000 up to 50,000 ng per mL is highly specific, with 2016 Ravelli criteria using ferritin above 684 ng per mL plus any 2 of platelet 181,000 or below, AST above 48 units per L, triglycerides above 156 mg per dL, fibrinogen 360 mg per dL or below.
HLH-2004 needs 5 of 8: fever, splenomegaly, bicytopenia, hypertriglyceridemia or hypofibrinogenemia, hemophagocytosis, low or absent NK activity, ferritin 500 ng per mL or higher, and sCD25 2,400 units per mL or higher.
HScore sums 9 variables (immunosuppression, fever, organomegaly, triglycerides, ferritin, AST, fibrinogen, cytopenias, marrow hemophagocytosis) with probability above 90 percent at 169 points or more.
Primary HLH is genetic cytotoxicity failure (PRF1, UNC13D, STXBP2, XIAP) in infants, while secondary HLH follows EBV, CMV, leishmania, lymphoma (T and NK cell), or autoimmune disease.
Pulse methylprednisolone 30 mg per kg daily with anakinra 2 mg to 10 mg per kg daily (IL-1 blockade) controls MAS rapidly; HLH-94 and HLH-2004 use dexamethasone with etoposide, adding intrathecal methotrexate for CNS disease.
EBV-HLH needs rituximab to delete infected B cells; malignancy HLH needs disease-directed chemotherapy; primary HLH cures only with allogeneic transplant.
Emapalumab (anti-interferon-gamma) rescues refractory primary HLH with 63 percent response; MAS in Still disease responds to IL-1 and IL-6 blockade without etoposide in most cases.
CNS HLH gives seizures, meningismus, and white-matter changes with CSF pleocytosis; intrathecal methotrexate with hydrocortisone pairs with systemic dexamethasone and etoposide.
Neonatal HLH from perforin defects presents within weeks of birth with fever, hepatosplenomegaly, and coagulopathy; family history of infant deaths plus consanguinity triggers urgent genetics with bridge therapy.
