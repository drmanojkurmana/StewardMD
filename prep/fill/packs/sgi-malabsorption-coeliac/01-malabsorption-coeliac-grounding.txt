# Malabsorption and coeliac disease
# Source: StewardMD gastroenterology knowledge base / Sleisenger 11e Ch. 106, 107

## Serological diagnosis and immunogenetics
Coeliac disease serologic diagnosis requires initial IgA anti-tissue transglutaminase (anti-tTG IgA) paired with total serum IgA to rule out selective IgA deficiency, with IgG-deamidated gliadin peptide (DGP IgG) used in selective IgA deficiency.
Human leukocyte antigen testing reveals that > 99% of coeliac disease patients carry either HLA-DQ2 (specifically DQ2.5 or DQ2.2 in 90% of cases) or HLA-DQ8; the absence of both alleles possesses an exceptionally high negative predictive value (> 99%).

## Histopathology and cutaneous disease
Modified Marsh-Oberhuber classification grades duodenal enteropathy: Marsh 0 (pre-infiltrative stage), Marsh 1 (intraepithelial lymphocytosis > 25 per 100 enterocytes), Marsh 2 (crypt hyperplasia), Marsh 3a-c (partial, subtotal, and total villous atrophy).
Dermatitis herpetiformis is a pathognomonic cutaneous manifestation of coeliac disease featuring intensely pruritic grouped papulovesicular eruptions on extensor surfaces with granular IgA deposition in dermal papillae, responding to dapsone and a gluten-free diet.

## Refractory disease and malignancy
Refractory coeliac disease (RCD) is defined by persistent malabsorptive symptoms and villous atrophy despite 12 months on a strict gluten-free diet; Type 2 RCD is characterised by clonal aberrant intraepithelial T cells (loss of surface CD3/CD8), carrying an EATL risk of up to 50%, treated with cladribine or stem cell transplantation.
Enteropathy-associated T-cell lymphoma (EATL) is an aggressive intestinal non-Hodgkin lymphoma arising directly from Type 2 refractory coeliac disease, carrying high perforation and mortality rates.

## Small bowel mucosal function and bile acid diarrhoea
D-xylose absorption test evaluates small intestinal mucosal absorptive integrity: normal 5-hour urinary excretion of >= 4 g following a 25 g oral dose indicates intact mucosal transport, differentiating mucosal malabsorption from pancreatic insufficiency where fecal elastase is < 200 ug/g.
Bile acid diarrhoea (type 1 ileal dysfunction, type 2 idiopathic, type 3 post-cholecystectomy) is diagnosed by 7-day 75SeHCAT retention < 10% (severe < 5%, normal > 15%) or serum 7-alpha-hydroxy-4-cholesten-3-one (C4), treated with colestyramine.

## Additional clinical context from Sleisenger
Duodenal biopsies should be obtained from both bulb and second portion of the duodenum.
Patients must continue dietary gluten before testing to avoid false negative histology.
Bile acid diarrhoea occurs when excess dihydroxy bile acids stimulate colonic secretomotor neurons, promoting secretory diarrhoea.
