Sex development disorders are grouped by chromosomes. DSD classification divides them into 46,XX DSD, 46,XY DSD, and sex chromosome DSD such as 45,X/46,XY mixed gonadal dysgenesis. The karyotype frames every subsequent decision, because management of an ovary-bearing 46,XX child differs completely from a dysgenetic 46,XY gonad with cancer risk.

The commonest neonatal cause is adrenal. Congenital adrenal hyperplasia from 21-hydroxylase deficiency, CYP21A2 mutation, is the leading cause of ambiguous genitalia in newborns, classified as 46,XX DSD with severe salt wasting. Cortisol failure drives ACTH excess and androgen overproduction that virilizes a genetic female, while aldosterone failure wastes salt and threatens collapse within weeks.

Virilization is graded, not guessed. Prader staging from Stage I to Stage V quantifies virilization in 46,XX CAH girls, from mild clitoromegaly at Stage I to a fully virilized phallic urethra with fused scrotum at Stage V. The stage guides surgical planning and counseling, with higher stages needing more extensive reconstruction.

Androgen resistance creates a female phenotype from male chromosomes. Complete androgen insensitivity syndrome, a 46,XY DSD from androgen receptor gene mutations, presents with female external genitalia, a blind vaginal pouch, absent uterus, and abdominal or inguinal testes. The testes make testosterone and anti-Mullerian hormone normally, but no tissue can respond, so Wolffian structures regress and the child is raised female.

A second 46,XY condition virilizes at puberty. 5-alpha reductase deficiency from SRD5A2 mutation impairs conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone, presenting as pseudovaginal perineoscrotal hypospadias at birth with marked virilization and phallic growth at puberty as rising testosterone overwhelms the block. Gender assignment must anticipate this switch, because children raised female may virilize dramatically.

Y-bearing dysgenetic gonads must come out. Malignancy risk peaks in intra-abdominal dysgenetic gonads carrying Y-chromosomal GBY material, where gonadoblastoma develops, so early prophylactic gonadectomy is mandated. The rule is anatomic and genetic together: streak or dysgenetic tissue plus Y material equals removal, while confirmed ovaries stay.

The confluence is mobilized as one unit. Total or partial urogenital mobilization brings the joined urethra and vagina to the perineum as a single unit, avoiding separate vaginal flap dissection. One mobilized channel replaces two dissections, cutting fistula risk and preserving blood supply to both passages in a single well-vascularized block.

Sensation is preserved by sparing nerves. Nerve-sparing clitoroplasty dissects the dorsal neurovascular bundle off the erectile bodies, recessing or reducing glans volume while the bundle keeps clitoral sensation. Erectile tissue is debulked but the nerve supply is never divided, so adult sexual function survives the cosmetic reduction.
