# Anticholinergic toxidrome
Anticholinergic toxidrome, Multisystem / Toxicology, Emergency medicine / Toxicology.
Aliases: antimuscarinic toxidrome; anticholinergic syndrome.
# Pathophysiology
The anticholinergic toxidrome results from blockade of muscarinic acetylcholine receptors, both centrally and peripherally. Central blockade produces agitation, delirium and hallucinations, while peripheral blockade causes mydriasis, tachycardia, reduced sweating with dry flushed skin, urinary retention and decreased gut motility. Impaired sweating undermines heat loss and predisposes to dangerous hyperthermia, and the constellation reflects loss of normal parasympathetic muscarinic tone.
