Acute uncomplicated pyelonephritis is driven by uropathogenic Escherichia coli, E. coli, in over 80%, announced by fever, flank pain, costovertebral angle tenderness, and pyuria, with urine culture sent before antibiotics so resistance guides the switch. Emphysematous pyelonephritis, EPN, is a necrotising gas-producing infection striking diabetics in over 90%, where E. coli and Klebsiella ferment glucose into tissue gas; crepitus or stormy diabetic sepsis means emergency drainage.

The Huang-Tseng classification stages EPN on CT from Class 1 gas in the collecting system, through Class 2 parenchymal gas, to Class 3A perirenal and Class 3B pararenal spread, ending at Class 4 bilateral or solitary kidney disease, summarised as EPN Class 1 to 4, with spread dictating urgency. Care has swung from instant nephrectomy to percutaneous drainage, PCD tubes under CT guidance, plus broad antibiotics, an EPN nephrectomy avoidance strategy preserving parenchyma in over 80%.

Xanthogranulomatous pyelonephritis, XPN, is a chronic destructive granulomatous attack by lipid laden foamy macrophages, usually around a staghorn calculus, mimicking tumour so histology must precede radical surgery. Its bear paw sign on contrast CT, rounded low density calices ringed by thinned parenchyma, defines the XPN CT appearance against tumour or hydronephrosis. Renal carbuncle seeds by hematogenous seeding when Staphylococcus aureus, S. aureus, travels from a distant skin boil, taking targeted antibiotics or percutaneous drainage past 3 cm, with the skin source sought and cleared. Obstructive pyelonephritis with hydronephrosis behind a calculus is an emergency for immediate emergency decompression by retrograde ureteral stent or percutaneous nephrostomy, never antibiotics alone, with definitive stone treatment once sepsis settles. Diabetics with poor glycaemic control merit a lower threshold for CT imaging, since gas forms fastest at high tissue glucose. Pregnancy with asymptomatic bacteriuria is screened and treated early, because untreated colonisation ascends to pyelonephritis with real frequency. Severe renal infections reward drainage before delay.
