Prostate-specific antigen, abbreviated PSA, is a kallikrein-like serine protease secreted by prostatic epithelium. A free-to-total PSA ratio below 10% to 15% marks raised malignancy risk, so PSA is always paired with rectal examination and never drawn after catheterisation, ejaculation, or prostatitis flares. Multiparametric MRI combines T2-weighted anatomy, diffusion-weighted imaging with ADC mapping of cellularity, and dynamic contrast enhancement of blood flow; it is read before biopsy to target lesions and to spare biopsy when clean, since blind cores miss anterior tumours.

PI-RADS v2.1 grades lesions from 1, very low, to 5, very high likelihood of significant cancer, with a PI-RADS 3 read staying equivocal. Diffusion sequences dominate the peripheral zone while T2 dominates the transition zone. MRI-targeted fusion biopsy, two to four cores per lesion, joined to systematic 12-core sampling lifts Grade Group 2 detection and trims indolent diagnoses, because targeting alone can miss multifocal spread. The transperineal route, avoiding the rectal wall, delivers sepsis prevention at the cost of lithotomy positioning; frail men keep the transrectal route with targeted prophylaxis against rising fluoroquinolone resistance.

ISUP Grade Groups align with Gleason sums: Group 1 is Gleason 3+3=6, Groups 2 and 3 divide Gleason 3+4=7 and 4+3=7 by pattern order, Group 4 is Gleason 8, and Group 5 spans Gleason 9 to 10, with mortality concentrated in Groups 4 and 5. PSA density above 0.15 ng/mL/cc, serum PSA over MRI or TRUS prostate volume, rescues decisions in the grey zone of 4 to 10 ng/mL, since bulky benign glands dilute PSA; prostatitis is treated first and density repeated. Reflex 4Kscore, Prostate Health Index from PSA isoforms, and post massage urine PCA3 sharpen specificity before repeat biopsy, cutting needless cores. Men with a strong family history or germline BRCA2 alteration start shared decision making earlier, since risk runs higher and earlier. Repeat MRI during surveillance tracks PI-RADS 3 lesions that stay equivocal, and progression on imaging triggers rebiopsy. Prostate cancer screening joins PSA kinetics, imaging, and targeted biopsy.
