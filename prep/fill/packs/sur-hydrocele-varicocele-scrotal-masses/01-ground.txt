Testicular torsion strikes through a bell-clapper deformity, a high tunica vaginalis insertion that lets the cord twist in intravaginal rotation; venous outflow blocks first, so pain and swelling explode within minutes. The golden window for testicular salvage is 6 hours with rescue above 90%, crashing under 10% after 24 hours, so no scrotal pain ever waits for morning. Manual detorsion turns open-book fashion, right testis counter-clockwise and left clockwise from the feet, while theatre is prepared, followed always by bilateral orchidopexy regardless of viability, since residual twist persists.

Twisted appendix testis, the hydatid of Morgagni on a stalk, shows the blue dot sign through upper scrotal skin and settles with rest unless doubt remains. Acute epididymo-orchitis instead eases with elevation, a positive Prehn sign, with hypervascularity and low resistive index on colour Doppler, and takes antibiotics once flow is confirmed. Under 35 years epididymitis is usually Chlamydia trachomatis with gonococcus on urethral swab, over 35 years coliform gut organisms led by Escherichia coli, E. coli, on midstream culture, so swab the young and culture the older.

Hydrocele pools serous fluid between tunica vaginalis layers and is cured by Jaboulay eversion, sac turned inside out, or Lord plication gathering thin sacs; transillumination is confirmed and tumour excluded first, since malignant hydrocele must never be stitched blindly. Communicating hydroceles of infancy that fill and empty through a patent processus vaginalis are repaired through the groin like hernias, not through the scrotum. Varicocele repair is reserved for infertility, pain, or adolescent growth arrest, diagnosed by bag of worms impulse on standing. Spermatocele is a benign retention cyst of the efferent ductules or epididymis holding dead, non viable spermatozoa apart from the testis, removed by spermatocelectomy only for pain or doubt, with the rest reassured. Acute scrotum care separates twist, nubbin, and infection within minutes.
