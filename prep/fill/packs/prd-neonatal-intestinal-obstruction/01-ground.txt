Necrotizing enterocolitis shows pneumatosis intestinalis, intramural linear or bubbly gas in the bowel wall, as the diagnostic hallmark at Bell stage IIA, progressing to stage III with perforation. Portal venous gas forms branching lucencies reaching within 2 cm of the hepatic capsule from centrifugal flow, unlike central pneumobilia. A persistent fixed dilated bowel loop unchanged over 24 to 36 hours signals full thickness ischaemia. Left lateral decubitus and cross-table lateral views collect free air over the liver surface. Massive pneumoperitoneum gives the football sign and Rigler double wall sign. Colour Doppler ultrasound showing absent bowel wall perfusion predicts transmural infarction. Meconium ileus gives the Neuhauser soap-bubble appearance in the right iliac fossa with microcolon on contrast enema and links to cystic fibrosis, whereas meconium plug syndrome shows transient rectosigmoid plugs that clear at enema. Post-NEC ischaemic strictures develop in 15 to 30 percent of survivors, mainly at the splenic flexure and sigmoid colon, and need contrast study before stoma closure or resection planning in growing infants.
 Duodenal atresia shows the double bubble sign with no distal gas and associates with Down syndrome, while annular pancreas and duodenal web give partial obstruction with some distal air. Malrotation with midgut volvulus shows the whirlpool sign on ultrasound, duodenojejunal junction displaced from the midline, corkscrew duodenum on contrast study and risk of total midgut necrosis within hours. Hypertrophic pyloric stenosis measures muscle thickness over 4 mm and channel length over 16 mm on ultrasound with the target and cervix signs. Intussusception shows a target or pseudokidney mass, led by lymphoid hyperplasia or a Meckel diverticulum, and reduces with air enema unless peritonitis or perforation is present. Anorectal malformations use Krickenbeck classification, invertogram or prone cross-table films, distal colostography for fistula level, and VACTERL screening of spine, heart, kidneys and limbs before reconstruction surgery.
 High-frequency linear transducers at 12 to 18 MHz show bowel wall thinning under 1 mm or thickening over 2.5 mm.
