Exam notes for heart disease in pregnancy and for cardio oncology, written for grounding, not for display.
The modified World Health Organization scheme, called the mWHO risk classification, grades maternal cardiovascular risk in pregnancy. Class 1, also written mWHO class I, is low risk, with a maternal cardiovascular event rate of about 2.5% to 5%. Class 4 is the mWHO Class IV group. Risk of death or grave maternal morbidity sits at 40% to 100%. Conception is advised against.
mWHO Class IV conditions include severe pulmonary arterial hypertension of any cause, severe systemic ventricular dysfunction with LVEF under 30% or NYHA class 3 to 4 symptoms, critical native mitral stenosis, symptomatic severe aortic stenosis, and vascular Ehlers Danlos.
Mechanical prosthetic heart valves need anticoagulation throughout pregnancy. If the warfarin dose that holds the INR in range is 5 mg daily or less, many programmes continue warfarin in the first trimester because the reported embryopathy rate is under 3%. An alternative is dose adjusted low molecular weight heparin with anti-Xa monitoring. The peak anti-Xa target used for mechanical valves is 0.8 to 1.2 IU/mL.
Peripartum cardiomyopathy is idiopathic heart failure with LVEF under 45%, presenting toward the end of pregnancy or in the months after delivery, once other causes are excluded. A simplified path asks whether heart failure is present in late pregnancy or postpartum, whether ejection fraction is under 45%, and whether pre eclampsia and myocarditis have been set aside. A proposed mechanism is cleavage of prolactin to a 16 kDa fragment that injures myocardial capillaries. Bromocriptine is a dopamine D2 agonist that suppresses prolactin and is used in some recovery protocols, not as a drug for every patient. The dilated ventricle can hold apical thrombus.
Anthracycline cardiotoxicity, typified by doxorubicin, is cumulative and largely irreversible type 1 injury. Topoisomerase II beta poisoning and oxidative stress are the mechanism. Clinical heart failure becomes much more common once the lifetime doxorubicin dose passes 400 to 550 mg per m2.
Dexrazoxane chelates intracellular iron and also inhibits topoisomerase II. It is the cardioprotectant used when a high anthracycline total cannot be avoided.
Trastuzumab cardiotoxicity is type 2 myocardial dysfunction. It does not depend on the cumulative dose in the way doxorubicin does, it usually lacks myocyte dropout, and the ejection fraction often returns after the drug is paused.
Cancer therapy related cardiac dysfunction, a form of cardiac dysfunction, is flagged by a relative fall in global longitudinal strain of more than 15% from the patient's own baseline, or by an absolute fall in LVEF of at least 10 percentage points to a value under 50%.
Drug names: warfarin heparin bromocriptine doxorubicin dexrazoxane trastuzumab.
