# Yao syndrome
Yao syndrome, Immunology / Autoinflammatory, Clinical Immunology / Rheumatology.
Aliases: NOD2-associated autoinflammatory disease; formerly NOD2-associated autoinflammatory syndrome; NOD2; nucleotide-binding oligomerisation domain 2 disorder.
# Pathophysiology
Yao syndrome is linked to variants in NOD2, an intracellular sensor of bacterial cell-wall components that regulates inflammatory signalling. Unlike the specific NOD2 mutations causing Blau syndrome, the variants in Yao syndrome are thought to dysregulate innate immune signalling and cytokine production, producing periodic systemic inflammation. This drives recurrent fevers, dermatitis, arthralgia, and gastrointestinal symptoms without the granulomatous triad of Blau syndrome. The condition is regarded as a polygenically influenced autoinflammatory disease rather than a simple single-gene disorder, which contributes to its variable presentation and treatment response.
