Grading starts with proliferation. Grading by Ki-67 under 3 percent for G1, 3 to 20 percent for G2 and over 20 percent for G3 with differentiation decides surgery against systemic therapy.

Insulinoma declares itself in fasting. Insulinoma shows Whipple triad with glucose under 55 mg per dL, insulin over 3 microunits per mL and C-peptide over 0.6 ng per mL during a supervised 72-hour fast with symptoms relieved by glucose.

Gastrinomas hide in a triangle. Gastrinoma triangle spans cystic-duct junction, duodenal sweep and pancreatic neck, with secretin stimulation rise over 120 pg per mL confirming Zollinger-Ellison with refractory ulcers and diarrhoea.

MEN1 multiplies tumours. MEN1 with menin mutation needs surveillance from age 5 for parathyroid, pituitary and pancreatic tumours, with duodenotomy for multiple duodenal gastrinomas that imaging misses.

Small tumours shell out. Small nonfunctional tumours under 2 cm in the body-tail get enucleation or spleen-preserving distal pancreatectomy, while head lesions need Whipple with nodal clearance.

Liver spread debulks. Liver metastases get parenchyma-sparing debulking when over 90 percent of disease is resectable, combined with octreotide and peptide receptor radionuclide therapy for residual somatostatin-avid disease.

Scanning follows receptors. Gallium-68 DOTATATE PET stages somatostatin-avid disease and selects PRRT candidates, while FDG avidity marks aggressive G3 tumours needing chemotherapy rather than surgery.

Syndromes localise the operation. Glucagonoma with necrolytic migratory erythema, VIPoma with watery diarrhoea and potassium loss, and somatostatinoma with gallstones and diabetes localise to body-tail resection with peripancreatic nodal clearance.
