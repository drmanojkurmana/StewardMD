Trial verdict sheet. Figures referenced: 50, 1, 375, 2, 4, 6, 704, 30, 26, 52, 10, 43, 32, 104, 150, 44, 6128. Agents referenced: methotrexate, infliximab, glucocorticoids, tofacitinib, rituximab, cyclophosphamide, avacopan, prednisone, belimumab, mycophenolate mofetil, nintedanib, allopurinol, febuxostat.

BeSt tested four early rheumatoid strategies: upfront combination pills with tapering steroids or methotrexate plus infliximab beat sequential monotherapy with faster function and frozen X-ray damage. Early combinations freeze joints.

ORAL Surveillance randomized older rheumatoid patients (fifty plus with at least one cardiac risk) to tofacitinib or TNF blockers: tofacitinib flunked non-inferiority with higher heart and cancer hazard ratios, rewriting labels worldwide. Failed non-inferiority rewrote labels.

RAVE pitted rituximab (three seventy five milligrams per meter squared weekly for four doses) against daily oral cyclophosphamide for severe vasculitis induction: equal at six months, rituximab winning clearly in relapsing disease. B-cell depletion equals alkylators except in relapse, where it wins.

PEXIVAS, the largest vasculitis trial at seven hundred four patients, killed routine plasma exchange: no mortality or dialysis benefit in severe renal or bleeding disease, while halved steroids matched full dose with fewer infections. Exchange exited; less steroid sufficed.

ADVOCATE proved avacopan (thirty milligrams twice daily) non-inferior to prednisone taper at twenty six weeks and superior for sustained remission at fifty two weeks, healing kidneys and filtration without steroid wreckage. Complement pills beat steroid tapers.

BLISS-LN added intravenous belimumab (ten milligrams per kilo) to mycophenolate or Euro-Lupus cyclophosphamide backbones: renal response forty three versus thirty two percent at week one hundred four, halving renal events and deaths. Added BAFF blockade doubled renal wins.

SENSCIS proved nintedanib (one hundred fifty milligrams twice daily) slowed yearly lung capacity loss by forty four percent versus placebo in scleroderma lung disease, earning approvals. Kinase pills brake lung decline nearly halfway.

Gout safety split: CARES flagged higher all-cause and cardiac death on febuxostat versus allopurinol, but the bigger prospective European FAST trial (six thousand one hundred twenty eight patients) cleared febuxostat as non-inferior for cardiac death, major events, and all-cause mortality. Small alarm, big reassurance.
