Erection starts when parasympathetic non-adrenergic non-cholinergic, NANC, fibres release nitric oxide onto cavernosal smooth muscle. Nitric oxide activates guanylate cyclase, which synthesises cyclic GMP, cGMP, the second messenger commanding relaxation; phosphodiesterase type 5 terminates the signal by hydrolysing cGMP, a balance every therapy manipulates.

Rising cGMP dephosphorylates myosin light chains, relaxing trabecular smooth muscle so arterial inflow engorges the sinusoids. Expanding tissue compresses the subtunical emissary veins against the unyielding tunica albuginea, and this veno-occlusive mechanism converts flow into rigidity; failure at either limb yields arterial insufficiency or venous leak.

PDE-5 inhibitors, sildenafil 50-100 mg and tadalafil 10-20 mg, preserve cGMP by blocking its breakdown, amplifying physiologic arousal. Concomitant nitrates exemplified by nitroglycerin donate parallel nitric oxide, and the combined cGMP surge precipitates severe hypotension, making nitrate co-administration an absolute contraindication screened at every prescription.

Intracavernosal injection therapy bypasses neural input: alprostadil, synthetic PGE1, with papaverine and the alpha blocker phentolamine as Trimix, relaxes trabecular muscle directly. As second-line medical therapy it succeeds in over 85% of oral failures, administered at home after office dose titration.

The three-piece inflatable penile prosthesis, IPP, pairs dual intracorporal cylinders with a scrotal pump and a preperitoneal reservoir, restoring on-demand rigidity. Satisfaction surpasses 95% for patients and partners, the highest among erectile therapies, justifying early implantation in injection failures.

Prosthesis infection afflicts 1% to 2% of primary implants in non-diabetics, higher after revisions, with Staphylococcus epidermidis and gram-negative bacilli predominating. Pain, erythema, and pump fixation signal colonised hardware that systemic antibiotics cannot sterilise.

The Mulcahy salvage protocol answers infection with immediate device explantation, sequential antiseptic and antibiotic washout of all compartments, and immediate reimplantation of sterile components, preserving corporal length and avoiding the fibrosis of delayed reoperation.

Vascular stratification uses penile duplex ultrasound after pharmacologic stimulation: peak systolic velocity, PSV, below 25 cm/s marks arterial insufficiency, while end-diastolic velocity, EDV, above 5 cm/s with resistive index under 0.8 marks veno-occlusive dysfunction, directing arterial versus venous management.
