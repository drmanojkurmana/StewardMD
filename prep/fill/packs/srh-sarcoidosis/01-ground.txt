Granuloma sheet. Figures referenced: 90, 80, 7, 0, 4, 10, 20, 30, 1, 25, 125, 18, 40. Agents referenced: prednisone, methotrexate, azathioprine, infliximab.

Sarcoidosis builds non-caseating epithelioid granulomas in organ after organ for no known reason, with lungs and chest nodes hit in over ninety percent. Compact granulomas without necrosis define the disease across every involved tissue.

Lofgren syndrome bursts acutely and kindly: bilateral hilar nodes with migratory ankle arthritis and erythema nodosum, melting away on its own in over eighty to ninety percent. Ankles with nodes and shin nodules predict spontaneous cure without any drugs.

Heerfordt fever (uveoparotid fever) stays rare and specific: anterior uveitis with parotid swelling, low fever, and seventh nerve facial palsy, uni- or bilateral. Eyes with glands and drooping faces mark Heerfordt.

Scadding stages chest films from zero to four: clean films, nodes alone, nodes with infiltrates, infiltrates alone, then fibrotic honeycombing with shrunken lungs. Films march from nodes to fibrosis.

Microscopy shows tight granulomas of CD four T cells with epithelioid histiocytes; Langhans giants hide star-shaped asteroid bodies and laminated Schaumann calcifications. Helper T cells ring star-bearing giants.

Calcium runs wild in ten to twenty percent: frank hypercalcemia in ten percent and urinary calcium spill in thirty percent, driven by macrophage one-alpha-hydroxylation of twenty five hydroxy vitamin D into active one twenty five calcitriol without parathyroid permission. Granulomas brew vitamin D on their own.

Hearts die quietly: high-grade blocks, malignant ventricular rhythms, failing cardiomyopathy, and sudden death. Cardiac MRI shows patchy epicardial or mid-wall late gadolinium enhancement; eighteen-F FDG PET-CT lights active myocardial inflammation. Scars enhance while inflammation glows.

Symptomatic or organ-threatening disease (heart, brain, eye, severe calcium, progressive lungs) starts oral prednisone twenty to forty milligrams daily; refractory or maintenance care shifts to methotrexate ten to twenty milligrams weekly, azathioprine, or infliximab. Steroids lead; methotrexate sustains.
