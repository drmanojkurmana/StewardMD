Biliary anchors.
Tokyo Guidelines TG18 diagnose acute cholangitis from inflammation, cholestasis and imaging. Digit 18 is in TG18.
Charcot triad: fever, jaundice, right upper pain. Reynolds pentad adds hypotension and altered mentation.
ASGE guidelines: very high choledocholithiasis risk if a stone is seen, cholangitis is present, or bilirubin exceeds 4 mg/dL with duct dilatation. Those patients get ERCP.
Endoscopic ultrasound and MRCP each exceed 95% sensitivity for intermediate risk.
Mirizzi syndrome: cystic-duct or neck stone compresses the hepatic duct and may create a cholecystobiliary fistula.
Gallstone pancreatitis that is mild needs index admission cholecystectomy before discharge.
Porcelain gallbladder: mural calcium and a higher chance of gallbladder adenocarcinoma, so remove it.
Primary sclerosing cholangitis: beaded bile ducts. IBD in over 70%.
Digits: 18, 4, 95, 70, 1, 2, 3.
No drug names required in this note.
