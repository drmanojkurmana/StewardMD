# Herpes Simplex Virus Infection
Herpes Simplex Virus Infection, Infectious Diseases.
Aliases: HSV.
# Pathophysiology
HSV-1 and HSV-2 are neurotropic DNA herpesviruses that establish lifelong latency in sensory ganglia after primary mucocutaneous infection; periodic reactivation produces recurrent localized disease (e.g., recurrent herpes labialis with HSV-1 and herpes genitalis with HSV-2). Virus enters and replicates within the epidermis at the site of inoculation. In HSV encephalitis there is characteristic unilateral temporal-lobe involvement. The virus can disseminate, particularly in the setting of disrupted skin (eczema herpeticum) or impaired cell-mediated immunity; visceral dissemination may occur even with only localized mucocutaneous disease.
