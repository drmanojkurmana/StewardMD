# Autoantibodies and serological testing (blueprint grounding: Kelley and Firestein, Harrison; ACR EULAR criteria)
ANA screening uses indirect immunofluorescence on HEp-2 cells. A titre of 1:160 or higher is significant in the 2019 ACR EULAR SLE classification criteria, with sensitivity above 98 percent. Anti-dsDNA is over 95 percent specific for SLE, tracks lupus nephritis activity, and consumes complement. Anti-Sm targets core snRNP proteins, is 99 percent specific for SLE, and does not track flares.
Anti-Ro SSA targets Ro60 and Ro52. Anti-La SSB accompanies it. Both occur in Sjogren syndrome and subacute cutaneous lupus. Maternal anti-Ro SSA crosses the placenta and causes neonatal lupus with congenital third-degree heart block in 2 percent of exposed pregnancies. Serial fetal echocardiography is required.
Anti-CCP is 95 percent to 98 percent specific for rheumatoid arthritis, appears years before synovitis, and predicts erosive damage. Rheumatoid factor is an IgM antibody against the Fc portion of human IgG; it is less specific and occurs in hepatitis C, subacute bacterial endocarditis, and healthy elderly people.
c-ANCA shows diffuse granular cytoplasmic staining on ethanol-fixed neutrophils, targets PR3, and is present in over 85 percent of active generalized GPA. p-ANCA targets MPO and predominates in microscopic polyangiitis and eosinophilic GPA. PR3 and MPO immunoassays are required for confirmation.
In systemic sclerosis, anti-centromere marks limited cutaneous disease with pulmonary arterial hypertension. Anti-Scl-70, against topoisomerase I, marks diffuse cutaneous disease with interstitial lung disease. Anti-RNA polymerase III marks diffuse disease with scleroderma renal crisis and synchronous malignancy.
Anti-Jo-1, against histidyl-tRNA synthetase, defines the antisynthetase syndrome of myositis, interstitial lung disease, Raynaud phenomenon, non-erosive arthritis, and mechanic's hands. Anti-Mi-2 occurs in classic dermatomyositis. Anti-TIF1-gamma marks malignancy-associated myositis. In SLE, anti-ribosomal P targets P0, P1, and P2 and associates with psychosis, depression, and hepatic involvement.
