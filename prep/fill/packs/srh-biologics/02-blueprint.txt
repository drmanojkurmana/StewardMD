# Biologic and targeted synthetic DMARDs (blueprint grounding: Kelley and Firestein, Harrison; ACR and EULAR guidance)
TNF inhibitors are infliximab (chimeric), adalimumab (human), golimumab (human), certolizumab pegol (pegylated Fab without Fc), and etanercept (p75 receptor Fc fusion). Certolizumab lacks Fc and neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) binding, so placental transfer is near zero across trimesters.
Latent tuberculosis screening with IGRA or tuberculin skin test plus chest radiograph precedes any biologic or JAK inhibitor. Monoclonal anti TNF antibodies reactivate tuberculosis more than etanercept; at least 1 month of prophylaxis precedes biologic start.
Hepatitis B screening needs HBsAg, anti-HBc, and anti-HBs. Rituximab with HBsAg or anti-HBc positivity reactivates hepatitis B in above 40 percent; entecavir or tenofovir prophylaxis starts before depletion and runs at least 12 to 18 months after.
Tocilizumab and sarilumab block the IL-6 receptor (soluble and membrane-bound). IL-6 drives hepatic CRP, so CRP turns undetectable and fever plus CRP cannot be trusted for sepsis; vigilance is required for lower intestinal diverticular perforation.
Abatacept fuses CTLA-4 extracellular domain to IgG1 Fc and binds CD80 and CD86 on antigen-presenting cells more avidly than CD28, blocking the second signal of T cell activation.
Rituximab is chimeric anti CD20 for pre-B and mature B cells, sparing pro-B and plasma cells; two 1,000 mg infusions 14 days apart with methylprednisolone premedication deplete B cells for 6 to 9 months, working best in seropositive RA.
JAK inhibitors are oral small molecules: tofacitinib (JAK1 and JAK3), baricitinib (JAK1 and JAK2), upadacitinib (JAK1 selective). They block STAT phosphorylation downstream of IL-6, interferons, and gamma-chain cytokines; herpes zoster reactivation is characteristic.
Biosimilars match the reference biologic in efficacy, safety, and immunogenicity without meaningful difference, allowing indication extrapolation and cost saving.
Switching a stable patient from originator to biosimilar requires shared decision making with retention of the same dose and interval; non-medical switching registries show persistence above 80 percent at 1 year.
Combination of a biologic with methotrexate at 15 mg to 25 mg weekly reduces anti-drug antibody formation, particularly against chimeric infliximab, and improves drug survival beyond 2 years.
