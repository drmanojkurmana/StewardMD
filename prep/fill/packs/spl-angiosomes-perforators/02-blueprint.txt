## Angiosomes, Perforator Flaps and Vascular Territories
Citations and Chapter Sources: Plastic Surgery 5E Vol 1 Ch 23 (Vascular territories)

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Taylor and Palmer defined an angiosome as a three-dimensional anatomical block of tissue supplied by a specific source artery and its companion veins.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Taylor and Palmer, angiosome, source artery

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Adjacent angiosomes communicate across boundary territories through true anastomoses without caliber change, or choke vessels that exhibit caliber narrowing.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: anastomoses, choke vessels

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Perforator flaps are nourished by cutaneous perforator vessels that pierce deep fascia, dissected retrograde through muscle or septum to the source vessels.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: perforator flaps, cutaneous perforator, deep fascia

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Musculocutaneous perforators traverse muscle fibres, whereas septocutaneous perforators travel entirely within intermuscular septa.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: musculocutaneous, septocutaneous

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Anterolateral thigh ALT flap is based on perforators from the descending branch of the lateral circumflex femoral artery LCFA.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: anterolateral thigh, ALT flap, lateral circumflex femoral artery

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Deep inferior epigastric perforator DIEP flap is based on periumbilical perforators piercing the anterior rectus abdominis sheath.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: DIEP flap, deep inferior epigastric, rectus abdominis

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Preoperative handheld acoustic Doppler, CT angiography CTA, and indocyanine green ICG fluorescence angiography reliably map perforator location and caliber.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: CT angiography, indocyanine green, Doppler

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Perforator flaps preserve the underlying source muscle intact, eliminating donor site muscle morbidity, hernia risk, and functional weakness.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: donor site morbidity, muscle preservation
