Exam notes for antiarrhythmic drugs, written for grounding, not for display.
Vaughan Williams class 1 drugs block the cardiac sodium channel. Class 1a agents are quinidine and procainamide. Class 1b agents are lidocaine and mexiletine. Class 1c agents are flecainide and propafenone. Encainide sat with flecainide in the CAST trial. Class 2 means beta blockade. Class 3 drugs are potassium channel blockers: amiodarone, sotalol, dofetilide and ibutilide. Class 4 means the calcium channel blockers verapamil and diltiazem.
The CAST trial showed that flecainide and encainide raise death after myocardial infarction. Use dependence means class 1c drugs bind more when the channel opens often, so they are dangerous in structural heart disease and scar. Sodium blockade in scarred ventricle slows conduction and invites re entry.
Amiodarone shows properties of all 4 Vaughan Williams classes and is the rhythm drug most often continued in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction. Harm includes pulmonary fibrosis, thyroid disease of both underactive and overactive kinds, corneal deposits, liver injury and blue grey skin. Amiodarone induced thyrotoxicosis has two patterns: type 1 from iodine load in a nodular gland, and type 2 from destructive thyroiditis. Surveillance covers thyroid, lung, liver and eyes. Baseline and follow up electrocardiograms watch the QT interval.
Dronedarone lacks iodine, so thyroid and lung toxicity matter less than with amiodarone. ANDROMEDA enrolled unstable heart failure and mortality was higher. PALLAS enrolled permanent atrial fibrillation and also found harm. Both trials make those two groups a hard contraindication.
Sotalol combines nonselective beta blockade with class 3 block of the rapid delayed rectifier potassium current, called IKr. Reverse use dependence means the potassium block, and QT lengthening, is stronger at slow rates. Start sotalol in hospital on telemetry and follow the QTc, because torsades de pointes is the risk.
Dofetilide is a pure selective IKr blocker. The dose follows kidney function and is titrated in hospital with serial QTc checks. It may be used when the left ventricular ejection fraction is reduced.
Adenosine acts on A1 adenosine receptors at the atrioventricular node. A G protein opens the inward rectifier potassium current IKAch, the cell hyperpolarises, and atrioventricular nodal re entry stops. The half life is under 10 seconds. It will not stop ventricular tachycardia and it can be dangerous in pre excitation with atrial fibrillation.
Ibutilide is an intravenous class 3 agent. It promotes a slow inward sodium current and blocks delayed rectifier potassium channels. It is used to stop recent onset atrial flutter or fibrillation. The same potassium block prolongs repolarisation, so the QT lengthens and torsades can follow. A polymorphic ventricular tachycardia electrocardiogram shows a rapid irregular wide complex rhythm with a changing QRS height. Stop the culprit drug, give magnesium, correct potassium, and overdrive pace if torsades persists.
