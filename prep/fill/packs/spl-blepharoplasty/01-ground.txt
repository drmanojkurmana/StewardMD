Exam grounding notes for blepharoplasty and eyelid assessment, written for grounding, not for display. Paraphrased from Plastic Surgery 5E Volume 2 on eyelid surgery.

The upper lid crease forms where terminal fibres of the levator palpebrae superioris aponeurosis insert into the deep dermis. The crease sits 8 to 10 mm above the ciliary margin in females and 7 to 8 mm in males. Marking respects this dimorphism or the lid looks operated.

Upper lid fat occupies two compartments: the central preaponeurotic fat pad and the medial pale fat pad. The lacrimal gland fills the superolateral orbital compartment and must never be mistaken for fat. Resecting gland instead of fat dries the eye.

Lower lid fat divides into three distinct compartments called medial, central, and lateral. The arcuate expansion of the inferior oblique muscle separates them like curtains. Each compartment herniates independently with age.

The transconjunctival route enters the retroseptal space through the conjunctiva, never violating the orbital septum or the orbicularis oculi muscle. No external cutaneous scar results, which suits younger patients with herniated fat and tight skin.

Hamra lower lid surgery mobilises orbital fat pedicles over the inferior orbital rim into a tear trough subperiosteal pocket. Filling the nasojugal groove with living fat beats resecting it, because excision alone hollows the lid-cheek junction.

Laxity testing precedes every lower lid case. Snap-back and distraction tests measure horizontal laxity; distraction beyond 6 to 8 mm or a delayed snap-back mandates lateral canthopexy or canthoplasty, otherwise post-blepharoplasty ectropion with scleral show follows.

Blepharoptosis workup records margin reflex distance-1, where MRD1 of 4.0 to 4.5 mm is normal, and levator excursion, normal at 12 to 15 mm. Low MRD1 with good excursion suits aponeurosis repair; poor excursion needs a sling or frontalis drive.

Retrobulbar haematoma is the feared emergency: bleeding behind the globe raises acute intraocular hypertension into an orbital compartment syndrome that blinds within hours. Treatment is immediate bedside lateral canthotomy with inferior cantholysis, cutting the eyelid corner and the lower lid retractors.
