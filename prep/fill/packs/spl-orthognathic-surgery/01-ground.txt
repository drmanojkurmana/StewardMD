## Orthognathic Surgery: Le Fort Osteotomy, BSSO and Cephalometrics
Citations and Chapter Sources: Plastic Surgery 5E Vol 3 Ch 29 (Orthognathic surgery)

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Angle classification of malocclusion defines Class I normal molar relationship, Class II retrognathic mandible overjet, and Class III prognathic mandible underbite.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: I, II, III
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Angle classification, Class I, Class II, Class III

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Bilateral sagittal split osteotomy BSSO splits the mandibular ramus sagitally, allowing controlled advancement or setback of the dentate mandible while maintaining condylar position.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: two
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: BSSO, mandibular ramus, condylar position

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Inferior alveolar nerve IAN injury paresthesia is the most common sensory complication of BSSO, occurring in 10% to 30% of cases at one year.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 10%, 30%
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: inferior alveolar nerve, IAN, paresthesia

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Le Fort I maxillary osteotomy mobilises the maxilla on a palatal soft tissue pedicle, enabling three-dimensional repositioning for vertical maxillary excess or hypoplasia.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: I
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: Le Fort I osteotomy, maxillary repositioning

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Distraction osteogenesis Ilizarov principle generates new bone via mechanical tensile strain across a corticotomy gap following a 5 to 7 day latency period.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 5, 7
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: distraction osteogenesis, Ilizarov, corticotomy

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Standard distraction rate is 1.0 mm per day delivered in two to four increments 0.25 mm four times daily to optimize bone and soft tissue regeneration.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 1.0, 0.25, four
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: distraction rate, increments

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Consolidation phase in distraction osteogenesis requires a minimum of 6 to 8 weeks or double the distraction time before device removal.
Key numerical metrics, dimensions, intervals, and angles: 6, 8
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: consolidation phase, device removal

Core Must-Know Examination Principle: Genioplasty osteotomy of the mandibular symphysis alters chin projection in sagittal, vertical, and transverse planes without disturbing dental occlusion.
Standard anatomical nomenclature and operative terminology: genioplasty, chin projection, symphysis

Surgical planning in Orthognathic Surgery Principles requires meticulous anatomical grounding, precise knowledge of blood supply, innervation, fascial planes, and tissue biomechanics.
Operative interventions balance functional restoration of speech, swallowing, mastication, sensory discrimination, and locomotion with aesthetic symmetry and minimal donor site morbidity.
Preoperative evaluation integrates acoustic Doppler, computed tomography angiography, indocyanine green fluorescence imaging, and patient-specific risk factor optimization including smoking cessation, strict glycemic control, and blood pressure management.
Postoperative care prioritizes early mobilization, monitoring of capillary refill, tissue turgor, pinprick bleeding, active rehabilitation, and vigilant surveillance for hematoma, seroma, infection, or microvascular thrombosis.
