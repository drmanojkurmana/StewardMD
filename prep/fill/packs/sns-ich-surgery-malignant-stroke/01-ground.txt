Deep bleeds stay medical. STICH found no routine open-surgery benefit for supratentorial ICH, so deep hypertensive clots are individualised.

Keyholes cut death. MISTIE III catheter drainage with alteplase lowers mortality without functional gain, for selected lobar clots with day-seven volume checks.

Go early for surface clots. ENRICH within 24 hours improves function for lobar bleeds, best in moderate clots before secondary cascades mature.

Respect the small fossa. Cerebellar clots above 3 cm, squashed fourth ventricle or tonsillar descent get craniectomy plus drainage at once.

Wash blocked ventricles. CLEAR III alteplase through the EVD lowers mortality for small clots with massive ventricular blood, dosed per protocol.

Open giant strokes fast. Malignant MCA infarcts over half the territory need hemicraniectomy within 48 hours, tripling independence below 60 years.

Counsel the elderly honestly. Above 60, DESTINY II survival usually means dependency, with aphasia likely in dominant strokes.

Treat back-brain mass effect. Effaced quadrigeminal cisterns or hydrocephalus trigger suboccipital decompression plus drainage; brainstem infarcts stay supportive.
