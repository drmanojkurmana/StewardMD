Scleroderma organ sheet. Figures referenced: 80, 30, 10, 15, 20, 2, 5, 90, 1. Agents referenced: mycophenolate mofetil, nintedanib, captopril, prednisone, rifaximin, diltiazem, sodium thiosulfate.

Lung scarring dominates diffuse scleroderma, touching more than eighty percent of diffuse cases and thirty percent of limited cases. Chest HRCT shows basilar ground glass with traction bronchiectasis in NSIP pattern. Mycophenolate mofetil or nintedanib slows the fibrotic slide. Fibrosis leads even when skin stays quiet.

Vessel narrowing in lungs builds group one pulmonary hypertension in ten to fifteen percent of scleroderma patients. Right heart catheterization proves it: mean artery pressure above twenty, wedge fifteen or below, resistance above two Wood units. Endothelin blockers plus PDE-five inhibitors form the drug backbone. Breathlessness raises both lung and vessel alarms.

Kidney crisis strikes five to ten percent of diffuse cases as sudden hypertensive emergency. Steroid bursts at prednisone fifteen milligrams daily or more, plus anti-RNA polymerase three antibodies, light the fuse. Oral captopril given emergently saves kidneys and lives. Steroids fuel the fire that captopril quenches.

Gut involvement tops ninety percent: slack lower esophageal sphincter brings fierce reflux and Barrett change, while watermelon stomach (GAVE) oozes toward chronic iron deficiency anemia. Reflux burns while vessels leak.

Small bowel stasis breeds pseudo-obstruction and bacterial overgrowth, with chronic watery diarrhea, bloating, and malabsorption. Hydrogen breath testing confirms overgrowth; rotating rifaximin courses clear it and restore absorption.

Heart muscle fibrosis seeds patchy contraction band necrosis, stiff diastolic filling, HFpEF, and ventricular arrhythmias. Late gadolinium enhancement on cardiac MRI reveals the scar.

Calcium hydroxyapatite lumps (calcinosis cutis) sit under skin and around joints, breaking into chronic painful ulcers that invite secondary infection. Debridement removes deposits; diltiazem or sodium thiosulfate calms calcium and prolongs healing.

For rapidly progressive poor-prognosis diffuse disease failing conventional DMARDs, immunoablative conditioning followed by autologous stem cell rescue improves skin scores, pulmonary function, and long-term event-free survival. Transplant rescues what drugs cannot hold.
