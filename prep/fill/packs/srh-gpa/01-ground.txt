Wegener triad sheet. Figures referenced: 85, 90, 3, 50, 375, 2, 4, 30, 52, 500, 6, 18, 24, 5. Agents referenced: rituximab, cyclophosphamide, avacopan, prednisone, azathioprine, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole.

Granulomatosis with polyangiitis, once Wegener disease, is a necrotizing granulomatous vasculitis of small and medium vessels striking nose, lungs, and kidneys together. Three organs inflame as one syndrome.

Eighty five to ninety percent of active generalized cases carry cytoplasmic ANCA against proteinase three. Surface proteinase three on primed neutrophils grabs the antibody, triggering degranulation, oxygen burst, and endothelial death. Antibodies turn neutrophils against vessels with necrotizing fury.

Noses crumble first in over ninety percent: stubborn rhinosinusitis, nosebleeds, crusting, septal perforation collapsing into saddle nose, mixed hearing loss, and choking subglottic stenosis. Crusts today predict saddles tomorrow.

Lungs show bilateral nodules cavitating with thick ragged walls in up to fifty percent on CT, patchy consolidations, and catastrophic diffuse alveolar hemorrhage from necrotizing capillaritis. Nodules cavitate; capillaries bleed.

Kidneys show pauci-immune necrotizing crescentic glomerulonephritis: cellular crescents with fibrinoid necrosis but almost no immune deposits on immunofluorescence. Rapidly progressive renal failure with active urinary sediment (red cells, red cell casts, proteinuria) announces the lesion. Crescents grow while deposits stay absent.

The RAVE trial proved rituximab (three seventy five milligrams per meter squared weekly for four doses) matches daily oral cyclophosphamide for induction, winning clearly in relapsing disease. Antibody therapy equals alkylators, beating them in relapse.

The ADVOCATE trial proved avacopan (thirty milligrams twice daily), an oral complement five-a receptor blocker, beats tapering prednisone for sustained remission at week fifty two while dodging steroid toxicities. Complement blockade outlasts steroids with far fewer metabolic scars.

Maintenance belongs to rituximab (five hundred milligrams every six months for eighteen to twenty four months), superior to azathioprine in MAINRITSAN. Everyone on rituximab or cyclophosphamide needs Pneumocystis prophylaxis with trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole throughout immunosuppression. Rituximab maintains; sulfa protects lungs.
