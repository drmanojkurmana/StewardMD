# Nasopharyngeal carcinoma
Nasopharyngeal carcinoma, ENT / Otorhinolaryngology, ENT.
Aliases: NPC; nasopharyngeal cancer; carcinoma of the nasopharynx.
# Pathophysiology
Nasopharyngeal carcinoma develops in the epithelial lining of the nasopharynx, typically in the recess of the fossa of Rosenmuller near the Eustachian tube opening. Malignant transformation is closely linked to latent Epstein-Barr virus infection, together with genetic susceptibility and environmental exposures such as diets rich in preserved salted foods and nitrosamines. The tumour tends to spread submucosally and infiltrate early, obstructing the Eustachian tube to cause middle ear effusion, and eroding through the skull base foramina to involve cranial nerves and the cavernous sinus. Its rich lymphatic drainage leads to early and often bilateral cervical lymph node metastasis, and distant spread to bone, lung and liver can occur.
