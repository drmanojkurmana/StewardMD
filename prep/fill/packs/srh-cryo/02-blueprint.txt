# Cryoglobulinaemia and cryoglobulinaemic vasculitis (blueprint grounding: Kelley and Firestein, Harrison)
Cryoglobulins precipitate below 37 degrees Celsius, redissolving on warming, and are tested in serum kept strictly at 37 degrees until clot separation, unlike cold agglutinins which spare serum.
Brouet types are type I monoclonal IgM, IgG, or IgA in lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma, myeloma, or MGUS, and types II (monoclonal IgM with rheumatoid factor activity against polyclonal IgG) and III (polyclonal IgM against polyclonal IgG) as mixed cryoglobulinaemia.
Mixed disease links to chronic hepatitis C in Southern Europe with membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis, and to hepatitis B, HIV, autoimmune disease (SLE, Sjogren), and B-cell lymphoma.
Meltzer triad is purpura with arthralgias and weakness in mixed cryoglobulinaemic vasculitis, with palpable purpura, ulcers, and Raynaud phenomenon.
Renal disease is type I membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis with subendothelial IgM-kappa deposits, presenting nephritic or nephrotic with low C4 and rheumatoid factor positivity.
Type I hyperviscosity gives headache, blurred vision with flame hemorrhages and venous sausaging, deafness, and digital ischemia; plasmapheresis is emergency therapy.
DAAs cure the hepatitis C driver: sofosbuvir with velpatasvir for 12 weeks or glecaprevir with pibrentasvir for 8 to 12 weeks, with anti-CD20 rituximab for vasculitic flares.
HCV-negative vasculitis takes glucocorticoids with rituximab 375 mg per square meter weekly for 4 weeks, delaying DAA only in severe vasculitis.
Peripheral neuropathy as painful sensory polyneuropathy or mononeuritis multiplex affects up to 60 percent of mixed cryoglobulinaemia; skin biopsy shows leukocytoclastic vasculitis of post-capillary venules.
False transport at room temperature dissolves the cryoprecipitate and causes false-negative testing; the laboratory must be warned before sampling, with tubes pre-warmed and carried in a 37-degree flask.
Rituximab dosing at 375 mg per square meter weekly for 4 weeks totals 1,500 mg per square meter per course; premedication with methylprednisolone 100 mg with antihistamine precedes each infusion.
Essential mixed cryoglobulinaemia without any driver takes the same rituximab-glucocorticoid regimen as HCV-negative disease, with maintenance rituximab every 6 months for relapsing courses.
