Postoperative strictures present months after cholecystectomy with cholestasis and cholangitis, and MRCP maps the level while endoscopic dilation with multiple plastic stents treats short lesions. Long or hilar strictures need hepaticojejunostomy.

Primary sclerosing cholangitis shows beaded ducts with ulcerative colitis in 70 percent, progressing to cirrhosis with 10 to 15 percent cholangiocarcinoma risk. Surveillance covers both bowel and bile ducts.

Dominant strictures under 1.5 mm in PSC with rising bilirubin need balloon dilation with brush cytology because of cancer risk, repeated annually. Stenting follows incomplete dilation.

IgG4 sclerosing cholangitis with serum IgG4 over 135 mg per dL and sausage pancreas responds to prednisolone 0.6 mg per kg, distinguished from cancer by steroid trial. Relapse needs rituximab or azathioprine maintenance.

Recurrent pyogenic cholangitis with intrahepatic pigment stones and strictures in Southeast Asia needs hepatectomy for atrophic segments with hepatolithiasis plus biliary access loop. Stones recur without resection.

Biliary parasitic infestation with Ascaris or Clonorchis causes relapsing cholangitis, cleared by ERCP extraction with albendazole, and chronic Clonorchis raises cholangiocarcinoma risk. Stool ova testing guides therapy.

Charcot triad (3 features: pain, fever and jaundice) marks cholangitis while Reynolds pentad (5 features: adding hypotension and confusion) marks suppurative disease needing urgent drainage. Pentad carries high mortality.

Severe cholangitis with organ dysfunction needs ERCP drainage within 6 hours, moderate disease within 24 hours, and mild disease electively after antibiotics. Resuscitation with fluids and vasopressors runs alongside. Bismuth-Corlette types I to V locate hilar strictures, with type IV needing bilateral drainage. Percutaneous transhepatic access rescues failed ERCP. Mirizzi syndrome with gallstone compressing the hepatic duct mimics cancer, needing partial cholecystectomy with biliary repair. Post-transplant strictures respond to repeated ERCP stenting before retransplant listing. Stent exchange every 3 months prevents cholangitis between dilations. Benign strictures after liver resection cluster at the cut surface, managed by hepaticojejunostomy revision.
