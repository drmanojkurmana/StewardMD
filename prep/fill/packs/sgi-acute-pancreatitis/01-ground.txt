Pancreatitis anchors.
Revised Atlanta classification needs 2 criteria out of 3. Enzyme ceiling: lipase or amylase tripled versus the lab reference. Imaging is the third possible criterion.
Moderately severe: transient organ failure within 48 hours, or a local complication. Severe: persistent organ failure beyond 48 hours.
Modified Marshall scoring covers 3 systems. A system score of 2 or higher counts as organ failure.
WATERFALL trial: aggressive fluid raised fluid overload and heart failure.
Early oral refeeding or enteral nutrition beats starvation and intravenous feeding.
step-up approach for infected pancreatic necrosis: drain first, necrosectomy second.
Walled-off necrosis: inflammatory wall after at least 4 weeks.
rectal indomethacin or diclofenac 100 mg. post-ERCP pancreatitis reduced by over 50%.
Agents: indomethacin, diclofenac.
Digits: 2, 3, 48, 4, 100, 50, 1.
