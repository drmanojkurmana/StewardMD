Resectable disease has no arterial contact and venous contact under 180 degrees without contour irregularity, borderline has limited arterial abutment under 180 degrees, and unresectable encases celiac or superior mesenteric artery over 180 degrees. NCCN criteria standardise reporting.

Pancreaticoduodenectomy removes head, duodenum, distal bile duct and first jejunal loop with Child reconstruction, and pylorus-preserving suits benign and premalignant disease. Classic Whipple removes the antrum.

RAMPS radical antegrade modular pancreatosplenectomy takes Gerota fascia with N1 nodes for body-tail cancers, improving posterior margin clearance. Anterior RAMPS suits tumours clear of adrenals.

Modified FOLFIRINOX for 6 months is the adjuvant standard after resection per PRODIGE 24, and neoadjuvant FOLFIRINOX converts borderline disease with CA 19-9 response guiding resection. Gemcitabine-capecitabine suits frail patients.

Postoperative pancreatic fistula is drain amylase over 3 times serum from day 3, graded ISGPS A biochemical through C with organ failure, managed by drainage, antibiotics and somatostatin analogues. Persistent fistula needs endoscopic stenting.

Delayed gastric emptying needing nasogastric tube beyond day 3 or reinsertion by day 14 is graded ISGPS A to C and managed by prokinetics with pyloric preservation techniques at reoperation. Erythromycin prokinetics bridge the gap.

Germline BRCA1, BRCA2 and PALB2 testing guides platinum sensitivity and olaparib maintenance per POLO, and all patients get somatic KRAS, mismatch repair and NTRK profiling. Family cascade testing follows positive germline results.

Unresectable head tumours get endoscopic metal biliary stenting with 6 to 10 month patency plus celiac plexus neurolysis for pain, and duodenal obstruction gets enteral stenting or gastrojejunostomy. Early palliative care improves survival. Venous resection with graft reconstruction extends resectability when the portal vein is involved. Adjuvant chemoradiation suits positive margins after chemotherapy. Staging laparoscopy with peritoneal cytology precedes laparotomy in body-tail cancers. Preoperative biliary drainage is avoided unless cholangitis or neoadjuvant delay mandates stenting. Total neoadjuvant therapy is explored in trials for high-risk resectable disease.
