# Arthropathy of systemic and endocrine disease (blueprint grounding: Kelley and Firestein, Harrison)
Hemochromatosis arthropathy affects 40 percent to 60 percent of homozygous C282Y HFE carriers, with non-inflammatory degeneration of second and third MCP joints, hook-like osteophytes, and chondrocalcinosis.
Ochronosis in alkaptonuria is autosomal recessive tyrosine pathway disease from homogentisate 1,2-dioxygenase (HGD) deficiency; homogentisic acid binds collagen and stains connective tissue dark blue-black.
Ochronotic spines show universal wafer-like disc calcifications with profound multilevel lumbar disc narrowing, plus osteopenia and brittle cartilage.
Acromegalic arthropathy from chronic growth hormone and IGF-1 excess starts with cartilage hypertrophy and wide joint spaces, then premature secondary osteoarthritis, with carpal tunnel syndrome in above 50 percent.
Thyroid acropachy is rare Graves disease with high-titer TSH receptor antibodies, giving the triad of digital clubbing, digit soft tissue swelling, and painless subperiosteal new bone in hand and foot tubular bones.
Charcot joints follow lost pain and proprioception: diabetic sensory neuropathy hits tarsal and metatarsophalangeal joints, while tabes dorsalis and syringomyelia hit shoulder and elbow.
Charcot radiographs move from fragmentation and capsular distension through osteolysis and dislocation to exuberant disorganized new bone, the classic bag of bones.
Acute active Charcot foot requires strict non-weight-bearing total contact casting (TCC), then custom CROW bracing to prevent collapse.
Nitisinone (NTBC) inhibits upstream hydroxyphenylpyruvate dioxygenase and lowers homogentisic acid in alkaptonuria; joint replacement is often needed by the sixth decade despite therapy.
Diabetic Charcot foot mimics cellulitis and osteomyelitis with a hot swollen foot; skin temperature above 2 degrees Celsius warmer than the other foot with intact skin points to active Charcot, while ulcers with exposed bone point to infection.
Syringomyelia from Chiari malformation or intramedullary tumour destroys shoulder and elbow over years; tabes dorsalis from tertiary syphilis adds lightning pains and Argyll Robertson pupils to the joint collapse.
IGF-1 shares structural homology with insulin and mediates most growth hormone effects on cartilage and bone; diabetes from insulin resistance is common in active acromegaly.
