# EGPA essentials (blueprint grounding: Kelley and Firestein, Harrison; MIRRA and MANDARA trials)
EGPA is necrotizing vasculitis with severe adult-onset asthma, blood and tissue eosinophilia, and granulomatous small-to-medium vessel inflammation.
Three phases run allergic prodrome (adult asthma, rhinitis, nasal polyps), eosinophilic phase (blood eosinophils above 1,500 per microliter or above 10 percent, with eosinophilic pneumonia and gastroenteritis), then systemic vasculitis (mononeuritis multiplex, purpura, glomerulonephritis).
ANCA is positive in 30 percent to 50 percent, almost always p-ANCA against MPO; ANCA-positive disease brings glomerulonephritis, mononeuritis multiplex, and purpura, while ANCA-negative disease brings eosinophilic cardiac infiltration.
Heart disease leads mortality with eosinophilic endomyocarditis, myocardial fibrosis, restrictive cardiomyopathy, mural thrombi, and heart failure, seen as late gadolinium enhancement on cardiac MRI.
Neuropathy as mononeuritis multiplex or asymmetric polyneuropathy from ischemic epineurial arteritis hits up to 70 percent to 80 percent, the commonest vasculitic feature.
Five-Factor Score of 1 or higher with cardiomyopathy, gut disease, or renal failure requires induction with systemic glucocorticoids plus IV cyclophosphamide. FFS 0 with no poor factors takes glucocorticoids alone.
Mepolizumab 300 mg subcutaneously every 4 weeks raised remission weeks with steroid tapering in the MIRRA trial of relapsing or refractory non-organ-threatening EGPA.
Benralizumab matched mepolizumab for sustained remission in the MANDARA trial, killing eosinophils by antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity through the IL-5 receptor alpha.
