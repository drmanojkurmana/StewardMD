Short bowel defines failure. Short bowel under 200 cm with colon or under 100 cm without colon causes intestinal failure needing parenteral nutrition, with citrulline under 20 micromol per L marking severe loss.

Continuity decides weaning. Ileocecal valve and colon in continuity slow transit and salvage fluids, so jejunocolic anatomy weans faster than end-jejunostomy under 100 cm.

STEP staples zigzags. STEP serial transverse enteroplasty staples dilated bowel over 3.5 cm into zigzag channels, lengthening by 50 percent and cutting parenteral need in children with dilated remnant loops.

Bianchi splits lengthwise. Bianchi longitudinal lengthening splits a dilated loop over 4 cm along mesenteric leaves, doubling length while halving calibre for better transit in very dilated remnant bowel.

Teduglutide grows villi. Teduglutide GLP-2 analogue at 0.05 mg per kg daily grows villi and cuts parenteral volume by 20 percent or more in a third of treated patients.

Transplant rescues the irreversibly failed. Intestinal transplant suits irreversible failure with liver disease, lost access or recurrent sepsis, with 5-year survival near 60 percent at expert centres after careful listing.

D-lactic clouds thinking. D-lactic acidosis with confusion after carbohydrate meals in short bowel needs thiamine, bicarbonate and antibiotics for bacterial overgrowth rather than fluids alone, with carbohydrate restriction when symptoms recur.

High output needs salt and slowing. High-output stoma over 2 L per day gets fluid restriction with oral rehydration salts, loperamide, octreotide and proton pump inhibition to cut secretions and protect kidney function with daily checks.
