Echinococcus granulosus from dog faeces forms unilocular liver cysts in 70 percent of hydatid cases, with right lobe predominance and slow growth of 1 cm per year. Sheep-rearing regions carry the highest burden.

WHO stages CE1 to CE5 separate active unilocular and multivesicular cysts from transitional degenerating and inactive CE4 and CE5 calcified cysts, deciding between drugs, PAIR and surgery. Ultrasound with the wall sign stages most cysts.

PAIR puncture-aspiration-injection-reaspiration suits CE1 and CE3a cysts over 5 cm, using hypertonic saline or alcohol scolicidal with albendazole cover. Biliary communication must be excluded first, since scolicidal in bile ducts causes sclerosing cholangitis.

Albendazole 400 mg twice daily in 28-day cycles with 14-day breaks, or continuously with monitoring, sterilises small cysts and must precede and follow any intervention. Liver enzymes and blood counts are checked each cycle.

Cystobiliary communication with jaundice, cholangitis or hydatid membranes in bile needs ERCP clearance plus surgery with cavity-bile leak closure and omentoplasty. Major communications may need biliary-enteric diversion.

Pericystectomy removes the cyst with its fibrous capsule for peripheral lesions, while central cysts near vessels get partial pericystectomy with capitonnage to avoid major bleeding. Liver resection suits multiple or complicated cysts.

Intraoperative spillage risks anaphylaxis and secondary peritoneal hydatidosis, prevented by scolicidal-soaked packs, closed aspiration and albendazole prophylaxis. Adrenaline readiness is mandatory before opening any cyst.

Alveolar echinococcosis from Echinococcus multilocularis behaves like cancer with infiltrative growth, needing radical resection with 2 cm margins plus 2 years of albendazole. Transplantation is considered for unresectable hilar disease. Serology with ELISA and immunoblot supports the diagnosis, and eosinophilia is inconstant. Calcified rims on CT suggest inactive disease, while daughter vesicles mark active cysts needing treatment. Laparoscopic management suits accessible cysts with the same scolicidal precautions. Pregnancy favours deferring surgery to the second trimester with albendazole avoided in the first trimester. Follow-up ultrasound continues for years after treatment.
