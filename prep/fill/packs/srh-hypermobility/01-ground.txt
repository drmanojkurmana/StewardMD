Beighton scoring counts bendiness: five of nine in adults, six of nine in children, four of nine past fifty years. Pinkies past ninety degrees, thumbs to forearms, elbows and knees past ten degrees, palms flat to floor: each side scores. Bendy tallies sort the supple.

Hypermobile EDS (twenty seventeen rules) needs no gene test: generalized hypermobility plus systemic connective tissue signs in two or more groups, without fragile skin or vessel rupture, seals it. Clinical clusters diagnose the commonest EDS.

Vascular EDS (type four) springs from COL three A one defects in type three collagen: medium and large arteries split, bowels burst, pregnant wombs rupture, faces thin to vermilion sharpness. Type three collagen failure bursts tubes.

Classical EDS (types one and two) springs from COL five A one or A two defects in type five collagen: skin stretches past one point five centimeters, scars widen into cigarette paper, joints swing loose. Type five collagen failure loosens skin.

Marfan syndrome (Ghent rules) springs from dominant FBN one fibrillin one defects: aortic roots swelling to Z-scores of two point zero or more plus superotemporal lens dislocation clinch it, or systemic tallies of seven or more with family history. Tall roots with slipped lenses mark Marfan.

Loeys-Dietz springs from TGF-beta pathway defects (TGFBR one and two, SMAD three, TGFB two and three): twisty aneurysmal arteries with wide-set eyes and split or cleft uvulas. Twisted vessels with split uvulas mark the pathway.

Brittle bone disease (types one to four) springs from COL one A one and A two defects in type one collagen: trivial trauma snaps bones, sclerae glow blue, teeth crumble amber, stapes fixation deafens conduction. Blue eyes with snapping bones mark type one collagen.

Vessel guarding in vascular EDS and Marfan means yearly echo and vascular MR scans, banning contact and isometric sports, and taming pressures with celiprolol for vascular EDS or beta-blockers and angiotensin receptor blockers for Marfan. Scans with gentle sports and pressure pills guard tubes.
