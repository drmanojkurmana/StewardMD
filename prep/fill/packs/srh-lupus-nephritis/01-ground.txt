Kidney siege strikes forty to sixty of each hundred lupus patients. Biopsy becomes mandatory with stubborn proteinuria past half gram daily or matching spot ratio, or active sediment bearing dysmorphic red cells or cellular casts. Protein plus sediment means needle.

Histology sorts six ISN classes: one minimal mesangial, two mesangial proliferative, three focal under half of glomeruli, four diffuse at or past half, five membranous, six burnt-out with ninety percent global sclerosis. Class decides guns.

Fluorescence lights full house: IgG plus IgA plus IgM plus C3 plus C1q in mesangium and loops, with bulky subendothelial wire loops in hot class four. Full house plus loops equals active proliferative disease.

Induction pairs steroids with mycophenolate two to three grams daily or Euro-Lupus cyclophosphamide five hundred every two weeks six times, three grams total; remission rates tie. Pick by fertility, bladder, access.

Triple multitarget stacks mycophenolate plus calcineurin blockade (tacrolimus two to four daily or voclosporin) plus low steroids, winning faster complete responses in proliferative and mixed disease. Three prongs beat two in hard kidneys.

Belimumab anti-BAFF joins induction per BLISS LN, lifting primary renal response and cutting flares atop mycophenolate or Euro-Lupus backbones. B-cell help blockade deepens wins.

Won induction transitions to mycophenolate one to two grams or azathioprine two per kilo daily for three to five years minimum before feather-slow withdrawal, with lifelong hydroxychloroquine running beneath. Years, not months, guard kidneys.

Background care never pauses: hydroxychloroquine capped five per kilo actual weight trims flares, clots, deaths; ACE or ARB plus sub one twenty systolic pressure per KDIGO shields protein and vessels. Support drugs save lives too.

Repeat biopsy adjudicates refractory proteinuria versus chronicity versus nonadherence; activity versus scars redirect therapy. Pregnancy planning demands quiescent disease plus safe drugs, swapping teratogens for azathioprine well ahead.
