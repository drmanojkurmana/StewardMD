IBS and constipation anchors.
Rome IV criteria: recurrent abdominal pain, at least 1 day each week, last 3 months, at least 2 associated features. Version digit 4. Onset over 6 months earlier.
Bristol Stool Form Scale from Type 1 to Type 7. IBS subtypes C, D, M, U.
Linaclotide and plecanatide are guanylate cyclase-C agonists via CFTR.
Lubiprostone opens type 2 chloride channels.
Prucalopride is a 5-HT4 receptor agonist for chronic idiopathic constipation.
Eluxadoline is contraindicated without a gallbladder because of sphincter of Oddi spasm.
Dyssynergic defecation: balloon expulsion test, 50 mL water, within 1 minute. Biofeedback.
Low-FODMAP diet restricts fermentable oligosaccharides. Relief up to 70%.
Agents: linaclotide, plecanatide, lubiprostone, prucalopride, eluxadoline.
Digits: 4, 1, 3, 2, 6, 7, 5, 50, 70.
