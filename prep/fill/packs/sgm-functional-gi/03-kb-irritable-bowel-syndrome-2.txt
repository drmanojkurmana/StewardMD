# Irritable bowel syndrome
Irritable bowel syndrome, Gastrointestinal, Gastroenterology.
Aliases: IBS; spastic colon.
# Pathophysiology
Irritable bowel syndrome is a disorder of gut-brain interaction in which altered gastrointestinal motility, visceral hypersensitivity, changes in the gut microbiota, low-grade mucosal immune activation, and disturbed central processing of visceral signals combine to produce pain and altered bowel habit. Bidirectional signalling along the gut-brain axis, often modulated by stress and psychological factors, amplifies visceral perception. There is no structural or biochemical abnormality that defines the condition, which is characterised instead by its symptom pattern.
# Prognosis
The condition is chronic and relapsing but benign, and most patients achieve worthwhile symptom control with an individualised combination of diet, medication and psychological approaches.
# Clinical pearls
A disorder of gut-brain interaction defined by recurrent abdominal pain related to defecation and associated with a change in stool frequency or form
It is subclassified by predominant bowel habit into constipation-predominant, diarrhoea-predominant, mixed and unclassified types
