# Fibromuscular dysplasia
Fibromuscular dysplasia, Nervous system / Cerebrovascular, Neurology.
Aliases: FMD; fibromuscular hyperplasia; medial fibroplasia.
# Pathophysiology
Fibromuscular dysplasia is an idiopathic, segmental, non-inflammatory and non-atherosclerotic disorder of medium-sized arteries. Abnormal proliferation and disorganised deposition of collagen and fibrous tissue within the arterial wall, most often the media, create alternating areas of stenosis and mural dilatation that give the beaded angiographic pattern; focal forms produce discrete stenoses. The disordered wall architecture predisposes to dissection, aneurysm formation, and thromboembolism, so cerebrovascular events may arise from stenosis, dissection, or embolism rather than from atherosclerotic plaque.
