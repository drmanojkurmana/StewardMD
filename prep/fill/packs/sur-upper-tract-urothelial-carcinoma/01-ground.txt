Upper tract urothelial carcinoma, abbreviated UTUC, of the renal pelvis and ureter forms 5% to 10% of urothelial cancers, yet 60% are invasive at presentation, against only 15% to 25% of bladder tumours, because the thin ureteric wall is breached early. Lynch syndrome, the inherited mismatch repair defect in MSH2, MLH1, MSH6, or PMS2, multiplies lifetime UTUC risk 14- to 22-fold, so carriers need annual urine cytology with upper tract imaging alongside bowel and endometrial surveillance.

Aristolochic acid in herbal remedies scars the cortex and grows bilateral extensive upper tract cancers bearing A:T to T:A transversions in TP53; Balkan endemic nephropathy is the classic regional form. Radical nephroureterectomy with en bloc excision of the ipsilateral bladder cuff cures high risk disease: kidney, full length ureter, and cuff leave as one piece, since piecemeal surgery seeds tumour. Leaving the stump invites bladder cuff recurrence of 30% to 50% at the ureteral stump or hemitrigone, so the orifice is always excised with a rim.

Kidney-sparing surgery, endoscopic laser ablation or segmental ureterectomy, suits low risk lesions that are unifocal, under 2 cm, low grade, and non invasive, and imperative cases such as solitary kidneys or bilateral disease, with lifelong ureteroscopic surveillance. Within 2 to 10 days of nephroureterectomy a single bladder instillation of mitomycin C or pirarubicin cuts later bladder recurrence by 40% to 50% by killing floating cells. Neoadjuvant gemcitabine cisplatin downstages advanced disease and lengthens survival, provided cisplatin fitness is confirmed; split dosing or carboplatin substitution weakens the gain. High grade ureteroscopic biopsy, sessile architecture, hydronephrosis, and tumour size above 2 cm all push the decision toward radical surgery, while favourable biology allows a kidney-sparing path. MSH6 and PMS2 carriers face lower but real urothelial risk alongside MSH2 families, so pedigree review guides who enters screening. TP53 transversions mark aristolochic injury specifically, distinguishing it from smoking related urothelial cancer.
