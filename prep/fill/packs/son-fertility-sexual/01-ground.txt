Fertility, sexual function and gonadal damage after cancer treatment: grounding notes

Combined regimens
- When several drugs are combined, their gonadal effects add together. No synergy has been shown, so the total risk is roughly the sum of each drug's contribution.
- Total dose matters more than the rate at which it is given. Spreading the same total over a longer period changes the outcome less.
- An older estimate put the cyclophosphamide threshold at about 19 g/m2 for half of men to be permanently sterile. Adding doxorubicin at a median of 450 mg/m2 lowered that threshold to about 15 g/m2.
- Standard references also express alkylator exposure as a cyclophosphamide equivalent dose (CED), which converts each alkylating agent into cyclophosphamide units. Azoospermia is uncommon when CED stays below 4000 mg/m2.

Risk tiers in men
- Alkylating agents form the most sterilizing class. Chlorambucil and cyclophosphamide, given alone, cause prolonged azoospermia.
- Procarbazine and high-dose cisplatin are also strongly sterilizing. Busulfan usually causes azoospermia only when combined with other agents.
- Methotrexate, vincristine and bleomycin give temporary drops in sperm count at usual doses. Dacarbazine is the single alkylating agent in that milder group.
- Doxorubicin mainly adds to damage from other drugs rather than causing it alone.
- Regimen tables give the share of men with prolonged azoospermia. CyOPPd, the Hodgkin combination built on cyclophosphamide, vincristine, procarbazine and prednisone, reached 100 percent after 4 to 9 courses. Busulfan plus cyclophosphamide before stem cell transplant reached 50 percent. ABVD, built on doxorubicin, bleomycin, vinblastine and dacarbazine, gave 0 percent after 6 courses with radiotherapy.

Germ cells and Leydig cells
- Spermatogonia are the dividing stem cells of sperm production. Many cytotoxic drugs destroy them, and the semen may then contain no sperm.
- Later germ cells resist killing, so sperm counts fall only after a delay.
- Leydig cells make testosterone. Most regimens leave them intact, so puberty timing and testosterone usually stay normal.
- Busulfan with cyclophosphamide before a stem cell transplant is the exception, delaying puberty in about 30% of boys.
- Radiation must exceed 18 Gy before androgen output is affected. Major damage needs more than 30 Gy.
- When germ cells are lost, the pituitary raises luteinizing hormone so that testosterone stays near normal.

Female fertility preservation
- Embryo cryopreservation, which means freezing fertilised embryos, is the most successful banking method. It is preferred whenever it is viable.
- Oocyte vitrification, which means rapid freezing of eggs, suits women without a partner.
- A standard IVF cycle takes 2 to 3 weeks. Random-start stimulation begins at presentation and reduces the time to about 12 days. Without random-start, oocyte banking may delay treatment by 12 days to 6 weeks.
- Ovarian tissue cryopreservation removes ovarian strips for freezing and later grafting. It needs no ovarian stimulation, so it suits girls before puberty. Its drawback is the return of malignant cells, a risk that matters most in leukemia.

GnRH analogs
- GnRH analogs have inconsistent results for ovarian protection across cancer treatments. Most studies do not back hormone treatment for girls who have not started menstruating.
- Their better-established use is menstrual suppression, which limits heavy bleeding in blood cancers.
- ASCO guidance from 2025 says these drugs should not replace established methods. In practice, they could serve as add-on therapy for women with breast cancer, and also suppress menstruation when treatment is urgent.

Sexual function measures
- The IIEF is a validated, self-completed scale covering sexual function after prostate cancer treatment, including surgery.
- Completion takes 5 to 10 minutes.
- The full IIEF has 15 items across five domains. The abridged SHIM uses 5 items and is a validated short form.
- Clinicians use the SHIM to grade erectile dysfunction, meaning inability to get or keep an erection, and to measure treatment response.
