# Idiopathic inflammatory myopathies (blueprint grounding: Kelley and Firestein, Harrison)
IIM gives symmetric proximal weakness with raised CK, aldolase, and transaminases plus EMG irritability (fibrillations, positive sharp waves, early recruitment).
Dermatomyositis shows Gottron papules over MCP and PIP joints, Gottron sign, heliotrope periorbital erythema with edema, shawl and V-neck signs; biopsy shows perifascicular atrophy.
Polymyositis lacks rashes with subacute limb-girdle weakness; biopsy shows endomysial CD8 cytotoxic T cells invading non-necrotic fibers with perforin expression.
IMNM gives acute or subacute proximal weakness with CK above 10,000 to 20,000 units per liter, pauci-immune necrosis on biopsy, and anti-SRP or anti-HMGCR antibodies (statin linked).
IBM affects men above 50 years with asymmetric deep finger flexor and quadriceps weakness; biopsy shows rimmed vacuoles, amyloid deposits, and TDP-43 inclusions, with no immunosuppression response.
Antisynthetase syndrome joins myositis, interstitial lung disease, Raynaud, non-erosive arthritis, and mechanic's hands with anti-Jo-1, anti-PL-7, or anti-PL-12 antibodies.
Adult dermatomyositis carries 3-fold to 6-fold malignancy risk; anti-TIF1-gamma and anti-NXP2 mark cancer-associated myositis in up to 50 percent, requiring full age-appropriate screening.
Induction is high-dose glucocorticoids (prednisone 1 mg per kg daily or IV methylprednisolone pulses) with methotrexate, azathioprine, or mycophenolate, plus IVIG 2 g per kg monthly for refractory or IMNM disease.
Dysphagia with nasal speech and aspiration complicates severe dermatomyositis and IBM; videofluoroscopy guides diet modification and IVIG often improves swallowing faster than limb strength.
Exercise within tolerance preserves muscle during active myositis; prolonged bed rest accelerates atrophy, while graded physiotherapy started alongside immunosuppression improves 6-month strength scores.
Calcinosis complicates juvenile dermatomyositis in up to 40 percent with delayed therapy; interstitial lung disease with anti-MDA5 antibodies progresses within weeks and needs combination tacrolimus with cyclophosphamide urgently.
Statin-associated IMNM often persists months after stopping the statin; rechallenge with any statin is avoided permanently, with PCSK9 inhibitors preferred for lipid control.
