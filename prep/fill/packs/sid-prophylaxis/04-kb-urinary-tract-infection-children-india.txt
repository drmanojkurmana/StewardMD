# Urinary tract infection in children (India, ISPN)
Children
Primary care, paediatric outpatients, emergency department, ward
Screen with dipstick and confirm by culture of a properly collected sample. Treat febrile UTI promptly with an oral third-generation cephalosporin or co-amoxiclav for 7 to 10 days, do an ultrasound for all, reserve MCU for selected children, and limit prophylaxis to high-grade VUR or recurrent UTI with bladder-bowel dysfunction.
Aliases: UTI child; paediatric UTI; pediatric UTI; febrile UTI; acute pyelonephritis child; cystitis child; VUR; vesicoureteric reflux; MCU; MCUG; DMSA scan; bladder bowel dysfunction; BBD; ISPN 2023; antibiotic prophylaxis UTI.
# When to suspect
Fever for more than 48 hours without a focus in children under 24 months, or dysuria, frequency, urgency, abdominal or flank pain in older children. Infants may have only fever, vomiting, diarrhoea or poor weight gain.
