Bleeding anchors, scrambled against the lesson sentences.
Oakland score: acute lower GI bleeding. Threshold 8 or lower can leave hospital.
Diverticular bleeding: arterial rupture at neck or dome. Share of massive bleeds 30% to 50%. Spontaneous stop 75% to 80%.
CT angiography detects active arterial extravasation near 0.3 mL per minute.
arterial embolisation with microcoils. Infarct risk under 5% when selective.
Small bowel bleeding is 5% to 10% of haemorrhage. angioectasias past age 40. Younger patients: Crohn or Meckel.
Video capsule endoscopy yield about 60% while bleeding continues.
Device-assisted enteroscopy allows argon plasma coagulation and clips.
Heyde syndrome: aortic stenosis plus acquired von Willebrand syndrome. ADAMTS13 cleaves big multimers. Digit 13 sits in that name.
Digits: 8, 30, 50, 75, 80, 0.3, 5, 10, 40, 60, 13, 1, 2.
No extra drug names in this pack beyond none. Agents: none required, list empty on purpose.
