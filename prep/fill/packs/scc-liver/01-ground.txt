Paracetamol injury earns transplant candidacy through lasting acidemia under 7.3 despite volume, or through the triad of deep coma (grades 3 to 4), creatinine beyond 3.4, and INR beyond 6.5. These King hospital rules flag livers unlikely to self rescue. Same day transfer follows once met, because deeper coma only invites brain harm.

Outside paracetamol, candidacy rests on INR beyond 6.5 solo, or on three of five markers: age below ten or past forty, toxic or cryptogenic trigger, week plus jaundice coma interval, INR past 3.5, and bilirubin past 17.
