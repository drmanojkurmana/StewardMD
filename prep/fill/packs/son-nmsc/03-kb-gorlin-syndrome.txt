# Gorlin syndrome
Gorlin syndrome, Genetics / Oncology, Clinical Genetics / Medical Genetics.
Aliases: naevoid basal cell carcinoma syndrome; NBCCS; basal cell naevus syndrome; PTCH1; Gorlin-Goltz syndrome.
# Pathophysiology
Gorlin syndrome arises from germline inactivating variants in PTCH1 (or less often SUFU), key components of the Sonic hedgehog signalling pathway. PTCH1 normally inhibits the transmembrane protein Smoothened; loss of PTCH1 function releases this inhibition, leading to constitutive hedgehog pathway activation and unchecked proliferation in susceptible tissues. As a tumour-suppressor mechanism, tumour formation follows a somatic second hit. Aberrant hedgehog signalling explains the developmental anomalies and the predisposition to basal cell carcinomas, odontogenic keratocysts and medulloblastoma. The same pathway dependence underlies the therapeutic use of hedgehog pathway inhibitors in advanced disease.
# Prognosis
Life expectancy is usually near normal, but lifelong burden from recurrent basal cell carcinomas and jaw cysts is considerable and requires ongoing surveillance and treatment. Medulloblastoma in early childhood is the most serious potential complication and drives paediatric monitoring.
