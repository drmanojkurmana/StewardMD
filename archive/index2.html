Before you continue
StewardMD is an educational clinical reasoning aid, built from guideline references for self-study and teaching discussion. It is not a validated clinical decision support tool and has not been clinically tested or peer-reviewed for direct patient care.

It does not replace clinical judgment, local antibiograms, institutional protocols, or specialist input. Always verify recommendations independently before applying them to any patient. The creator and any contributors accept no liability for clinical decisions made using this tool.

8-question stewardship framework

IN Clinical Findings

0 findings selected
Golden rule of StewardMD — every recommendation must answer:
  1. Why is this the diagnosis?
  2. Why are antibiotics needed or not needed?
  3. Why are these pathogens likely?
  4. Why were these antibiotics selected?
  5. What organisms do these antibiotics cover?
  6. What is NOT covered?
  7. When should therapy be narrowed?
  8. When should therapy be stopped?
If any answer is missing, no antibiotic recommendation should be made. This tool is a clinical reasoning aid built from guideline references — it does not replace clinical judgment, local antibiograms, or specialist input.
