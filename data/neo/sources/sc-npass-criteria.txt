Scoring Criteria

Crying / Irritability -2 Û No response to painful stimuli, e.g.: ˜ No cry with needle sticks ˜ No reaction to ETT or nares suctioning ˜ No response to care giving -1 Û Moans, sighs, or cries (audible or silent)
minimally to painful stimuli, e.g.
needle sticks, ETT or nares suctioning, care giving 0 Û Not irritable  appropriate
crying ˜ Cries briefly with normal stimuli ˜ Easily consoled ˜ Normal for gestational age +1 Û Infant is irritable/crying at intervals  but can be consoled ˜ If intubated  intermittent silent cry +2 Û Any of the following: ˜ Cry is high-pitched ˜ Infant cries inconsolably ˜ If intubated 
silent continuous cry |

Behavior / State -2 Û Does not arouse or react to any stimuli: ˜ Eyes continually shut or open ˜ No spontaneous movement -1 Û Little spontaneous movement, arouses briefly
and/or minimally to any stimuli: ˜ Opens eyes briefly ˜ Reacts to suctioning ˜ Withdraws to pain 0 Û Behavior and
state are gestational age appropriate +1 Û Any of the following: ˜ Restless,
squirming ˜ Awakens
frequently/easily with minimal or no stimuli +2 Û Any of the following: ˜ Kicking ˜ Arching ˜ Constantly awake ˜ No movement or minimal arousal with
stimulation (inappropriate for gestational age or clinical situation, i.e.
post-operative)

Facial Expression -2 Û Any of the following: ˜ Mouth is lax ˜ Drooling ˜ No facial expression at rest or with
stimuli -1 Û Minimal facial expression with stimuli 0 Û Face is relaxed at
rest but not lax  normal expression with stimuli +1 Û Any pain face expression observed intermittently +2 Û Any pain face expression is continual |

Extremities / Tone -2 Û Any of the following: ˜ No palmar or planter grasp can be elicited ˜ Flaccid tone -1 Û Any of the following: ˜ Weak palmar or planter grasp can be
elicited ˜ Decreased tone 0 Û Relaxed hands and feet 
normal palmar or sole grasp
elicited  appropriate tone for gestational age +1 Û Intermittent (<30 seconds duration)
observation of toes and/or hands as clenched or fingers splayed ˜ Body is not tense +2 Û Any of the following: ˜ Frequent (≥30 seconds duration)
observation of toes and/or hands as clenched, or fingers splayed ˜ Body is tense/stiff

Vital
Signs: HR, BP, RR, & O 2 Saturations -2 Û Any of the following: ˜ No variability in vital signs with stimuli ˜ Hypoventilation ˜ Apnea ˜ Ventilated infant  no spontaneous
respiratory effort -1 Û Vital signs show little variability with stimuli
 less than 10%
from baseline 0 Û Vital signs and/or oxygen saturations are within normal
limits with normal variability  or normal for gestational age +1 Û Any of the following: ˜ HR, RR, and/or BP are 10-20% above baseline ˜ With care/stimuli infant desaturates
minimally to moderately (SaO 2 76-85%) and recovers quickly (within 2 minutes) +2 Û Any of the following: ˜ HR, RR, and/or BP are > 20% above
baseline ˜ With care/stimuli infant desaturates
