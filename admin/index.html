Institution coupons
Issue a code to an institution that pays for its doctors. Each doctor redeems it in-app to unlock Pro. Revoke anytime (pulls Pro from redeemers who haven't since paid).
AI credits · daily cost cap · co-resident pool
All keyed by the doctor's account email. Daily cost cap is rupees of AI/day (blank = clear to default). Credits are prepaid AI rupees spent above the cap. Co-resident pool links a second account to a pool owner so both share one AI bucket.
