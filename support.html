Support & Help

StewardMD is a clinical decision-support tool for qualified healthcare professionals. We're happy to help with any question, problem, or feedback.

📧 Email us: privacy@stewardmd.in

We aim to reply within a few working days. Please include your device, what you were doing, and a screenshot if relevant — but never include patient-identifying information in an email.

Report a problem or clinical concern

If you believe a recommendation, dose, or piece of content is inaccurate or unsafe, please tell us at privacy@stewardmd.in with the subject "content concern". We review these promptly. Remember: StewardMD is decision support only — always verify against primary sources and your clinical judgment.

Common questions

Is StewardMD a substitute for clinical judgment?

No. It is an educational decision-support aid. Every recommendation must be independently verified against current guidelines, local protocols, and full prescribing references before use.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Account → Delete account & data permanently removes your account and all saved cases from your device and the cloud. In guest mode, use Erase all data on this device. You can also email us to request deletion.

What data does the app collect?

See our Privacy Policy. In short: your account (if you sign in) and cases you save; optional AI features process your input transiently; no analytics or ad tracking.

Who can use it?

Only qualified, registered healthcare professionals, within their scope of practice.

The app seems out of date or stuck on an old version.

Fully close and reopen it; it updates automatically when online. If a problem persists, email us with your device and app version.
